Author

Andrew Dobrow

Founder and Publisher, Treasure Coast Today

Andrew Dobrow is the founder and publisher of Treasure Coast Today, an independent local news outlet serving Martin, St. Lucie and Indian River counties.

Based in Hobe Sound, Andrew focuses on timely, useful coverage of the issues that affect Treasure Coast residents, including local government, public safety, development, schools, sports, community events and breaking news. He created Treasure Coast Today to give readers a fast, accessible source for local reporting without unnecessary sensationalism or clutter.

Andrew is committed to building stronger connections between residents, public agencies, local organizations and businesses throughout the Treasure Coast.

As publisher, Andrew is responsible for the editorial standards, accuracy and independence of everything published on Treasure Coast Today. You can read more about how our coverage is produced, how we handle corrections, and how the site is owned and funded.

Get in touch. Readers can reach Andrew at hello@treasurecoast.today with story tips, questions, or feedback.
