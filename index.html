Property value growth has declined for four consecutive years in a Florida county, affecting tax revenues and local government budgets.

Yesterday, 9:06 AM ET

A Florida county has experienced declining property value growth for four straight years, marking a significant shift in the local real estate market. The slowdown in property value appreciation affects how much tax revenue local governments collect to fund schools, roads, public safety, and other services.

Property values serve as the foundation for local government budgets throughout Florida, where property taxes fund county and municipal services. 13 hours ago

A black bear was spotted Tuesday in the Town Park community within Tradition, according to local officials. The sighting has prompted authorities to remind residents to remain cautious and follow BearWise guidelines when encountering wildlife.

Tradition residents are being urged to take precautions to avoid attracting bears to residential areas. This includes securing garbage, removing bird feeders, and not leaving pet food outside. Black bears are native to Florida and sightings in developed areas have become more common as urban development expands into natural habitats.

Officials recommend that residents who encounter a bear should remain calm, keep their distance, and never approach or feed the animal. If a bear is spotted in a residential area, residents should report the sighting to the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission to help authorities track bear movements and ensure public safety.

Property value growth declines for fourth straight year in Florida county

A Florida county has seen property value growth decline for four consecutive years, a trend that affects local government budgets and tax revenue.

Yesterday, 9:06 AM ET

Property value growth has slowed for the fourth year in a row in a Florida county, marking a sustained cooling in the real estate market after years of rapid appreciation. The declining growth rate has implications for county and municipal budgets that depend on property tax revenue to fund schools, public safety, roads, and other essential services.

Property values across much of Florida surged in recent years, driven by migration from other states, low interest rates, and strong demand for coastal and retirement destinations. The Treasure Coast saw particularly strong appreciation as buyers sought more affordable alternatives to South Florida. However, rising interest rates, insurance costs, and affordability concerns have slowed the pace of growth. A decline in the rate of appreciation means property values are still increasing, but at a slower pace than in previous years.

For local governments, slower property value growth creates budget challenges. Counties and cities must balance maintaining service levels with limited revenue growth. School districts in particular depend heavily on property taxes for funding. Homeowners may see smaller increases in assessed values, which affects their annual tax bills depending on millage rates set by local taxing authorities. The four-year trend suggests the red-hot Florida real estate market of the early 2020s has entered a more moderate phase of growth.
