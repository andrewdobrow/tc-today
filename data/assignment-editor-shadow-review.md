## Business & Development

### Source pool

1. New data center proposed for western St. Lucie County near Interstate 95

### Variant A

**Hero:** Data center proposed for 174 acres off West Midway Road in St. Lucie County
**Hero source choice:** Source #1: New data center proposed for western St. Lucie County near Interstate 95
**Urgency:** 7

Willow Lakes LLC submitted a rezoning application in June to St. Lucie County to change 174 acres of its 203-acre property off West Midway Road and Interstate 95 to light industrial use for a data center. The rezoning application states the applicant intends to develop a data center on the portion of the property to be zoned light industrial.

 The land was originally planned for the Koa Bay Surf Park Community with developer Devcon Enterprises. Attorney Lee Dobbins, who represents Willow Lakes LLC, said those plans fell through after the property owner could not raise sufficient capital for the project. Dobbins said the landowner believes the property is well-suited for a data center because of existing Florida Power & Light transmission lines on the site and a man-made lake that could be used for a closed-loop cooling system.

 The rezoning application requests the remaining 26 acres be zoned general commercial for development of commercial retail uses, which could include a hotel, gas station, restaurants, or fast food establishments. Fort Pierce City Commissioner Michael Broderick said he wants developers to demonstrate there will be no impact to groundwater, no impacts to user fees from power consumption, and no noise pollution. Broderick said if they don't demonstrate these protections, he will not be supportive of the proposal.

 Parker Whiteside, who lives about a mile and a half from the property, said she values the quiet, open character of the area. When asked about the potential data center, Whiteside said she hates the idea. The application is scheduled to go before the Planning and Zoning Board in September. If rezoning is approved, the applicant intends to enter into an agreement with a partner company which would develop the data center and submit a site plan application to the city for approval.
