# Newsletter Layout and Sticky-Bar Containment Review Guide

## Desktop homepage and category views

1. Confirm the hero and inline newsletter form form one left column.
2. Confirm Latest News occupies the full height beside both components.
3. Confirm the Latest News item list scrolls internally when necessary.
4. Confirm Top Stories begins below the taller of the two lead columns.

## Desktop sticky bar

1. Test in a private window so Kit's frequency cookie cannot suppress the form.
2. Confirm Kit is configured for **All Devices**.
3. Confirm the bar appears above the TCT masthead rather than behind it.
4. Confirm the masthead moves below the bar and remains sticky while scrolling.
5. Confirm the close control remains accessible.

## Mobile

1. Confirm the sticky bar is no taller than 58px.
2. Confirm the email field and Subscribe button remain fully visible.
3. Confirm the TCT logo/navigation begins below the sticky bar.
4. Confirm closing the form removes the reserved space.
5. Confirm the page order is hero, inline newsletter, Latest News, then Top Stories.

## Kit settings note

Kit controls display frequency, trigger timing, return-visitor behavior and device targeting. Site code can correct stacking and size only after Kit injects the form. During review, temporarily set the display interval to zero days or use a private window.
