# Research notes

Product descriptions were written conservatively from manufacturer or reputable product documentation where available. Amazon affiliate URLs and TCT image URLs are preserved exactly from the supplied product list.

- Jackery Explorer 300: 293Wh capacity, 300W inverter, 500W surge, six-device support, multiple outputs and solar-compatible charging.
- Midland emergency weather radios: NOAA weather reception/alerts, alternate charging methods, emergency flashlight and device-charging features vary by model.
- GearLight LED flashlights: compact tactical-style lights with adjustable beam and multiple light modes.
- Collapsible lantern and Coozment container: copy intentionally avoids unverified lumen, runtime and capacity claims.
- KitchenAid manual can opener: manual operation, large turning knob and easy-grip design.
- LifeStraw Personal: 0.2-micron membrane, bacteria/parasite/microplastic filtration, up to 1,000 gallons; does not remove viruses or all chemical contaminants.
- MaiVoz N9: listed 56,800mAh capacity, 22.5W charging and multiple USB outputs.
- Renogy portable solar panel: copy avoids asserting an exact wattage because the supplied name/link did not identify the specific panel variant; compatibility warning included.
- BAND-AID all-purpose kit: intended for minor cuts, scrapes and burns; household-specific medications and supplies should be added.
- Amazon Basics tarp: polyethylene weather protection with reinforced edges and tie-down points; described only as temporary coverage.
- DEWALT jobsite fan: 20V MAX battery compatibility, variable airflow and adjustable positioning; battery-inclusion warning included.
