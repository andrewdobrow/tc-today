Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Treasure Coast Today is an independent local news website and iPhone app owned and operated by Treasure Coast Today LLC, a Florida limited liability company founded and owned by Andrew Dobrow. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the website or app, how it is used, and your choices.

Free articles and voluntary reader contributions

Treasure Coast Today's news articles are freely available without a subscription, sign-in or contribution. Readers may optionally contribute to support our reporting. TCT is operated by Treasure Coast Today LLC, a for-profit business and not a tax-exempt charity; contributions are not tax-deductible charitable donations and do not purchase ad-free access, subscription rights or editorial influence.

Our one-time, choose-your-own-amount contribution checkout is hosted by Stripe. We may receive transaction amounts, payment status, dates, Stripe identifiers, and contact details supplied for confirmations or receipts. Stripe handles payment credentials; TCT does not collect or store full card numbers. We retain payment records when needed for customer support, accounting, fraud prevention or applicable legal obligations.

Optional tips in our iPhone app. In the iPhone app, optional one-time, repeatable tips are purchased only through Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit), not through Stripe. Apple processes the payment and associated billing details under its own terms and privacy policy. TCT may receive limited purchase reporting, such as transaction amounts, dates, and product identifiers, through App Store Connect; our app verifies and completes purchases through StoreKit. TCT does not collect or store full payment card numbers for Apple purchases. Tips are voluntary and do not unlock articles or features, remove advertisements, create a subscription, or confer editorial influence. No TCT account is needed to leave an in-app tip.

Existing paid subscriptions and billing records continue to be handled through Stripe and Supabase while previously purchased periods conclude. Eligible subscribers may sign in to receive the ad-free website and iPhone app experience already included in their paid terms. New articles do not require paid access. We display clearly labeled sponsorships and advertising, including Mediavine on the website and Google AdMob in the iPhone app, as described below.

Information we collect

We collect information needed to operate the website and app, manage optional contributions and pre-existing paid accounts, deliver requested news alerts and emails, understand readership, and respond to messages:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and general, aggregated information about our audience. This helps us improve our coverage.
  • Existing subscriber account information. For previously purchased memberships, we use the associated email address and account identifiers to send sign-in links, verify remaining ad-free benefits and provide access to billing management. No account is required to read public articles.
  • Payment and contribution information. Stripe processes voluntary reader payments and historical subscriptions. We receive related confirmations, amounts, dates, customer contact information when provided, Stripe payment identifiers and existing subscription status. We do not receive or store complete payment card numbers.
  • Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles optional iPhone tip payments through StoreKit. TCT may receive limited sales reporting through App Store Connect, including amounts, dates and product identifiers, but does not collect your full payment card number or require an app account for tipping.
  • Information you send us. If you email us a news tip, correction, or question, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message.

Information used by our iPhone app

Headlines, news articles and event listings are available without purchasing a membership. Older installed app versions may temporarily retain legacy article-access screens until readers update. The app retrieves content from Treasure Coast Today services over an internet connection. As with ordinary website and API requests, service providers may process technical connection details such as IP address, request times and device/network information.

  • Optional news alerts. If you turn on push notifications, our app receives an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token and uses a randomly generated app-installation identifier to register your phone. Our systems store the token, installation identifier, enabled or disabled status, your selected county and breaking-news preferences, the APNs environment, and relevant registration timestamps. This information is used to deliver and manage the alerts you request. You can turn off notifications in the app or through iOS Settings.
  • App authentication and remaining paid benefits. Existing paid readers who sign in provide an email address and session credentials so the app can verify their remaining ad-free entitlement. Credentials are stored in iOS Keychain. Stripe handles historical website subscription payments; optional iPhone tips are processed by Apple. No login is required for ordinary free news reading.
  • App preferences and legacy reading limits. The app locally stores settings and notification preferences. Older versions may also retain a historical monthly article-meter record on the device; new public-access versions do not use a paid reading allowance to unlock ordinary news articles.

Advertising in our iPhone app

The Treasure Coast Today iPhone app uses Google AdMob, powered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display advertising to eligible readers. Google and its advertising partners may process data including IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, advertisements viewed or interacted with, usage information, and performance or diagnostic data. This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, perform analytics, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

Advertising consent and tracking. Where required, the app presents advertising consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission request before allowing tracking that requires that permission. You can decline tracking permission and manage it in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Depending on applicable consent choices, non-personalized advertising may still appear. Where required, additional advertising privacy options are available in the app.

Paid subscribers. Readers with an active paid membership receive an ad-free experience in the iPhone app. The app does not request advertising or display AdMob ads while their paid access is active.

For more information about Google's handling of advertising data, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

How we use information

We use the information described above to operate and improve the website and app, deliver requested push notifications and newsletters, process optional contributions, manage pre-existing subscriptions and their ad-free benefits, send secure account messages, understand readership, and respond to messages you send us. We share information with the service providers and advertising partners described in this policy. Sharing information with advertising partners may be considered a sale or sharing of personal information under applicable privacy laws, even when we do not receive payment specifically for that information. For questions about your privacy rights and available choices, contact hello@treasurecoast.today.

News content and sources

Treasure Coast Today uses automated tools to assist in gathering and organizing information from public sources. Published coverage is produced under the editorial oversight of the publisher. For more on how our coverage is produced, see our Editorial Standards.

Third-party services

Treasure Coast Today relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics for website analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe for optional one-time contributions, receipts, and management of historical membership billing.
  • Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) for voluntary, one-time tips offered within the iPhone app. Apple processes the purchase and provides associated transaction records. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase for remaining subscriber authentication, account records, legacy billing status, article services and site functions.
  • Resend as an email-delivery provider for secure existing-account sign-in messages.
  • Kit for readers who voluntarily subscribe to the Morning Brief email newsletter; readers may unsubscribe through the links in those emails.
  • Formspree for information submitted through advertising inquiry forms, such as a contact name, company and message.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to deliver optional iPhone news alerts. The Apple-issued device token is used for this purpose.

Mediavine Programmatic Advertising (Ver 1.1)

The Website works with Mediavine to manage third-party interest-based advertising appearing on the Website. Mediavine serves content and advertisements when you visit the Website, which may use first and third-party cookies. A cookie is a small text file which is sent to your computer or mobile device (referred to in this policy as a “device”) by the web server so that a website can remember some information about your browsing activity on the Website.

First party cookies are created by the website that you are visiting. A third-party cookie is frequently used in behavioral advertising and analytics and is created by a domain other than the website you are visiting. Third-party cookies, tags, pixels, beacons and other similar technologies (collectively, “Tags”) may be placed on the Website to monitor interaction with advertising content and to target and optimize advertising. Each internet browser has functionality so that you can block both first and third-party cookies and clear your browser’s cache. The "help" feature of the menu bar on most browsers will tell you how to stop accepting new cookies, how to receive notification of new cookies, how to disable existing cookies and how to clear your browser’s cache. For more information about cookies and how to disable them, you can consult the information at All About Cookies.

Without cookies you may not be able to take full advantage of the Website content and features. Please note that rejecting cookies does not mean that you will no longer see ads when you visit our Site. In the event you opt-out, you will still see non-personalized advertisements on the Website.

The Website collects the following data using a cookie when serving personalized ads:

  • IP Address
  • Operating System type
  • Operating System version
  • Device Type
  • Language of the website
  • Web browser type
  • Email (in hashed form)

Mediavine Partners (companies listed below with whom Mediavine shares data) may also use this data to link to other end user information the partner has independently collected to deliver targeted advertisements. Mediavine Partners may also separately collect data about end users from other sources, such as advertising IDs or pixels, and link that data to data collected from Mediavine publishers in order to provide interest-based advertising across your online experience, including devices, browsers and apps. This data includes usage data, cookie information, device information, information about interactions between users and advertisements and websites, geolocation data, traffic data, and information about a visitor’s referral source to a particular website. Mediavine Partners may also create unique IDs to create audience segments, which are used to provide targeted advertising.

If you would like more information about this practice and to know your choices to opt-in or opt-out of this data collection, please visit National Advertising Initiative opt out page. You may also visit Digital Advertising Alliance website and Network Advertising Initiative website to learn more information about interest-based advertising. You may download the AppChoices app at Digital Advertising Alliance’s AppChoices app to opt out in connection with mobile apps, or use the platform controls on your mobile device to opt out.

For specific information about Mediavine Partners, the data each collects and their data collection and privacy policies, please visit Mediavine Partners.

Your choices, retention and requests

You can decline or disable news alerts and change your selected notification topics through the app and iOS Settings. You can sign out of the app, and you can access your billing and cancellation options through the Treasure Coast Today membership website.

We retain information as reasonably necessary to administer remaining paid benefits, optional contributions, requested services and applicable legal, accounting, security and recordkeeping obligations. For questions about personal information, or to request access to or deletion of account information, contact hello@treasurecoast.today. Some records may need to be retained when required by law.

Children's privacy

Treasure Coast Today is a general-audience local news website and iPhone app, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
