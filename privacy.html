Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Treasure Coast Today is an independent local news website owned and operated by Andrew Dobrow. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the site, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

Treasure Coast Today is a free website with no login and no accounts, so we do not ask you to create an account or provide personal information to read the news. We collect limited information automatically and through third-party services:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and general, aggregated information about our audience. This helps us improve our coverage.
  • Advertising data. We display advertising through Google AdSense to keep the site free to read. Advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads.
  • Information you send us. If you email us a news tip, correction, or question, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How we use information

We use the limited information we collect to operate and improve the site, understand our readership in aggregate, display advertising, and respond to messages you send us. We do not sell your personal information.

News content and sources

Treasure Coast Today uses automated tools to assist in gathering and organizing information from public sources. Published coverage is produced under the editorial oversight of the publisher. For more on how our coverage is produced, see our Editorial Standards.

Third-party services

Treasure Coast Today relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics for website analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense for advertising. See Google Advertising Policies.

Children's privacy

Treasure Coast Today is a general-audience local news website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
