# Inline Newsletter Module Border Review Guide — v1.12.2.5

## Desktop checks

1. Open the homepage or a category page and locate the inline newsletter below the hero.
2. Confirm a light gray border is visible on all four sides.
3. Confirm the border radius and subtle shadow match the visual language of the hero and Latest News modules.
4. Confirm the Latest News rail still extends beside the hero-plus-newsletter stack.

## Article checks

1. Open a generated article and scroll to the inline newsletter above the share controls.
2. Confirm the same border and shadow appear without changing article-column width.

## Mobile checks

1. Confirm the inline form remains full width within the normal page gutter.
2. Confirm its rounded corners, border, and content are not clipped.
3. Confirm the mobile modal and desktop sticky-bar behavior remain unchanged.
