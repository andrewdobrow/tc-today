Got a story we should know about?

Treasure Coast Today covers Martin, St. Lucie and Indian River counties. The best local stories usually start with a resident who noticed something first. If that is you, we want to hear it.

News tips
tips@treasurecoast.today

Tap the address to open your email app. Tell us what you saw, where, and when. Photos and video help. Every tip is read.

What makes a good tip

  • Something happening right now. A fire, a crash, a road closure, a police response, a water main break.
  • Local government. A commission decision, a budget item, a zoning fight, a school board vote that deserves more attention.
  • Business openings and closings. A new restaurant, a store shutting down, construction starting on a long-empty lot.
  • Neighborhood issues. Flooding that keeps coming back, a dangerous intersection, a project that stalled.
  • Community news. A local team, a fundraiser, a neighbor doing something worth knowing about.
  • Anything the bigger outlets are missing. If it matters on your street, it probably matters to your neighbors.

A note on corrections

We would rather be corrected than be wrong. If you spot an error, send the story link and what is inaccurate to corrections@treasurecoast.today and we will review it promptly.

Sharing a tip privately. If you would rather not be named, say so in your email and we will not identify you. Please do not send anything that puts you at risk, and never trespass or interfere with police, fire or emergency crews to get a photo. No story is worth that.
