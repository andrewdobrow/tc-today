# TCT v1.13.4.2 — Membership Design Polish

Design-only dark-launch refinement for the production subscription/paywall UX.

- Centers plan pricing and plan notes, including `$4.99` and `Cancel anytime`.
- Adds a useful `Most flexible` pill to the monthly plan so the monthly and annual cards have matching visual structure.
- Anchors both subscription buttons to the bottom of their cards, so button rows stay aligned regardless of copy length.
- Adds a subtle coral gradient and highlight to the membership surface for more depth.
- Adds a light card shadow for separation without changing the established palette.
- Applies the same treatment to `/subscribe.html` and the eventual in-article paywall.
- Does not change Stripe, Supabase, entitlement, checkout, or launch-switch behavior.
