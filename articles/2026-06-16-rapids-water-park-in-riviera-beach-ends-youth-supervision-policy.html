Rapids Water Park hosts free admission for dads June 20-21, joins global water safety effort

Rapids Water Park in Riviera Beach is offering free admission for fathers on June 20-21 during Father's Day weekend. The park, located at 6566 N. Military Trail, is also launching special events throughout the summer to appeal to different age groups. According to Greg Fuller, the park's general manager, families should take advantage of the free admission opportunity for dads this weekend.

The water park is bringing back Adults Night events designed for guests 21 and older. Elliott Russell, director of marketing and sales, explained that these nighttime events feature music, glow items, and exclusive access to the park's main attractions. Attendees can enjoy the slides, lazy river, and wave pool under the stars during these special adult-only sessions. The park is also introducing new food offerings this season, including specialty funnel cakes and other viral-style treats that will be available to guests.

On June 25, Rapids Water Park will participate in the World's Largest Swim Lesson, joining Live Like Jake and participants from around the world. This coordinated event aims to raise awareness about water safety and educate people of all ages on proper swimming techniques and safety practices. The park's involvement in this global initiative reflects a commitment to promoting water safety in the community beyond typical entertainment offerings.

Rapids Water Park operates daily with multiple ticket package options available for visitors planning their summer outings. Whether families are interested in the Father's Day weekend promotion, adults want to experience the glow-filled nighttime events, or guests of any age want to participate in the water safety lesson later in the month, the park is positioning itself as a hub for summer entertainment and education. For more information about the park's events, hours, and ticket options, visitors can contact the park directly or visit their official website.
