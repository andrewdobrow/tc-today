Rapids Water Park in Riviera Beach offers adults-only nights, Father's Day free admission, and global safety event

Rapids Water Park in Riviera Beach is rolling out several attractions this summer to draw visitors of different ages and interests. The park is hosting an adults-only evening experience, offering free entry for fathers during Father's Day weekend, and participating in a global water safety initiative later in June.

Elliott Russell, the park's director of marketing and sales, explained that Adults Night has returned with a focus on entertaining guests aged 21 and older. The evening event features music and glow items while allowing adult visitors exclusive access to the park's slides, lazy river, and wave pool after dark. This experience gives grown-ups a chance to enjoy the water attractions in a different setting without children present. Greg Fuller, the park's general manager, confirmed that families can make use of a Father's Day promotion running June 20 and 21, during which all dads receive complimentary admission to the facility.

On June 25, Rapids Water Park will join Live Like Jake and numerous other participants around the world in the World's Largest Swim Lesson. This coordinated global event aims to raise public awareness about water safety. The park's involvement connects it to a broader initiative focused on educating people about staying safe in and around water.

The water park sits at 6566 N. Military Trail in Riviera Beach and operates daily throughout the summer season with multiple ticket options for visitors. This year, the park is also introducing new food offerings, including specialty funnel cakes that are gaining popularity on social media. These viral treats represent part of the park's effort to refresh its dining options and attract guests looking for something different. Whether visitors are interested in an adults-only night out, a family Father's Day outing, or learning about water safety, Rapids Water Park is offering reasons to visit during the coming weeks.
