Rapids Water Park in Riviera Beach hosts Adults Night and Worlds Largest Swim Lesson

Rapids Water Park in Riviera Beach is offering special events this summer designed to appeal to visitors of all ages. The park is planning an Adults Night featuring music and glow items, providing exclusive access for guests 21 and older. According to Elliott Russell, the park's director of marketing and sales, the event gives adults an opportunity to experience the slides, lazy river, and wave pool in the evening hours. The park is also recognizing Father's Day by offering free admission for dads on June 20 and 21, allowing families to celebrate together without paying regular ticket prices.

Beyond these recreational offerings, Rapids Water Park is participating in a worldwide initiative focused on water safety. On June 25, the park will join Live Like Jake and numerous other organizations around the globe for the World's Largest Swim Lesson. This coordinated effort aims to raise awareness about water safety and promote swimming instruction on a global scale. By participating in this event, Rapids contributes to a larger mission of educating people about safe practices in and around water.

The park, situated at 6566 N. Military Trail in Riviera Beach, operates daily throughout the season with multiple ticket package options available to suit different budgets and visit lengths. Greg Fuller, the park's general manager, highlighted that the facility continues to enhance the guest experience with new food offerings. This season, specialty funnel cakes and other viral treats are making their debut at the park, giving visitors additional dining options beyond standard amusement park fare.

Whether guests are seeking a fun night out with friends, a family celebration for Father's Day, or an opportunity to learn swimming skills while supporting water safety awareness, Rapids Water Park is positioning itself as a destination offering varied experiences. The combination of evening entertainment for adults, family promotions during holidays, and participation in important safety initiatives demonstrates the park's commitment to serving the Riviera Beach community throughout the summer season.
