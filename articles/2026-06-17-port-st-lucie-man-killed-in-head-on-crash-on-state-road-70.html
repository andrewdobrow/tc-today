Martin County Fire Rescue responds to 42 vehicle crashes on State Road 710 this year

Martin County Fire Rescue has responded to 42 vehicle accident-related calls on State Road 710 just this year. After a fatal crash in January, Martin County Sheriff John Budensiek called the road a "death trap." The road, also known as Beeline Highway, has been the site of multiple fatal crashes.

Bill Townsend, who lives nearby, said the road has long been a concern for residents. "People don't pay a whole lot of attention to other people on the road, and [it's] kind of a dangerous road sometimes," Townsend said. "Everybody's always in a hurry… just living here makes me worry about the young people learning to drive and learning to drive on that road concerns me, because I have young kids." The Florida Department of Transportation has plans to widen the road from two lanes to four, but construction is not expected to begin until 2031. In the meantime, FDOT has begun improvements to the road, including pavement, markings, signage and new turn lanes.
