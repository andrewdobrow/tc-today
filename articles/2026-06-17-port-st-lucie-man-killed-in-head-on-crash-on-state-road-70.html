Crime & Safety 2026-06-28

2 people killed in head-on crash on Allapattah Road in Indiantown

The crash occurred around 6:30 a.m. at 8113 SW Allapattah Road, also known as the Allapattah S-Curve, according to the Martin County Sheriff's Office. Two people were killed in the head-on collision.

Allapattah Road was blocked for about 6 hours Saturday morning while the Florida Highway Patrol processed the scene. The road has since reopened.
