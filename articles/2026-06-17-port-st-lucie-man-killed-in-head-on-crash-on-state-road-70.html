40-year-old driver killed in head-on crash on Beeline Highway in Martin County

A 40-year-old man died early Sunday morning following a head-on collision on State Road 710 in Martin County, according to the Florida Highway Patrol. The crash occurred around 2 a.m. near the intersection of SR-710, commonly called Beeline Highway, and Southwest Silver Fox Lane.

The FHP investigation determined that a 2015 Kia Optima traveling northbound on SR-710 crossed into the opposing lane and struck a 2021 Toyota Tundra head-on. The front left portion of the Kia collided with the front left side of the Tundra. The impact caused the Kia to catch fire immediately. After coming to rest, the Kia sat on the grass shoulder facing north. The Tundra remained in the southbound lane, positioned facing southeast.

The driver of the Kia, identified as a 40-year-old man, was pronounced dead at the scene. The driver of the Tundra, a 41-year-old Indiantown resident, sustained injuries from the crash. That driver was airlifted by Lifestar helicopter from the scene for emergency medical treatment.

The Florida Highway Patrol continues to investigate the circumstances surrounding the crash. The name of the deceased driver has not yet been released. This remains an active investigation as authorities work to determine the cause of the northbound vehicle crossing into the southbound lane.
