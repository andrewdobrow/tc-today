Delray Beach's S.D. Spady Elementary jumps from C to A rating as Palm Beach County schools earn third consecutive A

Principal Rona Tata, who has served 15 years as principal and five years as assistant principal before that, led the turnaround at S.D. Spady Elementary School in Delray Beach. The school earned an A rating this year after receiving a C rating the previous year. Tata said the school had an A rating two years ago and narrowly missed a B rating last year by just a few points. Three years ago, staff began modifying the school's early childhood curriculum program, which starts at age 3 and combines with kindergarten. Many of those students are now in third grade and performing at higher levels, contributing to the renewed A rating. The school fine-tuned its entire academic approach, focusing on testing grades while also challenging students and staff to raise expectations across campus. The school's theme last year was "Be the reason you succeed."

Community member Chris Ceasar, who attended Spady as a child and now has family members enrolled there, said the A rating will draw more talent to the school and allow students being bused to other schools to return to their community. Spady Elementary was one of four district schools to improve from a C rating to an A this year, alongside Dwight D. Eisenhower K-8, Glade View Elementary, and West Gate Elementary. District-wide, 143 district-operated schools, or 84 percent, earned A or B ratings, up from 120 schools the previous year. The district also achieved a record-setting 93.5 percent graduation rate.
