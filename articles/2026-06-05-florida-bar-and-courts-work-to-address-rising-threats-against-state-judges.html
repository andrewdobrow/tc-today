Florida Bar and Courts Work to Address Rising Threats Against State Judges

The Florida Bar is working to address an increasing problem of threats against judges across the state. The professional organization that regulates attorneys in Florida has made addressing judicial security and threats against the judiciary a priority.

The effort comes as judges in Florida face growing security concerns in their courtrooms and communities. Threats against judges can undermine the independence of the judicial system and create an atmosphere of intimidation that affects the administration of justice throughout the state. The Florida Bar's involvement signals recognition that protecting judges is essential to maintaining a functioning court system for all Floridians.

The initiative reflects broader concerns about the safety of judicial officers as they carry out their constitutional duties. Judges make decisions in criminal cases, civil disputes, family law matters, and other proceedings that affect millions of Florida residents. When judges face threats or intimidation, it can compromise their ability to render impartial decisions based solely on the law and facts. The Florida Bar has positioned this work as part of its mission of pursuing justice, promoting professionalism, and protecting the public.

The Florida Bar announced the effort on June 4, 2026, as one of its top stories. The organization oversees attorney conduct, provides continuing legal education, and works on issues affecting the legal profession and justice system in Florida. While the announcement did not detail specific threat incidents or proposed solutions, the Bar's public focus on the issue indicates that judicial security has become a significant concern for legal professionals and court administrators statewide. Florida judges serve at all levels of the state court system, from county courts handling smaller matters to circuit courts, district courts of appeal, and the Florida Supreme Court.
