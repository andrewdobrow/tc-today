Serena Williams withdraws from Wimbledon doubles with sister Venus due to knee injury

Serena Williams withdrew from her doubles match with sister Venus at Wimbledon on Saturday due to a right knee injury. The 44-year-old Serena was injured during her singles return earlier in the week in a three-set loss. She posted on Instagram that she was heartbroken to have to withdraw and that the opportunity to play alongside Venus once more meant the world to her. The post included images of four syringes that Serena said shows the fluid they drained from her knee after her singles match. An accompanying video showed her walking with strapping up and down her right leg and what appeared to be one of her daughters holding a cane for her. Serena was beaten 6-3, 6-7 (6), 6-3 by 20-year-old Maya Joint of Australia on Tuesday.

The Williams sisters, with Venus at 46 years old, were scheduled to play Camila Osorio and Solana Sierra. The match was the only first-round doubles match that wasn't scheduled earlier in the week, having been pushed back to give Serena more time to recover. With the U.S. Open approaching, Serena indicated she could play again somewhere else soon, saying to stay tuned to a city near you. Venus played in the mixed doubles tournament on Friday together with Kevin Krawietz, losing in straight sets to Tereza Mihalikova and Lloyd Glasspool. The Williams sisters are six-time champions as partners in women's doubles at Wimbledon.
