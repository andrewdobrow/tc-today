Jacksonville Mayor Deegan to Address Property Tax Overhaul Headed to November Ballot

Jacksonville Mayor Donna Deegan is expected to discuss the local impact of a major property tax overhaul that state lawmakers passed during a special session Thursday. The measure is now headed to the ballot in November, where Florida voters will decide whether to approve the significant changes to the state's property tax system.

The property tax overhaul emerged from a special legislative session and represents one of the most substantial proposed changes to Florida's tax structure in recent years. State lawmakers advanced the measure through both chambers before sending it to voters for final approval. The plan would affect property owners across Florida, though the specific details of the tax changes and their fiscal impact on local governments remain central to the ongoing discussion.

Mayor Deegan's briefing comes as local officials across Florida work to understand how the proposed overhaul would affect municipal budgets and services. Property taxes serve as a critical revenue source for cities, counties, and school districts throughout the state, funding essential services including public safety, infrastructure, and education. Any significant change to the property tax system could require local governments to adjust their budgets and potentially reconsider service levels or seek alternative revenue sources.

The November ballot will give Florida voters the opportunity to weigh in directly on the tax overhaul. Between now and the election, local officials, advocacy groups, and political organizations are expected to mount campaigns both supporting and opposing the measure. Jacksonville represents one of Florida's largest cities, and the mayor's perspective on the proposal will likely influence discussions in other major metropolitan areas across the state. The outcome of the November vote will determine whether Florida makes sweeping changes to a property tax system that has been in place for decades and affects millions of homeowners and businesses statewide.
