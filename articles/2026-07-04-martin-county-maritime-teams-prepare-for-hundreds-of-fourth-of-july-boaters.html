Martin County maritime teams deploy two boats with medics for Fourth of July weekend

Hundreds of boaters are expected on Florida waterways this Fourth of July weekend. The Martin County Maritime Response Unit is deploying two boats with medics on standby, stationed in the north and south of the county to respond to emergencies. Lt. Brett Harry said the team is prepared to assist with incidents ranging from minor injuries like stubbed toes to serious medical events including cardiac emergencies and potential spinal injuries.

Boaters like Brendan and Ashley Shannon launched from Sandsprit Park in Martin County on Friday with their young daughter and puppy, prioritizing safety during the holiday weekend. Lt. Harry urged boaters to keep their eyes on the water, designate a driver, and pack life vests, whistles and flotation devices. "Just have fun and be safe," Harry said.
