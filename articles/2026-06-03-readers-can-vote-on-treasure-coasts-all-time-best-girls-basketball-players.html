Readers Can Vote on Treasure Coast's All-Time Best Girls Basketball Players

Treasure Coast sports fans have the opportunity to vote on the region's all-time best girls basketball players in a new poll. The voting initiative invites local readers to weigh in on which athletes have made the biggest impact on the hardwood across Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River counties over the years.

The poll represents a chance for the community to celebrate the standout basketball talent that has come through local high schools. Girls basketball has a strong tradition on the Treasure Coast, with programs across the region producing college-level athletes and memorable teams over the decades.

Voting allows fans to recognize players who excelled in scoring, rebounding, defense, and leadership during their high school careers. The competition highlights the deep bench of athletic talent that has represented Treasure Coast schools in girls basketball.

The poll remains open for readers to cast their votes and determine which players will be remembered as the best to ever play girls basketball in the region. Community participation will determine the final rankings of these standout local athletes.
