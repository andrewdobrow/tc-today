Treasure Coast Readers to Vote on All-Time Best Girls Basketball Players

Treasure Coast residents are being asked to vote on the region's all-time best girls basketball players. The voting initiative invites local basketball fans to weigh in on which athletes have made the biggest impact on the hardwood across Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River counties over the years.

The poll represents an opportunity for the community to celebrate the legacy of standout girls basketball players who have represented Treasure Coast high schools. Girls basketball has long been a source of community pride across the region, with many athletes going on to play at the collegiate level and beyond.

Voting is currently open, allowing residents to participate in honoring the athletic achievements of local players. The selection covers players from across the Treasure Coast's high school programs, spanning multiple generations of competition.

The voting initiative highlights the continued interest in high school sports throughout the region. Results of the poll will recognize the top players as selected by the community, providing recognition for athletes who have excelled on local courts.
