Delray Beach opens 1976 Bicentennial Time Capsule for America's 250th anniversary

Delray Beach's Fourth of July Celebration, a family-friendly tradition drawing upward of 20,000 people for more than 50 years, is an official America250 partner event marking the nation's 250th anniversary. On the Main Stage at A1A, the city will open a 1976 Bicentennial Time Capsule containing a message from those who celebrated America's 200th birthday to those marking its 250th. At 6 p.m., a 60-foot flag will rise at Atlantic Avenue and Seabreeze Avenue alongside the National Anthem and a reading of the Declaration of Independence by the Delray Beach Historical Society.

Beach activities begin at 8 a.m. with the 4's on the 4th volleyball tournament and the Star Spangled Sandcastles contest, a sand-sculpting competition presented by local Rotary and Kiwanis clubs with judging at 11 a.m. keeping creations patriotic, not political. From noon to 1 p.m., guided snorkel and scuba tours explore the S.S. Inchulva, the British steamship torn apart by an 1903 hurricane 150 yards offshore. Evening entertainment features tribute act Let's Groove Tonight channeling Earth, Wind and Fire and DJ SoNyce on the Main Stage, with Digital Vibez on the East Road Stage from 6:30 to 9 p.m. A Zambelli fireworks display lights the Atlantic at 9 p.m. Road closures run noon to 11 p.m. on East Atlantic and A1A. Free FreeBee rides circulate downtown for visitors parking west of the Intracoastal Bridge. For details, call Parks and Recreation at (561) 243-7250, option 3.
