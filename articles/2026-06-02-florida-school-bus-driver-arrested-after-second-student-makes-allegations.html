Florida school bus driver arrested after second student makes allegations

A Florida school bus driver has been arrested following allegations from a second student. The arrest comes after authorities received multiple student complaints about the driver's conduct.

The case represents the latest concern about student safety on school buses across the state. School districts require bus drivers to undergo background checks and training before they can transport students, but incidents involving driver misconduct continue to prompt law enforcement investigations.

Authorities acted on the allegations after receiving reports from students. When a second student came forward with similar complaints, investigators moved to make an arrest. The driver now faces charges related to the alleged conduct involving students.

School officials are working with law enforcement as the investigation continues. Parents with children who ride school buses have been encouraged to talk with their kids about reporting any concerning behavior. The district will need to assign a replacement driver to cover the route while the case moves through the court system.
