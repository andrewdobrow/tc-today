Hurricane Season Ready: 12 Treasure Coast Essentials to Stock Up On

Affiliate disclosureTreasure Coast Today may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through links in this article, at no additional cost to you.

Hurricane preparation on the Treasure Coast works best when it happens before a storm enters the forecast cone. Even a relatively short outage can mean dark rooms, dead phones, limited internet access and no easy way to open shelf-stable food. A longer disruption can make stored water, backup charging, cooling and basic repair supplies much more important.

The 12 products below cover common gaps in a household hurricane kit: official weather information, portable power, lighting, water storage and filtration, first aid, food access and post-storm cleanup. These are researched products to consider, not items we claim to have personally tested.

Match capacity and compatibility to your household, follow official evacuation instructions and never risk personal safety to protect property.

A dedicated weather radio gives your household another way to receive urgent information when internet service, television or cellular networks are unreliable. Midland emergency radios combine NOAA weather reception with alternate power options and emergency lighting, making one a practical centerpiece for a storm kit.

Best for: Receiving official weather information during outages

  • Receives NOAA weather information and emergency alerts
  • Multiple charging options help during extended outages
  • Built-in flashlight and emergency signaling features

GearLight LED Flashlights

Flashlights remain useful even when lanterns are available because they provide a focused beam for checking a breaker panel, walking outside after the storm or inspecting a dark room. GearLight's compact tactical-style flashlights offer multiple modes and an adjustable beam in a size that is easy to distribute around the house.

Best for: Targeted lighting and post-storm inspections

Best for Room Lighting

Collapsible LED Lantern

A lantern spreads light through a room more effectively than a handheld flashlight. The collapsible design is especially convenient for hurricane kits because it stores compactly, then expands when you need area lighting in a kitchen, bathroom or central living space.

Best for: Lighting shared rooms during a power outage

  • Provides broad area lighting rather than a narrow beam
  • Collapsible body saves space between storm seasons
  • Carry handles make it easy to move or hang where needed

Best for Water Storage

Coozment Collapsible Water Container

Stored water should be part of the plan before a storm approaches, especially if a prolonged outage affects pumps, treatment systems or normal shopping. A collapsible container provides dedicated water storage without taking up the same amount of room when it is empty. Clean and sanitize it according to the manufacturer's directions before filling.

Best for: Filling with potable water before storm conditions arrive

  • Collapses for easier off-season storage
  • Dedicated container helps separate drinking water from utility water
  • Designed for easier carrying and controlled dispensing

KitchenAid Manual Can Opener

Shelf-stable food is only helpful when you can open it. A dependable manual can opener belongs in the same bin as canned meals, beans, fruit and pet food because it works without electricity and takes almost no storage space. KitchenAid's classic design uses comfortable handles and a large turning knob for a secure grip.

Best for: Opening shelf-stable food during an outage

  • Works without batteries or household power
  • Large turning knob and easy-grip handles
  • Small enough to keep permanently inside the food-supply bin

Best Backup Water Filter

LifeStraw Personal Water Filter

LifeStraw is a compact backup option when a safe water source is uncertain. Its membrane filter is designed to remove bacteria, parasites, microplastics, dirt and cloudiness, and the filter is rated for up to 1,000 gallons. It should supplement—not replace—stored drinking water, and the personal model does not remove viruses or every chemical contaminant.

Best for: Emergency backup filtration from appropriate freshwater sources

  • Lightweight and easy to store in a household or evacuation kit
  • Filters bacteria, parasites and microplastics from freshwater
  • No batteries or charging required

The MaiVoz model is listed with a 56,800mAh capacity, 22.5W fast charging and multiple outputs, giving a household a portable charging option for an evacuation or a room without power.

Best for Extended Outages

Renogy Portable Solar Panel

A portable solar panel can extend the usefulness of a compatible power station when utility restoration takes longer than expected. Renogy's foldable panels are designed for portable charging, but buyers should verify voltage, connector type and input limits before pairing a panel with any battery or power station. Output will also vary with sun angle, clouds and heat.

Best for: Recharging compatible portable power equipment in sunlight

  • Foldable design is easier to store and position outdoors
  • Can support daytime recharging of compatible battery systems
  • Useful beyond hurricane season for camping and off-grid needs

Best for Minor Injuries

BAND-AID Brand All-Purpose First Aid Kit

Storm preparation and cleanup can lead to small cuts, scrapes, burns and insect bites. An organized first aid kit keeps bandages, gauze, cleansing supplies and basic treatments together instead of scattered across several cabinets. This type of all-purpose kit is a useful starting point, but households should add prescriptions, allergy medication and any supplies specific to family members.

Best for: Minor cuts, scrapes and routine first aid

  • Organized supplies for common minor injuries
  • Portable case can move with an evacuation kit
  • Easy foundation for a household-specific medical kit

Best Storm-Cleanup Utility

Amazon Basics Weatherproof Multipurpose Tarp

A weather-resistant tarp can cover supplies, protect a vehicle interior, create temporary shade or help limit additional rain exposure after minor property damage. Amazon Basics tarps use laminated polyethylene material with reinforced edges and tie-down points. A tarp is only a temporary measure—never climb onto a wet or storm-damaged roof or use one as a substitute for professional repairs.

Best for: Temporary protection during cleanup and recovery

  • Useful for temporary coverage and protecting stored supplies
  • Reinforced edges and grommets support secure tie-downs
  • Folds relatively flat for garage or hurricane-bin storage

The best hurricane kit is the one assembled before shelves empty and delivery windows disappear. Charge every battery, test the radio and lights, sanitize water containers, check first aid expiration dates and keep the entire kit where everyone in the household can find it.

No collection of products replaces a family communication plan, an evacuation route or guidance from local emergency officials. Think of these items as practical support for the hours and days when normal utilities and routines are interrupted.
