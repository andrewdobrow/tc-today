400-pound black bear killed in Martin County traffic accident

A 400-pound black bear was struck and killed in a traffic accident in Martin County, according to wildlife officials quoted in local reports.

The incident occurred as Martin County experienced a significant spike in traffic fatalities. The same week saw at least five fatal crashes across the county within a four-day period. On Monday alone, a double fatal crash closed all lanes of Florida's Turnpike in both directions near mile marker 132 around 3 p.m. The wreck killed two people and sent a third person with traumatic injuries to the hospital. The closure lasted several hours, and authorities urged drivers to seek alternate routes while warning of potential delays lasting two to three hours.

Earlier that same Monday morning, a Palm City resident died in a separate two-vehicle crash on the Turnpike. Additionally, a head-on collision on Allapattah Road in Indiantown on Saturday morning claimed two more lives. The string of fatal accidents underscored dangerous driving conditions throughout the county during that period.

Wildlife officials noted that bear sightings remain uncommon in Martin County, making the fatal traffic accident noteworthy for local wildlife monitoring. The circumstances surrounding the bear's presence on a roadway and the specific details of the collision were not detailed in available reports. Authorities indicated they are continuing to monitor the situation as wildlife encounters in the area remain relatively rare events.
