Five people injured in I-95 crash and vehicle fire in Stuart Monday night

Five people were hospitalized Monday night after a multi-vehicle crash on Interstate 95 in Martin County that also sparked a vehicle fire. The collision occurred just before 10 p.m. on I-95 northbound near Exit 101 and Halpatiokee Regional Park. Martin County Fire Rescue responded to the scene, where they found one of the vehicles on fire and quickly extinguished the flames.

Two people suffered traumatic injuries and were transported to the hospital, while three others sustained minor injuries and were also taken for medical treatment. All northbound lanes of I-95 were closed while law enforcement investigated the crash. As of Tuesday morning, all lanes have reopened. The Florida Highway Patrol is investigating the crash.
