United States eliminated from World Cup after 4-1 loss to Belgium in round of 16

The United States' hopes for a deep World Cup run at home ended when Charles De Ketelaere scored twice and assisted on another goal, helping Belgium expose the Americans' defensive liabilities in a 4-1 win Monday night at Lumen Field in Seattle. De Ketelaere put Belgium ahead in the eighth minute, the first time in this year's World Cup the U.S. conceded first. Malik Tillman tied the score 1-1 in the 31st minute with his second free kick goal of the tournament, energizing a largely red-white-and-blue crowd of 66,925, but De Ketelaere scored again just 61 seconds after the ensuing kickoff. Belgium added a third goal in the 57th minute after goalkeeper Matt Freese lost control of the ball in front of his net, and second-half substitute Romelu Lukaku scored Belgium's final goal in the third minute of stoppage time.

American star Christian Pulisic injured his right foot when he hit a boot of Belgium captain Youri Tielemans on a 52nd-minute shot attempt and was replaced seven minutes later. Coach Mauricio Pochettino showed his frustration by kicking a rack in front of the bench, sending four water bottles flying. Belgium extended its unbeaten streak to 18 games and will play 2010 champion Spain on Friday at Inglewood, California, for a semifinal berth against France or Morocco. The U.S. failed to reach the quarterfinals for the first time since 2002 and lost to Belgium for the seventh straight time since a victory at the initial tournament in 1930. All six CONCACAF nations have been eliminated from the tournament.
