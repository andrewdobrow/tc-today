Boca Raton homeowner battles My Safe Florida Home grant delays despite completed storm upgrades

Anne Bendel of West Boca Raton completed $8,000 in storm mitigation work on her home and submitted all required documentation for a My Safe Florida Home grant, but her application remains stuck in review. Bendel first contacted WPTV in May when her grant was held up by paperwork from her insurance company, which was eventually resolved. My Safe Florida Home then told her in May that her status was complete. One week later, representatives asked for more documentation: a permit of completion for a large impact window. After she provided it, questions arose about dates, sending her application back under review. Bendel says she has completed the work, obtained the permits, and secured the required insurance.

Bendel describes herself as persistent and thorough but says the grant process has pushed her to her limits. She handles the entire process on her phone without a computer and has no one to help her. The My Safe Florida Home program provides grants of up to $10,000 to help homeowners across the state fortify homes against storms and earn insurance discounts. Bendel questions whether the program is being fair, noting that each time she completes a task, representatives seem to find another requirement. WPTV has contacted the Florida Department of Financial Services in Tallahassee about her case and the status of her grant. Bendel says her confidence in the program's promises is being shaken as she waits to see if she will receive any grant money.
