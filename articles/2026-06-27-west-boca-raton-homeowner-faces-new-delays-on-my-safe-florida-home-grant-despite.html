Boca Raton homeowner waits on My Safe Florida Home grant despite completed work

Anne Bendel says she has done everything required to receive a state grant for home improvements, but the My Safe Florida Home program keeps asking for more documentation. The West Boca Raton homeowner first reached out to WPTV in May after her application stalled due to missing paperwork from her insurance company. Once that issue was resolved, program officials told her in May that her status was complete.

The relief was short-lived. A week after receiving word her application was finished, My Safe Florida Home representatives requested additional documents, specifically a permit of completion for a large impact window. Bendel provided the permit, but then officials raised questions about dates on her paperwork. Her application went back under review, leaving her uncertain whether she will receive any of the grant money she was promised. She paid $8,000 out of pocket for the home improvements and is hoping the state program will help cover those costs.

Bendel describes the process as exhausting, especially because she is working through the application on her phone without a computer or assistance from others. "I did the work, I had it completed, I got the permits, I had the insurance they asked for. Everything is done, but it seems like when everything is done they find some other little task to send you," she said. She expressed frustration that the program continues to request additional items even after officials declared her application complete.

The My Safe Florida Home program offers grants of up to $10,000 to help homeowners across Florida strengthen their homes against storms and qualify for insurance discounts. The initiative has assisted thousands of residents. However, Bendel's experience has shaken her confidence in the program's ability to deliver on its promises. WPTV has reached out to the Florida Department of Financial Services in Tallahassee to inquire about Bendel's case and the current status of her grant application.
