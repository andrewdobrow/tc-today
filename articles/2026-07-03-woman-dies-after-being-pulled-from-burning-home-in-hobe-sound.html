Hobe Sound community raises funds for man who lost fiancée in deadly home fire

A Hobe Sound man is leaning on his community after losing his fiancée in a deadly home fire. Misty Dawn White, 46, was found dead Monday after a fire broke out inside the couple's home. Preliminary findings from the sheriff's office point to an electrical issue as a possible cause. The fire remains under investigation. Nina Fenn, a friend of White's for eight years, learned of the death through a phone call from White's fiancé, Mark. "We received a call from Mark stating that Misty had passed away in a fire," Fenn said.

Friends have launched a fundraiser to help Mark rebuild after the flames destroyed many of his and White's belongings. Fenn reflected on her friendship with White, describing her as a Christian woman who loved gardening and plants and played the piano. "She was always the first to lend a hand," Fenn said. "She loves kids. She loves people." The loss is compounded by grief Mark has already endured. According to Fenn, Mark lost his one and only child, Jacob, tragically. White's death adds another layer of heartbreak for those who knew her, as her birthday is July 4, meaning she passed just a few days before her birthday.
