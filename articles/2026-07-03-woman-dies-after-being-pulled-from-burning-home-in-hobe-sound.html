Hobe Sound community raises funds for man who lost fiance Misty Dawn White in deadly home fire

Misty Dawn White, 46, was found dead Monday after a fire broke out inside her home in Hobe Sound she shared with her fiancé Mark. Preliminary findings from the sheriff's office point to an electrical issue as a possible cause. The fire remains under investigation.

Nina Fenn, a friend of White's for eight years, learned of the death through a phone call from Mark. Friends have since launched a fundraiser to help Mark rebuild after the flames destroyed many of his and White's belongings. Fenn described the scene as "completely a state of devastation." White was a Christian woman who loved gardening, plants and playing the piano. Fenn said White was always the first to lend a hand, loved kids and loved people. The loss is compounded by tragedy Mark has already endured. Mark lost his one and only child, Jacob. Adding to the heartbreak, Misty's birthday is the Fourth of July, and she passed away just days before her birthday. Fenn said White's memory will live on in the hearts of those who loved her.
