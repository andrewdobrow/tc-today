Hobe Sound woman dies in home fire, community raises funds for fiance

Misty Dawn White, 46, died in a fire at her Hobe Sound home on Monday. Preliminary findings from the sheriff's office point to an electrical issue as a possible cause, though the fire remains under investigation. White's fiancé Mark learned of her death and has since received community support through a fundraiser launched by friends to help him rebuild after the flames destroyed many of his and White's belongings.

Nina Fenn, who knew White for eight years, described her as a Christian woman who loved gardening, plants, and playing piano. "She was always the first to lend a hand. She loves kids; she loves people," Fenn said. The loss carries additional weight for Mark, who previously lost his only child, Jacob, tragically. White's death is further compounded by timing, as her birthday was the Fourth of July and she died just days before. Fenn said White's memory will live on in the hearts of those who loved her.
