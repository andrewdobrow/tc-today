Hobe Sound community raises funds for man who lost fiance Misty Dawn White in deadly house fire

Misty Dawn White, 46, was found dead Monday after a fire broke out inside the couple's Hobe Sound home. Preliminary findings from the sheriff's office point to an electrical issue as a possible cause. The fire remains under investigation. Friends have launched a fundraiser to help White's fiancé Mark rebuild after the flames destroyed many of his and White's belongings.

Nina Fenn, a friend of White's for eight years, said she received a call from Mark stating that Misty had passed away in a fire. Fenn returned to the scene in the days following the tragedy to process her grief and described the situation as completely a state of devastation. Fenn said the loss is compounded by grief Mark has already endured after losing his one and only child, Jacob, tragically. Fenn said Misty was a Christian woman who loved gardening and plants, plays the piano, loves kids and was always the first to lend a hand. White's birthday is the Fourth of July, so she passed just a few days before her birthday. Fenn said White has always been a good soul and will always be in their hearts.
