Hobe Sound woman dies in home fire, friends rally to support surviving fiance

A Hobe Sound man is turning to his community for support after his fiancée died in a devastating home fire earlier this week. Misty Dawn White, 46, was found dead Monday when fire broke out inside the couple's residence. Investigators from the sheriff's office have identified a possible electrical issue as the cause, though the investigation remains ongoing. The loss has deeply affected those close to White, particularly her fiancé Mark, who is now facing the monumental task of rebuilding his life and home.

Nina Fenn, who had been friends with White for eight years, learned of the tragedy through a direct call from Mark. "We received a call from Mark stating that Misty had passed away in a fire," Fenn said. In the days following the incident, Fenn returned to the scene to process her grief. "It's just a terrible shame and awful loss of life," she said. Friends have since organized a fundraiser to help Mark recover from the fire, which destroyed much of the couple's belongings. "It's completely a state of devastation," Fenn said.

Speaking about White's character and passions, Fenn painted a picture of a generous and devoted woman. "Misty was a Christian woman. She loved gardening and plants. She plays the piano," Fenn recalled. "She was always the first to lend a hand. She loves kids; she loves her kids. She loves people." Those who knew White want her remembered as someone who consistently showed up for others and made a positive impact on the lives around her.

The timing of White's death adds profound sadness for Mark and those who cared about her. Mark is already grieving the tragic loss of his only child, Jacob. Adding to the heartache, White's birthday was July 4, just days after she passed away. Despite the overwhelming circumstances, Fenn remains committed to supporting Mark during this difficult period. "I just am trying whatever I can to support him," she said. Reflecting on her friendship, Fenn expressed confidence that White's memory will endure. "She's always been a good soul to me. She'll always be in our hearts," Fenn said.
