Martin County manufactured home owners can apply for storm repair funding starting in August

Martin County manufactured home owners will soon be able to apply for state funding to help with emergency repairs and storm-hardening improvements. An online application portal will open in August through Martin County Human Services. Once it launches, approximately 75 percent of the county's annual SHIP funding, which typically ranges from $1 million to $1.5 million, will become available to eligible mobile and manufactured homeowners.

This new program opportunity stems from Senate Bill 594, which took effect July 1st and expands access to $167 million in state affordable housing funding for manufactured home owners. The law makes money available for lot rent, emergency repairs, and storm-hardening retrofits. The legislative change was prompted by an investigation revealing that nearly 700,000 Florida mobile and manufactured homes, about two-thirds of the state's total housing stock, were built before current federal safety standards. These older homes cannot withstand modern storms, and many seniors living in them lack the financial resources to make necessary improvements. Repair estimates often range from $10,000 to $15,000 per home.

Julie Stellatos with Martin County Human Services says residents can complete applications online or request a paper version by calling the county office. Stellatos notes that homeowners have been requesting assistance long before this law passed. Since funding is distributed on a first-come, first-served basis, residents should prepare their documentation as soon as possible. Stellatos explains that all counties typically follow a similar timeline based on a calendar from the Florida Housing Coalition, though the specific schedule varies by county. Indian River County plans to present the legislative changes to its Board on August 18th with a state submission deadline of September 30th. St. Lucie County is still awaiting final state guidance before announcing its application process, while Okeechobee County is updating its local housing assistance plan to comply with the new law.

Residents can begin preparing now by visiting the Florida Housing Finance Corporation website and selecting their county or city. The website provides information about available funding, income eligibility requirements, contact information, and application instructions. Gathering necessary paperwork ahead of the portal opening will help ensure applicants can submit their requests quickly when the system becomes active.
