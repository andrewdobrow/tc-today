Stuart attorney represents daughter of Florida Turnpike crash victim in wrongful death lawsuit

Angeline Dowdian, 22, has filed a wrongful death lawsuit against truck driver Harjinder Singh, White Hawk Carriers, and freight broker C.H. Robinson following the August 12, 2025 crash in St. Lucie County that killed her mother Faniola Joseph, 37, and two others. The crash occurred when Singh allegedly attempted an illegal U-turn in a clearly marked No U-turn zone on the Florida Turnpike, causing the minivan carrying Joseph, Herby Dufresne, 30, and Rodrigue Dor, 54, to become wedged underneath his semi-truck. All three people in the minivan were killed.

Angeline is represented by attorney Dax Bello of Stuart firm Tillman, Fox, Bianchi & Kane, and co-counsel Mark Brumer of Brumer & Brumer. The lawsuit alleges that C.H. Robinson, a multibillion-dollar freight broker, knowingly gave business to White Hawk Carriers despite serious safety violations in its record. Transportation expert James Lewis has publicly described White Hawk's violations as very egregious. Bello argues the crash was not an accident but a predictable end that came at the cost of three lives.

The case is bolstered by a May 14 U.S. Supreme Court ruling in Montgomery v. Caribbean Transport, which clarified that federal law does not shield freight brokers from state negligence claims when unsafe drivers are placed on the road. That ruling involved the same broker named in Angeline's case. Justice Brett Kavanaugh wrote in a concurring opinion that not all truck accidents can be prevented, but some can, noting that some carriers are known to be less safe and some truck drivers are known to be unfit.

Angeline lived in another country at the time of the crash and had not seen her mother in person in six years, though they spoke daily via video calls. On the day of the crash, she missed a call from her mother. She listens to a voice message from her mother every morning that says, Angeline, where are you? Angeline says her mother was both parents to her, since her father rejected her at birth. Singh faces criminal traffic homicide charges in Florida in addition to the civil case. White Hawk Carriers has shut down, though Bello warns they may reopen under another name.
