Vero Beach airport adds 5 new nonstop destinations

Vero Beach Regional Airport announced the addition of five new nonstop flight destinations. The expansion increases travel options for Indian River County residents and visitors.

The new routes provide direct service from Vero Beach to destinations that previously required connections. The airport has been working to expand its service offerings to meet growing demand in the area.
