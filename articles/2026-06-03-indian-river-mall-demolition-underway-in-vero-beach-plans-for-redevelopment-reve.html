Indian River Mall demolition underway to make way for mixed-use development in Vero Beach

Crews began tearing down portions of the Indian River Mall this week as part of a revitalization project for the shopping center. Joseph Scarfone, the project manager working on the redevelopment project, said crews are working on the first phase, taking down the Macys and soon the former Sears location.

Neighbors gathered in the parking lot to watch the demolition. Mary Alice Stopyra said she is happy they are tearing it down because it has degraded over time. Mark Manera from Vero Beach said he has been coming to the mall for years and witnessed a steady decline year after year. Manera said change is needed and that if it brings new life, new jobs, and new opportunity, that would be wonderful.

Scarfone said the site will be transformed into a new sleek and modern indoor and outdoor shopping center, with around 300,000 square feet of retail, restaurants and possible residential spaces. Shoppers will not see a dead mall that was getting cobwebs for the last 12 years, he said, describing the project as a city within a city.

Scarfone said he hopes the first phase can be done by 2027. The team is still working on permitting for the rest of the project.
