Jensen Beach Park restrooms and concession building rebuild expected by late 2027

Beachgoers at Jensen Beach Park are waiting for the rebuild of the park's restrooms and concession building, which was destroyed in a fire on New Year's Eve in December 2024. The site is currently an empty plot of land. Beachgoer Gonda Ghebert reached out to WPTV's Let's Hear It inbox and wrote that beachgoers are left with only one very small restroom facility to serve the entire beach. Ghebert feels it has become increasingly unreasonable during weekends, holidays, and peak tourist season. Those who frequent the beach say they also miss the cafe that was lost in the fire.

Kevin Abbate, the Parks and Recreation director, says the permitting process takes time. The project must go through the Department of Environmental Protection because the park sits on a protected dune, and that permit is currently in process. He says the design for the new 9,000 square foot Sea Turtle Beach Cafe is 80% complete. The facility will be about 15 feet in elevation with 100 seats and 33 at the bar. Abbate says the county wants to do it right and that proper design will result in fewer issues and a smoother construction process.

The county says final funding is expected to be approved in September. They hope to start construction in early 2027 with a completion goal of late 2027, just in time for the winter season. The county told WPTV they do not have any plans for additional temporary restrooms or facilities while beachgoers wait for the new building to be completed.

Regular visitors to Jensen Beach Park say they are looking forward to the new facility. Julie Goings said the reason she went there was because they had a nice, clean facility with showers and it was convenient being able to get a snack or a drink coming off the deck. Sam Elliott said the only thing really missing is concessions and food availability. Both Elliott and Goings expressed hope that construction would move quickly, with Goings saying it would be nice to have another convenient spot and Elliott saying he hopes it moves real fast. Abbate believes the cafe will be worth the wait once construction is complete.
