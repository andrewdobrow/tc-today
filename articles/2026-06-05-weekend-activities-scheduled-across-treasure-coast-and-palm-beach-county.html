Things To Do 2026-06-05

Weekend activities scheduled across Treasure Coast and Palm Beach County

The Treasure Coast and Palm Beach County have multiple entertainment and recreational options available for residents this weekend across Martin, St. Lucie, Indian River, and Palm Beach counties.

According to WPBF, there are fun things to do throughout the Treasure Coast and Palm Beach area for those looking for weekend entertainment. Local venues are hosting a variety of events that cater to different interests and age groups.

The weekend schedule spans several communities including Stuart, Jensen Beach, Palm City, Port St. Lucie, Fort Pierce, and Vero Beach. Residents can choose from cultural events, outdoor activities, and community gatherings happening throughout both regions.

Families have options ranging from recreational programs to entertainment venues across the area. The diverse activity offerings mean there should be something for most people, whether they prefer indoor or outdoor pursuits.

Beyond this weekend, the broader Treasure Coast region continues to develop and grow. Martin County is currently studying a major transformation of Treasure Coast Square mall, according to WPEC. This redevelopment project could significantly impact the shopping and entertainment landscape in the area.

The region is also focused on community support and wellbeing. Cleveland Clinic Florida has expanded its food security efforts across the Treasure Coast, demonstrating a commitment to helping residents access necessary resources.

For those interested in local governance, Martin County is currently holding a County Commission race with a candidate field that continues to change, according to Treasure Coast News.

For residents wondering about their communities' walkability and urban design, the EPA has ranked various Treasure Coast cities according to their pedestrian accessibility. This data may be helpful for those considering relocating or simply interested in understanding their area's infrastructure.

When planning this weekend's activities, residents should check with local venues and event organizers for specific times, locations, and any admission requirements. Many community events are free or low-cost, making them accessible options for families and individuals with various budgets.

The Treasure Coast and Palm Beach County's combination of cultural attractions, outdoor recreation, and community events makes the region an attractive place to live and visit. Whether residents are looking for a relaxing weekend or something more active and engaging, the various activities available should provide ample entertainment options for all ages and interests.
