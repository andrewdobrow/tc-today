Treasure Coast Square Mall redevelopment study sparks mixed reactions in Martin County

Treasure Coast Square Mall remains a longtime community landmark, yet renewed discussion about its future has prompted questions from shoppers and business owners about what changes might mean for them. Martin County initiated a study in 2023 that examined potential redevelopment approaches, including residential apartments, an amphitheater, and outdoor green spaces. County officials have characterized the study as an exploratory planning exercise rather than a confirmed blueprint.

Community members express divided opinions about proposed updates. Shopper Liz Permar supports a renovation, calling it a positive step. Fellow shopper Kimberly Grucza also welcomes improvements but has reservations about certain proposals. While Grucza appreciates the concept of updating the mall, she opposes the idea of adding apartments to the property, citing the existing abundance of residential developments already in the area.

Business operators leasing space inside the mall are closely monitoring the redevelopment discussions. Jessica Henry, who owns Plantitude and Flame, hopes that any changes preserve the ability for businesses to continue operating within the property, preferably through expanded open-concept layouts. Ron Gustafson, owner of MVP Sport Cards, reports that his business has performed well at its current location. Despite this success, Gustafson has dealt with persistent water damage issues affecting his storefront. He believes the property requires significant maintenance and upgrades, stating that with proper effort and management, the mall could recover. "I think it's in neglect. I think with some effort and the right management I think this could turn around," Gustafson said.

Martin County Administrator Don Donaldson confirmed that the 2023 study remains what it was originally intended to be: a planning exercise. Officials have not indicated whether the county intends to pursue any of the redevelopment options explored in the study. For now, the future of Treasure Coast Square Mall remains uncertain, with community stakeholders awaiting clarity on whether the county will move forward with any concrete development plans.
