Treasure Coast Square mall redevelopment study sparks debate over apartments, outdoor concept

Treasure Coast Square Mall, a longtime community gathering place, faces an uncertain future as a 2023 Martin County redevelopment study continues to generate debate among residents and business owners. The study explored potential changes including apartments, an amphitheater, and green space designed around an outdoor concept. County officials have emphasized that the study was a planning exercise only, with no final decisions made about whether any proposed changes will actually be implemented.

Community shoppers express cautiously optimistic views about potential updates to the aging mall. Liz Permar said the facility has always been enjoyable to visit and believes a renovation would be beneficial. Kimberly Grucza acknowledged that while the mall isn't her favorite destination, she welcomes improvements to the property. However, not all proposed changes receive universal support. Grucza expressed concern about one specific proposal, noting that the area already has numerous apartments and adding more would be redundant. Despite reservations about certain elements, both shoppers indicated they are open to seeing the mall modernized.

Business owners operating inside the mall are closely monitoring the redevelopment conversation because any changes could directly affect their operations. Jessica Henry, who owns Plantitude and Flame, expressed hope that if redevelopment moves forward, the mall will maintain open concept store layouts that would allow existing businesses to continue functioning. Ron Gustafson, owner of MVP Sport Cards, reported that his store has performed very well at its current location, but he acknowledges that the mall requires attention and improvements. Gustafson has experienced problems with water leaking into his store and believes comprehensive renovation is overdue. He said the mall suffers from neglect and that with proper effort and management, the property could recover and thrive again.

Martin County officials and Treasure Coast Square have confirmed through statements that the 2023 study remains in the planning phase. The county commissioned the research to examine options for the mall property, but no commitments have been made to move forward with any of the proposed developments. Whether the community will ultimately see changes to the property depends on decisions still to be determined by county leadership and mall ownership. For now, shoppers and business owners continue to wait as local officials evaluate what steps, if any, will be taken next regarding the mall's future.
