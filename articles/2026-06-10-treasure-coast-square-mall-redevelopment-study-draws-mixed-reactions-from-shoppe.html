Treasure Coast Square Mall redevelopment study divides shoppers and business owners in Martin County

Treasure Coast Square Mall, a longtime community shopping destination, is generating discussion about its future following a 2023 study commissioned by Martin County. The study examined redevelopment possibilities that would transform the mall into an outdoor concept featuring apartments, an amphitheater, and green space. County officials and mall management have both characterized the 2023 study as a planning exercise rather than a definitive proposal.

Community members expressed mixed feelings about potential changes to the mall. Shopper Liz Permar said the mall "has always been a fun place to go to" and believes a facelift would benefit the property. Kimberly Grucza acknowledged the mall is not her favorite destination but said she welcomes an update. However, Grucza expressed reservations about one specific element of the redevelopment concept. "I don't like the idea of apartments just because we already have so many," she said, citing concerns about oversaturation in the area.

Business owners operating inside the mall are closely monitoring the redevelopment conversation. Jessica Henry, who owns Plantitude and Flame, expressed hope that any future changes would preserve opportunities for existing retailers. "I hope if it goes through you know they do more open concept stores where we're still able to have our business," Henry said. Ron Gustafson, owner of MVP Sport Cards, reported that his store performs well at its current location. "The model of the mall store has worked for us. The store has been tremendously successful," Gustafson said. Despite the store's success, Gustafson identified maintenance issues as a concern. He has experienced water leaking into his store and believes the property requires attention. "I think it's in neglect. I think with some effort and the right management I think this could turn around," he said.

County Administrator Don Donaldson confirmed that the 2023 study remains preliminary and does not represent a commitment to redevelopment. Treasure Coast Square management released a similar statement confirming the study's exploratory nature. At present, no decisions have been made about whether the redevelopment plans will move forward. The future direction of the mall ultimately depends on further discussions among county officials, property management, business operators, and the community.
