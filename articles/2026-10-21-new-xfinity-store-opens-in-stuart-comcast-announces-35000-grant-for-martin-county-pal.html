Comcast Opens New Xfinity Store in Stuart and Announces $35,000 Grant for Martin County PAL

Stuart, FL – Comcast is celebrating the grand opening of its newest Xfinity Store in Stuart. Located at 3955 Southeast Federal Highway, Stuart, FL 34997, the store features an interactive, modern design that gives residents the opportunity to explore the full suite of Xfinity products and services. Local business leaders can also visit the store to discuss their technology needs with a Comcast Business expert.

“Opening this new Xfinity Store reflects how important it is to give customers a welcoming, convenient place to learn about Xfinity services,” said Jackie Guerry Maybeck, Vice President of Sales & Marketing for Comcast’s Florida Region. “We look forward to serving the Stuart community and helping residents and businesses explore fast, reliable Xfinity Internet with powerful WiFi, Xfinity Mobile, and entertainment solutions that work best for them.”

Local leaders, community partners and Xfinity team members gathered on October 21 for a ribbon-cutting ceremony at the new Stuart Xfinity Store. The event featured remarks from Jackie Guerry Maybeck, Vice President of Sales & Marketing for Comcast’s Florida Region; Ed Ciampi, Martin County Commissioner; Ted Astolfi, CEO of the Economic Council of Martin County; Troy McDonald, Interim Executive Director of the Business Development Board of Martin County; and Noel DelValle, Executive Director at Martin County PAL.

“Stuart and communities across Martin County value strong connections and convenient access to the services people rely on every day,” said Edward V. Ciampi, Martin County Commissioner. “This new Xfinity Store gives our constituents and local businesses another place to get hands-on support and find technology that meets their changing needs.”

The opening also drew support from Martin County’s business community, whose leaders highlighted what the new location represents for the area’s continued growth.

“The opening of Xfinity’s store in Stuart is a meaningful investment in Martin County, and a strong reflection of the confidence businesses continue to place in our community,” said Ted Astolfi, CEO of the Economic Council of Martin County. “A strong local economy depends on giving people and businesses the tools to move ideas forward, and this new location creates a place where customers can explore new technology and find solutions that fit the way they live and work.”

“Stuart continues to grow as a place where families can thrive and businesses can build for the future, and investments that bring new services and resources to our community help support that momentum,” said Troy McDonald, Interim Executive Director of the Business Development Board of Martin County. “We appreciate Comcast’s continued commitment to the area and look forward to seeing this new Xfinity Store become a valuable resource for residents, local businesses and families across our community.”

The new storefront expands Xfinity’s retail footprint in the Sunshine State, following recent store opening in Doral, Jacksonville, Fort Myers, Delray Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Kendall, Miami Gardens, Naples, North Miami, and Tallahassee. It also strengthens Xfinity’s presence in the Treasure Coast with three additional Xfinity Stores located nearby:

  • Stuart (Jensen Plaza) - 2830 NW Federal Highway, Stuart, FL 34994
  • Port St. Lucie - 277 SW Port St Lucie Blvd, Port St. Lucie, FL 34984
  • Vero Beach - 5840 20th Street, Vero Beach, FL 32966

During the grand opening, Comcast announced a $35,000 grant to Martin County PAL to support its Rising Responders: Digital Pathways to Emergency Medical Careers program. The initiative will equip 12 underserved middle and high school students with the technology, digital literacy and workforce skills needed to pursue careers in emergency medical services and healthcare.

The grant will also provide broadband training and digital navigation services to PAL families and community members, reaching an estimated 200 people annually through workshops and one-on-one support. The investment is part of Project UP, Comcast’s $1 billion commitment to advancing digital opportunity and building a future of unlimited possibilities.

“This investment will give young people access to the technology, training and practical skills they need to explore careers in emergency medical services and healthcare,” said Noel DelValle, Executive Director at Martin County PAL. “It will also expand critical digital literacy resources and one-on-one support to families across our community, extending the impact well beyond the students in the program.”

Store employees are ready to welcome customers into the new retail space, where visitors can explore the latest Xfinity Internet, Mobile, TV and smart home technologies, see products in action and receive personalized support from on-site specialists.

Explore Innovative Products

  • Xfinity Internet: Learn about multi-gigabit Internet speeds, powerful WiFi that reaches every corner of the home and super-responsive connections with low lag, all to power streaming, gaming, videoconferencing, and more – all available with a five-year price guarantee.
  • Xfinity Mobile and Comcast Business Mobile: Purchase new mobile phones, devices, and accessories, and get exclusive access to speeds of up to 1 gigabit per second with WiFi PowerBoost when connected to millions of Xfinity WiFi hotspots across the country – regardless of home Internet speed. Customers can get the first line of Xfinity Mobile included for a year with qualifying Xfinity Internet plans.
  • Xfinity TV: Xfinity brings together live TV, streaming, sports, and on-demand content in one easy-to-use experience. With the award-winning Xfinity Voice Remote, finding a show, channel, or game is fast, easy, and frustration-free.
  • Xfinity Home Solutions: Help protect what matters most with solutions ranging from WiFi Shield, included with eligible Xfinity Internet service, to Shield Select for self-monitored home protection, and Xfinity Home Security for professionally-monitored security.
  • Comcast Business: Connect with a subject-matter expert to discuss Comcast Business’ full suite of solutions, including fast, reliable connectivity, secure networking solutions, advanced cybersecurity, and a range of managed service options to meet the needs of businesses of all sizes.

Connect to the Nation’s Most Reliable WiFi Day One

New customers now have the option to choose same-day pick up at this local Xfinity store or, if available, same-day Gateway delivery to their home. And, even before the Gateway arrives, customers have immediate access to Xfinity’s network of more than 23 million secure WiFi hotspots the moment they sign up.

Engage with a Local, Dedicated Team of Experts

Xfinity Stores are staffed with highly trained, knowledgeable sales consultants who can walk customers through product demonstrations, help them sign up for services, learn more about their current services or devices, review accounts, manage equipment, and more.

Visitors can drop in and explore the store instead of waiting in line with a time-saving queuing system, or go online to https://www.xfinity.com/support/contact-us, click “Find one near you” and make an appointment.

The new Stuart Xfinity Store is open Monday through Saturday from 9 a.m. – 8 p.m., and Sunday from 10 a.m. – 6 p.m. For directions and store hours, click here.
