Port St. Lucie to gain professional soccer team in USL League One expansion

Port St. Lucie will welcome a new professional soccer team as part of an expansion by USL League One, according to a report by The Boca Raton Tribune. The announcement marks a significant addition to the city's sports and entertainment landscape, bringing third-division professional soccer to St. Lucie County.

Fort Lauderdale will also receive a USL League One franchise as part of the same expansion effort. USL League One represents the third tier of American soccer, positioned below Major League Soccer and the USL Championship. The league has been steadily expanding across the country, targeting markets that can support professional soccer while building local fan bases.

The arrival of a professional soccer team provides Port St. Lucie with a new community attraction and could bring economic benefits through game day attendance, local sponsorships, and youth soccer development programs. Many USL League One teams work closely with youth soccer organizations in their communities to develop local talent and create pathways for young players.

Further information about the team's name, home venue, ownership group, and inaugural season timeline will be announced as the expansion plans develop. Port St. Lucie residents interested in professional soccer will soon have a local team to support.
