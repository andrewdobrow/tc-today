Threshers defeat Mets 10-4 in Port St. Lucie

The Clearwater Threshers cruised to a 10-4 victory over the St. Lucie Mets on Friday in Port St. Lucie. The game marked a decisive win for the visiting Threshers in Florida State League play.

The Threshers built a commanding lead against the Mets, the home team based at Clover Park in Port St. Lucie. The 10-4 score represented a six-run margin of victory for Clearwater.

The loss continued a difficult stretch for the St. Lucie Mets, who play their home games in Port St. Lucie as the Class A affiliate of the New York Mets. The Threshers, affiliated with the Philadelphia Phillies, demonstrated strong offensive performance throughout the game.

The St. Lucie Mets will look to rebound in their next home stand at Clover Park. The team continues Florida State League play through the summer season.
