Junior Lifeguard Camp Opens on Treasure Coast for Summer

The Junior Lifeguard Camp is opening for its first two weeks of summer programming on the Treasure Coast, offering young residents hands-on training in water safety and beach rescue skills. The program provides children the opportunity to learn from certified lifeguards while building confidence in the ocean and developing physical fitness.

The camp runs during weeks one and two of the summer season, giving participants instruction in swimming techniques, first aid basics, and coastal safety awareness. Young campers work alongside experienced beach safety professionals who guide them through age-appropriate scenarios and skill-building exercises.

Junior lifeguard programs have become popular summer options for coastal families seeking enriching activities that combine education with outdoor recreation. The camps teach children to recognize dangerous conditions, understand rip currents, and develop respect for ocean environments while fostering teamwork and leadership.

Families interested in enrolling should check with local parks and recreation departments for registration information and availability. The program typically fills quickly as demand for quality summer camps continues to grow across Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River counties.
