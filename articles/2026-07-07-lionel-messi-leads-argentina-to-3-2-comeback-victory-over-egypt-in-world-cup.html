Argentina Rallies Past Egypt 3-2 as Messi Scores in World Cup Round of 16

Argentina defeated Egypt 3-2 on Tuesday despite trailing by two goals with only 11 minutes of regulation time to play. Lionel Messi scored his record-extending 21st goal at the World Cup in the 83rd minute to level the score at 2-2. Enzo Fernandez scored the winning goal in stoppage time to complete the comeback. Cristian Romero started the rally in the 79th minute. The goal was Messi's eighth of the tournament. Messi was in tears after the final whistle. Fernandez said this is a phenomenal group that never gives up and that four years have passed since Qatar.

Argentina had trailed 2-0 on goals from Yasser Ibrahim and Mostafa Zico. Ibrahim scored in the 15th minute when he got ahead of Lisandro Martinez to meet Marwan Attia's cross and head the ball into the bottom corner. Zico's goal came in the 67th minute. Messi missed a penalty kick in the first half when his shot was saved by Egypt goalkeeper Mostafa Shobeir after Haissem Hassan tripped Nicolas Tagliafico in the box. Messi has now missed four of eight penalty kicks at World Cups. He later hit the post when the score was 1-0. The defending champions will next face either Switzerland or Colombia in the quarterfinals on Saturday in Kansas City, Missouri. Argentina is bidding to become the first team to win back-to-back titles since Brazil did so in 1958 and 1962.
