Lionel Messi leads Argentina to dramatic 3-2 World Cup comeback victory over Egypt

Lionel Messi scored his record-extending 21st World Cup goal in the 83rd minute to level the score at 2-2, and Enzo Fernandez netted the winning goal in stoppage time to complete Argentina's improbable 3-2 victory over Egypt on Tuesday. The defending champions trailed by two goals with only 11 minutes of regulation time remaining before Cristian Romero started the rally in the 79th minute. Messi, who was in tears after the final whistle, scored his eighth goal of the tournament. The 39-year-old led Argentina to the comeback despite missing a first-half penalty kick that was saved by Egypt goalkeeper Mostafa Shobeir. Messi later hit the post when the score was 1-0, marking his second penalty miss of the tournament after also failing from the spot against Austria in the group stage.

Argentina will next face either Switzerland or Colombia in the quarterfinals on Saturday in Kansas City, Missouri. The team is bidding to become the first to win back-to-back titles since Brazil did so in 1958 and 1962. Egypt took a surprising lead in the 15th minute when Yasser Ibrahim got ahead of Lisandro Martinez to meet Marwan Attia's cross and head the ball into the bottom corner. Mostafa Zico scored Egypt's second goal in the 67th minute. Argentina coach Lionel Scaloni said he was so emotional about the group of players.
