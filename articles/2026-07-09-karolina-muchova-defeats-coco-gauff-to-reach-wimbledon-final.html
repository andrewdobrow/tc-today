Karolina Muchova ends Coco Gauff's Wimbledon run in dramatic tiebreaker

The ninth-ranked Muchova will face either Linda Noskova or Marta Kostyuk of Ukraine in Saturday's final. It will be Muchova's second Grand Slam final after losing to Iga Swiatek in the 2023 French Open championship match. Muchova is the fourth Czech woman in the last six years to reach the Wimbledon final. The other Czech women in the Wimbledon final recently were Karolina Pliskova, runner-up in 2021, Marketa Vondrousova, champion in 2023, and Barbora Krejcikova, champion in 2024.

For the seventh-ranked American Gauff, it was still her most successful Wimbledon. Previously, Gauff had gone only as far as the fourth round three times, including during her breakthrough run as a 15-year-old in 2019. Muchova's 6-2, 1-6, 7-6 (10) victory Thursday ended with a drama-filled tiebreaker on Centre Court. The men's semifinals on Friday feature top-ranked and defending champion Jannik Sinner against seven-time Wimbledon winner Novak Djokovic and French Open champion Alexander Zverev against British wild card Arthur Fery.
