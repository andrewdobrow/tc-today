Oxbridge Academy football roster grows under new head coach Mike Colee

The Oxbridge Academy Thunderwolves are preparing for the upcoming football season under the direction of newly appointed head coach Mike Colee. Just weeks into his tenure, Colee has already begun reshaping the program and boosting roster numbers. He explained that initial enrollment was modest because prospective players had already committed to summer plans before he arrived. Once he connected with parents and students and distributed the team schedule, participation began to increase consistently.

The coaching philosophy Colee has brought to the program is already earning respect from his players. Senior Cayden Pribramski highlighted Colee's emphasis on mental toughness and his ability to leverage each player's individual strengths. Pribramski noted that Colee does not impose a rigid, inflexible approach to coaching. Instead, Colee tailors his strategies around his players and supports them in reaching their potential. According to Pribramski, this personalized method of development will be crucial to the team's immediate success and the program's long-term growth.

The team is motivated to move forward from a difficult previous season. Last year, the Thunderwolves finished with an 0-11 record, a disappointing mark they are determined to overcome. Senior Brady Basis expressed the team's mindset going into the new season, saying the players are committed to sustained hard work and continuous improvement. He mentioned that the team recently achieved a positive outcome in spring competition and intends to carry that momentum and enthusiasm into the fall campaign.

As the regular season approaches, Colee continues to attract new players to the program. The focus remains on building mental resilience and developing individual player skills as the Thunderwolves prepare for competition. With renewed leadership and growing participation, the program appears positioned for a turnaround from last year's struggles.
