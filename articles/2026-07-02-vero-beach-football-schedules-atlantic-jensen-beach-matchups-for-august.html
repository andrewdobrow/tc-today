Vero Beach football schedules Atlantic, Jensen Beach matchups for August

Vero Beach High School has scheduled three football games across the late summer and early fall of 2026. The team will begin its season with a matchup against Jensen Beach on August 21, 2026, followed by a game against Atlantic one week later on August 28, 2026, according to Sports Illustrated.

Both of these early season contests fall within August, with the Atlantic game taking place during the final weekend of the month. These matchups will kick off Vero Beach's football campaign as the team prepares for the remainder of its schedule.

Beyond the August games, Vero Beach has a third scheduled matchup lined up for September 25, 2026, when the team faces Central. This game extends the early part of the season into the fall months, providing the team with three significant competitions spread across late summer and early autumn.

The three confirmed games represent the beginning of what appears to be a full schedule for the Vero Beach football program in 2026. Fans interested in following the team can track these matchups and potentially look forward to additional games beyond those currently announced.
