My Safe Florida Home grant delays leave homeowners unable to afford required upgrades

Kelly Hagen of Vero Beach was turned away for a $10,000 My Safe Florida Home grant after it took too long to find a contractor. Her insurance company requires impact windows, an expensive upgrade she has struggled to afford while working two jobs. She works a full-time job plus nights and weekends for Instacart but still cannot manage the costs. The roadblock Hagen faced in accessing the grant is one many Florida homeowners have encountered with the program.

Hagen's homeowners insurance costs $9,000 a year, which equals $750 a month on top of her mortgage payment for her three-bedroom, two-bath home of about 1,500 square feet that was built in 1994. Her premium went from $6,000 to $8,000 the following year and has continued increasing. Hagen said she can only hang on for probably just a couple more months before she will have to try to sell her house. She described the prospect of leaving as devastating.
