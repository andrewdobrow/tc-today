Jensen Beach teen Harper Aberman to represent Team USA at Sailing World Championship in France

Harper Aberman, a teenager from Jensen Beach, will represent Team USA at the Sailing World Championship in France this weekend. Her competitive sailing journey started at age four when her grandparents enrolled her in a four-week summer camp in Pennsylvania. Aberman quickly fell in love with the sport, unlike her cousin who disliked the experience. She ended up extending her stay to eight weeks. "They then decided to throw my cousin and me into summer camp, where they then did a four-week course, and my cousin hated it, and I fell in love with it, and I ended up going for eight weeks," Aberman said.

After her family relocated to Jensen Beach, Aberman joined the green fleet program at the US Sailing Center of Martin County, where her competitive sailing career truly developed. Looking back on that decision, Aberman said, "That is where the sailing adventure took off for me." She describes herself as an adrenaline junkie who thrives in challenging conditions. Aberman is drawn to windy situations on the water, though she acknowledges they present difficulties. "When it's windy, I've always struggled to stay upright and stay out of the water," Aberman said. Despite her modest approach to discussing her own abilities, she has accumulated an impressive record in youth sailing competitions.

Aberman has won countless tournaments and placed in several national competitions, establishing herself as one of the biggest names in the youth sailing circuit. She learned she would represent Team USA during Spring Break when she received the exciting news via email. The announcement triggered an immediate celebration with her family. "It was during Spring Break, I got the email. I started screaming, my parents started screaming, and we were like, 'This is not real. We're going to Europe this summer," Aberman said.

In preparation for the championship, Aberman has been training in Spain over the past several weeks. Racing begins Monday at the venue. Aberman expressed confidence about competing at this level while acknowledging the demands of preparing for such high-level competition. "We plan on taking the waters Monday for racing, and it's just been a lot of recovery and a lot of time and effort out on the water, but it's definitely going to be worth it. Especially in the venue we're in," Aberman said. Her journey from a summer camp discovery to international competition represents a significant achievement in youth sailing.
