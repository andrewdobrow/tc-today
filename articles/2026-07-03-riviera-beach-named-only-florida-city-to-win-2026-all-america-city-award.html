Riviera Beach named only Florida city to receive 2026 All-America City Award

Riviera Beach was named one of just 10 communities in the U.S. to receive the 2026 All-America City Award from the National Civic League, making it the only Florida city selected for the honor this year. The award was announced during the National Civic League conference in Denver. The prestigious award recognizes communities that bring residents, local government, businesses and organizations together to solve local challenges and create lasting change. Riviera Beach was a first-time winner.

Mayor Douglas Lawson called the recognition a proud moment for the city, saying "Little old Riviera Beach is one of the biggest, brightest, shining stars." Riviera Beach's application highlighted several programs including the Riviera Beach Youth Council, the Citizens Leadership Academy with a 98% completion rate, and the Neighborhood Reclaim Home Rehabilitation Program. Councilwoman KaShamba Miller-Anderson credited residents for the honor, stating "This award is a tribute to the heart and determination of our residents." She added that winning the award reinforces community pride and the city's positive momentum. The All-America City Award has been presented annually since 1949 and is considered one of the nation's most respected civic honors.
