Riviera Beach named only Florida city to win 2026 All-America City Award

The City of Riviera Beach was named one of just 10 communities in the U.S. to receive the 2026 All-America City Award from the National Civic League, making it the only Florida city selected for the honor this year. The award was announced during the National Civic League conference in Denver, where Riviera Beach leaders presented the city's efforts to strengthen community engagement. Mayor Douglas Lawson called the recognition a proud moment for the city, stating "Little old Riviera Beach is one of the biggest, brightest, shining stars." Lawson said the city earned the award through a long-term effort to revitalize Riviera Beach and improve quality of life for residents.

Riviera Beach's application highlighted several programs designed to increase civic participation and neighborhood investment, including the Riviera Beach Youth Council, which helps high school students become involved in civic leadership and advocacy, the Citizens Leadership Academy, a civic education initiative with a 98% completion rate, and the Neighborhood Reclaim Home Rehabilitation Program, which provides mini-grants to help longtime homeowners make critical repairs. Councilwoman KaShamba Miller-Anderson credited residents for the honor, saying "This award is a tribute to the heart and determination of our residents." The All-America City Award has been presented annually since 1949 and is considered one of the nation's most respected civic honors.
