Riviera Beach named only Florida city to receive 2026 All-America City Award

Riviera Beach was named one of 10 communities in the United States to receive the 2026 All-America City Award from the National Civic League, becoming the only Florida city selected for the honor. The award recognizes communities that bring residents, local government, businesses and organizations together to solve local challenges and create lasting change. City leaders presented Riviera Beach's efforts to strengthen community engagement at the National Civic League conference in Denver, where judges announced the city as a first-time winner. Mayor Douglas Lawson said the city earned the award through a long-term effort to revitalize Riviera Beach and improve quality of life for residents. "They're seeing the changes; they're seeing the revitalization and the reimagining of Riviera Beach that we set out so many years to do," Lawson said.

Riviera Beach's application highlighted several civic engagement programs including the Riviera Beach Youth Council for high school students, the Citizens Leadership Academy with a 98 percent completion rate, and the Neighborhood Reclaim Home Rehabilitation Program providing mini-grants to longtime homeowners. Councilwoman KaShamba Miller-Anderson credited residents for the honor, stating "Every event we host, every project we complete, and every challenge we overcome happens because our community shows up for one another." She said winning the award reinforces the city's positive momentum and positions the community for future opportunities and continued growth.
