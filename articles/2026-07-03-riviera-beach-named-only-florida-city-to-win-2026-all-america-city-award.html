Riviera Beach named only Florida city to win 2026 All-America City Award

Riviera Beach has been selected as one of just 10 communities across the United States to receive the 2026 All-America City Award from the National Civic League, making it the only Florida city honored this year. The announcement was made during the National Civic League conference in Denver, where city leaders presented Riviera Beach's work in strengthening community engagement and civic participation. Mayor Douglas Lawson expressed pride in the recognition, stating that "Little old Riviera Beach is one of the biggest, brightest, shining stars." He credited the award to the city's long-term commitment to revitalizing Riviera Beach and improving quality of life for residents.

The All-America City Award recognizes communities that bring together residents, local government, businesses and organizations to solve local challenges and create lasting change. City leaders emphasized unity during their presentation before the judges, saying "We are one community and one city and one Riviera Beach." Mayor Lawson noted that residents are witnessing the transformation firsthand. "They're seeing the changes; they're seeing the revitalization and the reimagining of Riviera Beach that we set out so many years to do," he said. The city's application highlighted three key programs demonstrating civic engagement and neighborhood investment: the Riviera Beach Youth Council, which develops civic leadership skills among high school students; the Citizens Leadership Academy, a civic education program with a 98 percent completion rate; and the Neighborhood Reclaim Home Rehabilitation Program, which offers mini-grants to longtime homeowners for critical home repairs.

Councilwoman KaShamba Miller-Anderson credited residents for the honor, saying the award reflects community dedication and collaboration. "This award is a tribute to the heart and determination of our residents. Every event we host, every project we complete, and every challenge we overcome happens because our community shows up for one another," Miller-Anderson said. She emphasized that the city is committed to treating civic engagement as an ongoing partnership between government, residents, faith groups, schools and community organizations. Miller-Anderson added that winning reinforces the city's positive momentum and brings back community pride.

The All-America City Award has been presented annually since 1949 and ranks among the nation's most respected civic honors. City officials say the designation celebrates Riviera Beach's progress and positions the community for future opportunities and growth. The award sends a message that Riviera Beach is a place where collaboration drives results and residents remain actively involved in shaping their community's future. Other communities receiving the 2026 award include Chelsea, Massachusetts; Franklin, Tennessee; Harlingen, Texas; Montgomery, Alabama; Morrisville, North Carolina; Norfolk, Virginia; North Charleston, South Carolina; Roanoke, Virginia; and Woodburn, Oregon.
