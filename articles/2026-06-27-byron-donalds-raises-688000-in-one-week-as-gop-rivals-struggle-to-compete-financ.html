Byron Donalds extends fundraising dominance in Florida Governor's race with $90 million raised

U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds has raised $90 million in his bid for the Republican gubernatorial nomination, according to his campaign. Division of Elections records through June 26 show nearly $10 million in contributions to his campaign account and more than $76.2 million to the Friends of Byron Donalds political committee. Between June 20 and June 26, Donalds raised nearly $2 million, with major contributors including GOPAC Election Fund, Ferreira Construction of New Jersey, Phillips Infrastructure of Tennessee, and the Southern Group. Donalds recently launched a $20 million statewide ad buy on television and digital platforms. He has secured endorsements from U.S. Sen. Ashley Moody, Attorney General James Uthmeier, Agriculture Secretary Wilton Simpson, CFO Blaise Ingoglia, incoming Florida Senate President Jim Boyd, and Florida House Speaker-designate Sam Garrison.

His opponents trail significantly in fundraising. Lieutenant Governor Jay Collins raised nearly $15,500 in hard money and just $100 to his Quiet Professionals FL political committee during the week ending June 26, with less than $3.5 million total as of that date. Former Florida House Speaker Paul Renner and investment manager James Fishback continue to fall further behind. The Republican primary is scheduled for August 18.
