Byron Donalds raises $90 million for Governor's race as opponents struggle to keep pace

U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds has raised $90 million for his gubernatorial campaign, according to Division of Elections records through June 26. His campaign account received nearly $10 million in contributions, while his Friends of Byron Donalds political committee raised more than $76.2 million. Between June 20 and June 26 alone, Donalds raised nearly $2 million, with major contributors including GOPAC Election Fund, Ferreira Construction of New Jersey, Phillips Infrastructure of Tennessee, and the Southern Group. Donalds has invested heavily in his campaign with a $20 million statewide ad buy on television and digital platforms. He has also consolidated endorsements from U.S. Sen. Ashley Moody, Attorney General James Uthmeier, Agriculture Secretary Wilton Simpson, CFO Blaise Ingoglia, incoming Florida Senate President Jim Boyd, and Florida House Speaker-designate Sam Garrison.

In stark contrast, Lt. Gov. Jay Collins raised only $15,500 in hard money and $100 to his Quiet Professionals FL political committee during the same week, with less than $3.5 million total as of June 26. Collins struggled during a Friday night debate, fumbling answers on issues ranging from data centers to voting for DeSantis in 2018, which he could not do because he was not a Florida resident at that time. Collins lost his temper at various points during the debate and needed notes to help with responses.
