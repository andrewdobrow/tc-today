Port St. Lucie to Gain Professional Soccer Team in USL League One

Port St. Lucie is gaining a professional soccer team as part of USL League One's expansion plans. The city will join Fort Lauderdale in welcoming teams to the third-tier professional soccer league, marking a significant addition to the Treasure Coast sports landscape.

USL League One operates as part of the United Soccer League system, which provides a pathway for player development and professional soccer competition across the United States. The addition of a Port St. Lucie franchise will bring regular professional sporting events to St. Lucie County, offering residents the opportunity to support a local professional team.

The expansion reflects growing interest in soccer across Florida and represents a notable investment in Port St. Lucie's sports infrastructure. Professional soccer teams typically play their home matches from spring through fall, bringing visitors and economic activity to their host communities throughout the season.

The timing of the team's first season and additional details about stadium arrangements and team branding will be announced as the expansion plans move forward. Port St. Lucie's entry into professional soccer places the city among a growing number of mid-sized American cities hosting USL League One franchises.
