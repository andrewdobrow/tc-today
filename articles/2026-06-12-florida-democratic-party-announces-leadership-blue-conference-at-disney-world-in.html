Florida Democratic Party Announces Leadership Blue Conference at Disney World in July

The Florida Democratic Party is preparing for its signature annual event as Leadership Blue Weekend returns to Central Florida next month. The conference will run July 17-19 at Disney's Gran Destino Tower at Coronado Springs Resort in Lake Buena Vista. The multiday gathering brings together candidates, activists, and party leaders to establish shared campaign priorities ahead of the 2026 election cycle. Sessions will include campaign training, policy discussions, strategy workshops, and networking opportunities designed to help Democrats prepare for the midterm elections. The event has historically functioned as a coordinating hub where Democratic campaigns, advocacy organizations, and party groups align efforts and build support before major statewide races.

The conference schedule culminates with the annual Leadership Blue Gala, the party's premier fundraising event. This gala provides Democratic candidates and organizations with a critical opportunity to connect with supporters and donors as the campaign season accelerates. Party officials have confirmed that prominent national leaders will participate in the event, though the complete speaker roster has not yet been revealed. Previous Leadership Blue conferences have included notable figures such as U.S. Senator Cory Booker and Kentucky Governor Andy Beshear, establishing the event's track record of attracting high-profile Democratic voices.

The gala is anticipated to draw elected officials, major donors, grassroots activists, and national media attention. Democrats see this gathering as essential to rebuilding momentum in Florida ahead of the 2026 midterm elections. The state has historically represented a challenging electoral landscape for the party in recent years, making the coordinated efforts fostered by Leadership Blue increasingly important to their strategic planning.

Interested attendees can access tickets and the full conference schedule through LeadershipBlue.com. The event represents a significant moment in the Democratic Party's 2026 electoral strategy, offering a structured opportunity for party members across different levels and roles to unify around shared campaign goals and messaging before heading into the election cycle.
