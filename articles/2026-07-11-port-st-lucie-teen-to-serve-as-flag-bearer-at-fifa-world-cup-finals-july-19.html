Port St. Lucie teen named Southeast Region Youth of the Year surprised with FIFA World Cup finals trip

Enrique Rosario Liz, a Port St. Lucie teenager named Youth of the Year for the Southeast Region of the Boys & Girls Clubs of America, was surprised at the Boys & Girls Club of St. Lucie County in Fort Pierce with a trip to the FIFA World Cup finals. He will serve as one of 12 club members from across the country selected to be an official Coca-Cola flag bearer at the finals on July 19. The surprise came courtesy of the Boys & Girls Clubs of America and their partnership with the Coca-Cola Company. When the box containing the news was walked into the room surrounded by fellow club members, Rosario Liz said the moment was crazy and insane. Along with the trip, he received official gear inside the box.

Rosario Liz said the Boys & Girls Clubs has been his everything and credited the club with shaping who he has become as a leader. He said the club helped him find what his leadership style was going to be and redefined what his definition of leadership was. Rosario Liz had already been juggling a packed summer, preparing for move-in at the University of Florida and an internship, but said his time is really being taken up by the World Cup right now, forcing his parents to watch every game. He closed with a message for younger club members, asking them to please stay with their Boys & Girls Clubs because one day he wants to see each and every one of them in the same position that he is in right now.
