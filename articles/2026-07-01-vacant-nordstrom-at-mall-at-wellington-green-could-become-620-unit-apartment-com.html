Vacant Nordstrom at Mall at Wellington Green could become 620-unit apartment complex

Bainbridge Companies has submitted plans to transform the vacant Nordstrom at the Mall at Wellington Green into a 620-unit multifamily apartment complex on 18 acres. The existing building would be demolished if the project is approved. Village Manager Jim Barnes said the development would include one-, two-, and three-bedroom units with shared amenities, three towers, and a parking structure. He noted the project addresses a broader housing shortage not only in the county but throughout the state and beyond.

Some residents oppose the proposal. Richard Meyers, who has lived in Wellington for more than a decade, said he opposes adding more apartments to the area, citing existing apartment complexes around the mall. Gaby Martinez raised concerns about traffic, saying added density could create bottlenecks on local roads. Barnes countered that new development could benefit remaining retailers, noting that Macy's, Dillard's, and JCPenney continue to perform well. He said the Macy's at the Mall at Wellington Green is the highest-performing store in the state. No timeline or cost estimate has been announced, and the plans have not been approved.
