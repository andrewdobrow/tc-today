Vero Beach group collecting earthquake relief supplies for Venezuela through Saturday

Latinas Nexus Network in Vero Beach is conducting a relief drive to collect essential supplies for communities devastated by a 7.5 magnitude earthquake in Venezuela. Organizers Lourana Navarro and Juliana Marino, along with Heidi Ramirez, are leading the effort to gather donations that will be shipped directly to affected areas. Navarro, who was born in Venezuela, said her own family in Caracas is safe, but surrounding communities have experienced catastrophic losses. She emphasized the scale of the disaster, noting that recovery will take decades. "Seeing the devastation — I know this is going to take decades to recover. We as a country don't have the resources," Navarro said.

The organizers are partnering with Global Empowerment Mission to ensure donated supplies reach verified humanitarian relief efforts, with 100 percent of donations going directly to those in need. Marino stressed the organization's commitment to distributing aid where it matters most. The founders estimate that hundreds of thousands of people currently face urgent needs. According to Navarro, the most critical shortages are food and shelter, as displaced residents lack places to sleep or bathe. The organizers are appealing to the entire community, not just the local Hispanic population, to contribute. "We are asking not just the Hispanic community, we are asking everyone. They really need us," the founders said.

The relief drive is accepting a wide range of items necessary for survival and basic hygiene. Needed supplies include powder Gatorade, non-perishable food, baby formula and baby food, diapers of all sizes, baby wipes, feminine hygiene products, soap, shampoo, toothbrushes and toothpaste, rain boots, toilet paper, blankets, sleeping bags with batteries, flashlights and batteries, heavy duty garbage bags, first aid kits, bandages and gauze, hand sanitizer, cleaning supplies, face masks, nitrile gloves, and plastic bins with lids.

Donations can be dropped off at 684 Old Dixie Highway in Vero Beach from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. through Saturday, June 27, 2026. Anyone interested in contributing or needing more information can contact the organizers directly: Lourana Navarro at 954-205-0700, Juliana Marino at 772-913-0805, or Heidi Ramirez at 772-213-2747.
