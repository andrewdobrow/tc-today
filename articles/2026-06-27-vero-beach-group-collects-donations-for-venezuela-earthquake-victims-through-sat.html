Vero Beach group collects donations for Venezuela earthquake victims through Saturday

Latinas Nexus Network in Vero Beach is hosting a Venezuela Earthquake Relief Drive, collecting essential supplies to ship directly to those affected by a massive earthquake. The drive is being organized by founders Lourana Navarro and Juliana Marino, along with Heidi Ramirez. Navarro, who was born in Venezuela and now lives in Vero Beach, said her family in Caracas is safe, but surrounding communities have lost everything. The earthquake measured 7.5 in magnitude.

The organizers are working with Global Empowerment Mission to ship collected supplies to Venezuela, and say 100% of donated supplies will be directed through verified humanitarian relief efforts. Marino said hundreds of thousands of people are in need right now. Navarro said the worst needs are food and places to sleep and bathe. She said the scale of destruction will take decades to address and Venezuela does not have the resources to recover.

Donations can be dropped off at 684 Old Dixie Hwy, Vero Beach. The collection runs through Saturday, June 27, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. The organizers are collecting items including powder Gatorade, non-perishable food, baby formula and food, diapers, baby wipes, feminine hygiene products, soap, shampoo, toothbrushes and toothpaste, blankets, sleeping bags, flashlights with batteries, first aid kits, bandages, hand sanitizer, cleaning supplies, face masks, nitrile gloves, and plastic bins with lids. To reach the organizers, contact Lourana Navarro at (954) 205-0700, Juliana Marino at (772) 913-0805, or Heidi Ramirez at (772) 213-2747.
