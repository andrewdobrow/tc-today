Vero Beach group launches Venezuela earthquake relief drive at Old Dixie Highway location

Latinas Nexus Network in Vero Beach is organizing a relief effort to help Venezuelan communities devastated by a recent 7.5 magnitude earthquake. The organization, founded by Lourana Navarro and Juliana Marino with assistance from Heidi Ramirez, is collecting essential supplies to send directly to affected areas. Navarro, who was born in Venezuela, said her own family in Caracas escaped injury, but neighboring communities suffered catastrophic losses. "Where it was, there is a lot of my family there, so when I started seeing the magnitude and that it was a 7.5, I really got scared," Navarro said. She emphasized the scope of the crisis, noting that recovery will require many years and resources beyond what the country currently has available.

The organizers are working with Global Empowerment Mission, a verified humanitarian organization, to ensure that collected donations reach people in need. Marino stressed the importance of getting supplies to the most vulnerable populations. "From here, the only thing that I can do is do our best to come together and help them and find different resources so we can get it directly to the people and bring the help that they need," Marino said. The founders promise that 100 percent of donated supplies will go through established relief channels rather than being diverted elsewhere. Both organizers highlighted the most pressing needs among survivors. "I can tell you that the worst is the food and the place to sleep — to take a bath — they don't have that right now," Navarro said. Marino added that hundreds of thousands of people currently need assistance.

The relief drive is accepting donations from all community members, not exclusively from the Hispanic population. "We are asking not just the Hispanic community, we are asking everyone. They really need us," the founders said. Items needed include powder Gatorade, non-perishable food, baby formula and baby food, diapers of all sizes, baby wipes, feminine hygiene products, soap, shampoo, toothbrushes and toothpaste, rain boots, toilet paper, blankets, sleeping bags with batteries, flashlights and batteries, heavy duty garbage bags, first aid kits, bandages and gauze, hand sanitizer, cleaning supplies, face masks, nitrile gloves, and plastic bins with lids.

Donations can be dropped off at 684 Old Dixie Highway in Vero Beach through Saturday, June 27, 2026, between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. Those interested in contributing can contact the organizers directly: Lourana Navarro at (954) 205-0700, Juliana Marino at (772) 913-0805, or Heidi Ramirez at (772) 213-2747.
