Martin, St. Lucie and Indian River counties face millions in losses from property tax referendum

A recent study from the Florida Association of Counties offered estimates of the potential losses. Martin County faces $50 million in losses in 2027-28 and $93 million in 2028-29. St. Lucie County faces $80 million in 2027-28 and $140 million in 2028-29. Indian River County faces $32 million in 2027-28 and $57 million in 2028-29.

Palm Beach County Commissioner Bobby Powell said county officials are working with the Office of Management and Budget to determine the full impact. Powell said if there is a cut in property taxes, there will be real shortfalls and challenges, and revenue will have to be made up somewhere.

The referendum to raise the homestead exemption to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028 will be decided by voters in November. State Rep. Toby Overdorf, R-Stuart, said the proposal is more of a break for the struggling family in a $300,000 home working several jobs to make ends meet. Republican lawmakers, including Gov. Ron DeSantis, contend local governments are taking in too much tax revenue and spending too much.
