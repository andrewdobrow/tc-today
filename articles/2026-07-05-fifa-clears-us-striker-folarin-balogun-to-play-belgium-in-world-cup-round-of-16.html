FIFA lifts Folarin Balogun suspension after Trump calls president before U.S. World Cup match

President Donald Trump called FIFA president Gianni Infantino after the United States' 2-0 round of 32 victory over Bosnia-Herzegovina on Wednesday, asking FIFA to review the red card given to striker Folarin Balogun, according to a person familiar with the call who spoke on condition of anonymity. FIFA announced Sunday that Balogun's automatic one-match suspension had been lifted for the round of 16 match against Belgium on Monday. Brazilian referee Raphael Claus initially didn't signal a card but showed Balogun red after a video review in the 64th minute for stepping awkwardly on the right ankle of Tarik Muharemović. The decision appeared to be the first time since 1962 that a red card during a World Cup did not result in a suspension. Trump posted on social media thanking FIFA for doing what was right and reversing a great injustice.

The Royal Belgian Football Association said it was astonished by the decision and is investigating all potential options to safeguard the legitimate rights of all participating teams and protect the fundamental principles of fair play. Belgium coach Rudi Garcia said through a translator that he didn't know that in FIFA offices the fifth of July was the first of April in Europe, comparing it to April Fools' Day. Balogun leads the U.S. team with three goals in the tournament, matching Landon Donovan in 2010 for the second-most goals by an American in a World Cup. The 25-year-old plays for Monaco, where he scored 13 Ligue 1 goals last season.
