Principal placed on administrative leave after Fetty Wap lyrics appear in yearbook

A school principal has been placed on administrative leave after lyrics from rapper Fetty Wap's song Trap Queen appeared in a yearbook. The rapper responded to the controversy by sending flowers to the principal.

The yearbook incident resulted in immediate administrative action against the principal. Fetty Wap learned about the situation and the consequences the principal faced for the lyrics appearing in the school publication.

The rapper sent flowers to the principal as a gesture following the administrative leave decision. Trap Queen, one of Fetty Wap's most popular songs, contains explicit content that school officials determined was inappropriate for a yearbook.

The principal remains on administrative leave while the school reviews the yearbook approval process. School officials have not announced when the principal will return or what additional steps will be taken regarding yearbook oversight.
