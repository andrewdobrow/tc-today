Breast cancer patient speaks out after Anthem Blue Cross denies reconstruction surgery hours before procedure

Beth Clark, 48, was diagnosed with invasive ductal carcinoma three months ago and underwent a full double bilateral mastectomy. Less than 24 hours before her scheduled reconstruction surgery, Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield denied coverage for the procedure. According to her denial letter, the surgery involving GalaFlex surgical mesh is considered "investigational" with "no or not enough proof it improves health." GalaFlex is a synthetic surgical mesh approved by the FDA for some uses in the body but not for breast reconstruction. In a 2023 letter to health care providers, the FDA stated its "efficacy and safety for breast cancer patients have not been determined."

Dr. Alicia Billington, Clark's plastic surgeon, uses GalaFlex mesh on most of her breast reconstruction patients and says the product gives her patients the least amount of complications compared to other options. Despite the denial, Clark received her reconstruction surgery with the GalaFlex mesh a little more than a week ago after Billington found a mechanism to make it happen. Anthem responded that it regularly reviews coverage policies to ensure they are based on medical evidence and aligned with treatment standards, but without FDA approval for breast reconstruction, the mesh does not meet their coverage standards. Clark and Billington are still fighting the denial, and a new bill has been introduced to modernize the Women's Health and Cancer Rights Act.
