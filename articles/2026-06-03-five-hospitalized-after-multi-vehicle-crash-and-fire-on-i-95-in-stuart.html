Five hospitalized after multi-vehicle crash and fire on I-95 in Stuart

Martin County Fire Rescue responded to the crash just before 10 p.m. on I-95 northbound near Exit 101 and Halpatiokee Regional Park. When crews arrived, they found one of the vehicles on fire and quickly extinguished the flames. Two people suffered traumatic injuries and were transported to the hospital, while three others sustained minor injuries and were also taken for medical treatment.

All northbound lanes of I-95 were closed Monday night while law enforcement investigated the crash. As of Tuesday morning, all lanes have reopened. The Florida Highway Patrol is investigating the circumstances of the collision.
