Vehicle fire shuts down Florida's Turnpike northbound lanes in Martin County

All northbound lanes of Florida's Turnpike in Martin County are shut down Tuesday after a vehicle caught fire and became fully engulfed in flames. The fire was reported at the 137-mile marker, about one mile south of Becker Road. All occupants safely exited the vehicle before it became engulfed, and no injuries have been reported.
