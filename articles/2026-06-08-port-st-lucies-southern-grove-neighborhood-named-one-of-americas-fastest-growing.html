St. Lucie County 2026-06-08

Port St. Lucie's Southern Grove neighborhood named one of America's fastest-growing areas

Port St. Lucie's 34987 zip code has been named one of the fastest-growing zip codes in the country by RentCafe, driven by the Southern Grove neighborhood that barely existed a decade ago. The area has experienced remarkable housing growth, with inventory expanding by 177 percent, representing one of the largest growth spurts across Florida. The neighborhood has earned recognition for its educated residents and strong homeownership rates, making it an attractive destination for those seeking to relocate.

The expansion of Southern Grove has transformed it into a major employment center. Large employers including FedEx and Amazon have established operations in the area, with Amazon demonstrating particular confidence in the neighborhood's future. The company is closing its current facility this year to construct a substantially larger facility in Southern Grove, signaling long-term commitment to the region's development.

The growth of Southern Grove was not organic but rather the result of deliberate planning. The neighborhood was designed as one of four communities specifically created to offer compelling reasons for people to move to the area and establish roots there. According to Alexandra Both, a writer for RentCafe, "Florida's high number of up-and-coming communities, second only to Texas, reflects the state's favorable tax environment and relatively lower housing costs compared to major markets like New York and California." Both noted that sustained infrastructure investment has also contributed significantly to construction activity across many of these developing suburbs.

Port St. Lucie is not alone in experiencing this growth surge within Florida. Ponte Vedra Beach earned the number one position on RentCafe's "America's 50 Newest Neighborhoods" list, noted for offering an ideal balance between work and leisure that attracts families, retirees, and young professionals. Daytona Beach and the St. Augustine area also achieved recognition within the top 50 neighborhoods, with both communities highlighted for their excellent schools and strong housing demand. This concentration of rapidly developing communities reflects broader trends reshaping Florida's landscape as more people and businesses choose to relocate to the state.
