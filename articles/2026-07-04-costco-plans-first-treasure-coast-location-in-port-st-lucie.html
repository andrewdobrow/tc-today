Costco plans first Treasure Coast location in Port St. Lucie

Costco has announced plans to open its first store on the Treasure Coast in Port St. Lucie, bringing its membership-based warehouse shopping model to St. Lucie County for the first time. The warehouse retailer operates stores that require paid memberships and offer bulk merchandise, groceries, and other products at discounted prices. This location will serve the three-county Treasure Coast region, which previously had no Costco presence.

The arrival of Costco to Port St. Lucie comes as the city undergoes significant infrastructure improvements to support growth. The city is working on a major $95 million project to widen Midway Road and add a partial interchange to Florida's Turnpike. According to Scott Samples, strategic communications manager for Port St. Lucie, these improvements are designed to address transportation challenges. "There's a pretty significant gap between the entrances and exits to the Turnpike currently in Port St. Lucie," Samples said. The interchange project aims to improve accessibility while preparing for future development over the next 20 to 50 years.

The Midway Road Widening Project is currently in its first phase, expanding the road from two lanes to four lanes between Selvitz Road and Jenkins Road. The second phase, which will widen the road from Jenkins Road to Glades Cut Off Road, remains in the design phase with construction anticipated to begin in Summer 2027. The project also includes improvements to the Turnpike bridge and a partial interchange, both still in design phases. The city council has already approved the first reading of an ordinance to grant 7 acres of land to the Florida Department of Transportation to support the interchange. Additional improvements include new bike and pedestrian paths, along with sidewalks throughout the project area.

Local residents have noticed the ongoing construction but recognize its importance. Port St. Lucie resident Erin Scheumeister acknowledged that while construction can be disruptive, the expansion is necessary for the community. "We definitely needed it to be expanded," she said. "It'll be a lot better and easier to get to work." These infrastructure investments, combined with Costco's arrival, signal Port St. Lucie's continued growth and development as a commercial hub on the Treasure Coast.
