Park Vista football team aims for state playoffs after strong 2025 finish

Park Vista Community High School's football team is building on late-season momentum as the Cobras set their sights on the state playoffs in 2026. The team struggled to find its footing early in 2025 but finished strong, winning four of its last five games. "Felt good throughout the second half of that season," senior Harris Bender said. "We're just working towards being able to carry that through the whole season, like you said, and we have all the pieces in place now, it's just about executing." The ultimate goal is the state playoffs and eventually a state title, something the program has not achieved since 2017.

Park Vista head football coach Donald Hanna emphasized a measured approach to reaching those goals. "We got to take it one game at a time," Hanna said. "We can't look past anyone. We got to handle business weekly and if we do that, do our job, that'll get us in the playoffs." Senior Erik Lee said the team has the necessary talent but must put in the work. "Coming in, I feel like we got the guys that we need to get it done," Lee said. "But also, we got to put the work in to get there as well." Senior Justice Jones expressed confidence about the upcoming season. Park Vista opens the 2026 season on the road against Atlantic in August.
