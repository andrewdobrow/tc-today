Median-income Florida households need 17 months longer to save for home down payment than a decade ago

A new study by Smart Asset found that it now takes the median-income household earner in Florida 17 months longer to save up for a down payment in 2026 than in 2016. For Floridians who earn a minimum wage of $14 an hour, it takes nearly 26 years to save up for a down payment on a home. Florida ranked No. 25 on the list.

Residents in Idaho have been affected the most in the last 10 years, now needing 40 months longer to buy a home than in 2016. Only in three states can median-income households save a down payment faster than they could in 2016: Louisiana, Mississippi and North Dakota. West Virginia has the nation's lowest down payment burden, with a median-income household needing 5.5 years to save for a down payment in 2026, just three months longer than in 2016. The study found that saving for a down payment on a minimum-wage income alone is essentially impossible in every state.
