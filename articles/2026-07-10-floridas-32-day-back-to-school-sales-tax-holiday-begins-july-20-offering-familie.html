Florida tax-free holiday saves Treasure Coast families on back-to-school shopping through Aug. 20

Families across the Treasure Coast are shopping during Florida's back-to-school tax-free holiday, which runs through Aug. 20. The exemption applies to clothing and accessories priced under $100 each, general school supplies under $50 each, educational toys and flashcards under $30 each, and electronics like calculators, printers, laptops, and tablets under $1,500 for personal use. A random school supply list for a fourth grader in Palm Beach County rings up to $263.52, saving shoppers $18.45 in taxes alone.

Katrina Stoneking was shopping at Office Depot in Boynton Beach for her soon-to-be fifth grader when she told WPTV she bought highlighters, pens, and items to match her daughter's locker. She typically spends around $200 or more each year on school supplies and hopes to save $50 to $60 during the holiday. Tameica Petgrave, general manager of the Boynton Beach Office Depot, said the store has extended hours for the tax-free period.

She has worked for the company for three years but this is her first back-to-school season at the Boynton Beach location. The most requested item from parents has been 3-ring paper. The tax discount is applied automatically at checkout on qualifying items with no coupon or special request needed.

Notable exclusions include briefcases, suitcases, watches, jewelry, skis, swim fins, rollerblades, and any clothing item over $100. The exemption is not available at theme parks, entertainment complexes, hotels, public lodging establishments, or airports. Business purchases do not qualify for the exemption.

Items ordered online during the holiday period qualify for the exemption, even if they are delivered after Aug. 20. Customers who buy an eligible item during the holiday and later exchange it for the same item in a different size or color will not be charged tax on the exchange.

The official first day of school in the Palm Beaches and the Treasure Coast is Aug. 10. Back-to-school shopping costs parents an average of $922 this year, and 47% of families are expected to pay more than last year, according to a study by accounting firm PricewaterhouseCoopers. WPTV is partnering with The Boys and Girls Club of Palm Beach County and Office Depot for a back-to-school supply drive.

Drop-off locations include Seacoast Bank locations on 5th Avenue in Delray Beach, Federal Highway in Boca Raton, and Glades Road in Boca Raton, along with Palm Beach State College Palm Beach Gardens Campus, Norton Museum of Art in West Palm Beach, Jupiter Branch Library on Military Trail, Royal Palm Beach Branch Library on Civic Center Way, Palm Beach County Public Library on Summit Boulevard, and Ed Morse Toyota and Ed Morse Cadillac in Delray Beach. The Boys and Girls Club and Office Depot will hold a drop-off event on July 23, with donations accepted through July 31.
