Multi-county hardware store theft ring hits Stuart and Fort Pierce, 3 arrested in Operation Hammer Time

The Martin County Sheriff's Office announced arrests in a retail theft operation that targeted hardware stores across the region, including locations in Stuart and Fort Pierce. Major Ruben Romero said Johnny Batista, 19, Roberto Hernandez-Castro, 39, and Roberto Aldana-Ferrera, 31 were involved in a series of burglaries at hardware stores across Martin, Palm Beach and St. Lucie counties from December 2025 to April 2026.

At least 40 stores across 13 counties were burglarized, with stolen goods taken back to Miami-Dade County where the suspects lived to be sold. Roughly $500,000 worth of property was stolen, in addition to damage to stores. According to the affidavit from Martin County Sheriff's Office, hardware stores in Stuart, Fort Pierce, Lake Worth, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, West Palm Beach and Jupiter were targeted, as were stores in Broward County.

The crews used a sledgehammer or other blunt object to smash the glass and often completed the thefts within minutes, before police arrived or alarms were triggered. Investigators say Batista is connected to the first known theft at Sewell Hardware in Stuart in December 2025. Romero said the thefts impact the entire community through higher costs. "There's already inflation that we notice severely in our state, and the more they steal, the more damage they cause, the more expensive things are. People can't go to work because they can't afford the tools."

Aldana-Ferrera is in custody in Georgia, Batista was arrested in February for a vehicle theft and is in Martin County Jail, and Hernandez-Castro has a warrant out for his arrest. A fourth suspect, Maikell Fleitas, is deceased. The suspects are facing charges of racketeering, burglary over $1,000 in damage while masked, burglary of an occupied structure and burglary of an unoccupied structure. Romero said there are burglaries that happened previous to December, and investigators are still looking for one other suspect.
