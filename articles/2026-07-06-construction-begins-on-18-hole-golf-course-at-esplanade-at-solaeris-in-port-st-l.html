Construction begins on 18-hole golf course at Esplanade at Solaeris in Port St. Lucie

Taylor Morrison has started construction on an 18-hole golf course at Esplanade at Solaeris, the company's new golf residential community in Port St. Lucie. The course is designed by Wilczynski.

The Esplanade at Solaeris development represents Taylor Morrison's entry into the golf community market in Port St. Lucie. The community will combine residential housing with golf amenities.

The Wilczynski-designed course will serve as the centerpiece of the residential development. Taylor Morrison is building the community to attract golfers and families seeking access to golf facilities.

The construction marks the first phase of the golf course development at the Port St. Lucie site. Taylor Morrison has not announced a completion date for the course.
