Port St. Lucie woman charged with attempted murder after setting fire to home with 62-year-old woman inside

A Port St. Lucie woman is in custody facing attempted murder and arson charges after allegedly setting fire to a bedroom door while a 62-year-old woman was inside the room Sunday night. Michelle Picard, 26, was arrested following the incident on Southeast Airoso Boulevard in the 1500 block.

Port St. Lucie police responded to reports of a domestic disturbance and active house fire around 9:05 p.m. Sunday. Officers arriving at the scene found flames inside the residence and immediately worked to evacuate everyone. The investigation revealed that the victim first noticed smoke coming from underneath her bedroom door and discovered Picard standing nearby when she opened it. When the victim attempted to retrieve a fire extinguisher, Picard allegedly physically attacked her and tried to prevent her from putting out the fire. Police say Picard made threatening statements and attempted to push the victim toward the flames. The victim managed to escape to a bathroom, lock the door, and call 911. During the emergency call, Picard could be heard banging on the bathroom door in the background.

Officers found Picard outside the residence and detained her. She resisted arrest, causing injuries to one officer's leg and requiring another officer to receive treatment for smoke inhalation. Both officers were treated at a local hospital and released. The victim was also hospitalized for treatment of injuries sustained during the incident. Fire investigators determined the blaze was intentionally set.

Picard faces charges of attempted murder, arson and resisting arrest without violence. Her total bond is set at $510,000. The fire damaged the residence, and the American Red Cross is assisting the victim with temporary housing and support services. The Port St. Lucie Police Department's Criminal Investigations Division, Crime Scene Investigators, St. Lucie County Fire District, and the State Fire Marshal's Office all responded to investigate. Police emphasized this was an isolated domestic incident and there is no ongoing threat to the community.
