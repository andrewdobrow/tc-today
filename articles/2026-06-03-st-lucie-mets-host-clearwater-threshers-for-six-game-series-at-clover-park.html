St. Lucie Mets host Clearwater Threshers for six-game series at Clover Park

The St. Lucie Mets are facing the Clearwater Threshers in a six-game series at Clover Park in Port St. Lucie. The matchup brings Low-A Florida State League baseball action to the Treasure Coast over multiple days this week.

The series opener scheduled for Tuesday was postponed. The teams are now set to continue the series with games running through the week at the Mets' home facility.

Clover Park serves as home to the St. Lucie Mets, the Low-A affiliate of the New York Mets organization. The stadium hosts minor league baseball throughout the season and serves as a spring training facility.

Fans can catch the remainder of the series at Clover Park as the Mets look to compete against the Threshers, the Low-A affiliate of the Philadelphia Phillies. The games give local baseball fans a chance to see developing talent in the Mets farm system.
