St. Lucie Mets and Clearwater Threshers Series Postponed at Clover Park

The St. Lucie Mets series against the Clearwater Threshers at Clover Park has been affected by a postponement, with the opener called off. The series was scheduled to run over six days at the Port St. Lucie ballpark, home of the Mets minor league affiliate.

The postponement affects fans who planned to attend games at Clover Park, a key sports venue in Port St. Lucie. The Mets are competing in their regular season schedule against the Threshers, a Florida-based minor league team.

Clover Park serves as the spring training home for the New York Mets and hosts the St. Lucie Mets during the minor league season. The facility draws baseball fans from across the Treasure Coast throughout the year for both exhibition and competitive games.

Rescheduling information for the postponed opener will determine when fans can see the Mets take the field against the Threshers. The series was originally planned to give local baseball fans multiple opportunities to watch games at the Port St. Lucie stadium over nearly a week.
