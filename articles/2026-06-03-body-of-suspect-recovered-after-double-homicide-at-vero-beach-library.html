Body of suspect recovered after double homicide at Vero Beach library

The body of a suspect who fled into the ocean after killing two people at a Vero Beach library has been recovered, according to authorities. The suspect killed his wife and another man at the library before escaping into the water.

The incident took place at a Vero Beach library, where the violent attack left two people dead. After the killings, the suspect fled the scene and entered the ocean. Law enforcement launched a search operation to locate him.

Authorities have now confirmed that the suspect's body was found. The recovery of the body brings closure to an intensive search effort that followed the deadly attack. The incident represents one of the most serious violent crimes to occur in the Vero Beach area in recent memory.

The investigation into the double homicide continues as authorities work to understand the full circumstances of the attack. The library shooting has shaken the local community, which relies on the facility as a public gathering space and resource center.
