Boca Raton Community High School band to march at National Independence Day Parade in Washington

The Boca Raton Community High School band has earned a coveted spot in Washington, D.C.'s National Independence Day Parade, representing the entire state of Florida. On July 4 at 10:30 a.m., the students will march a full mile along the National Mall, from Constitution Avenue to Independence Avenue. Only bands nominated by state officials receive invitations to this prestigious event. The parade will be streamed live on YouTube for those watching from home.

Junior Lola Cabezas acknowledged the pressure of performing for millions of people watching nationally. "There are millions, if not billions, of people watching," Cabezas said. "It's a lot of pressure, but I think we'll be able to rise to the occasion." She also explained the physical demands of marching band, noting that students must play their instruments, breathe properly, and walk while maintaining consistent sound. Senior Stephin Restivo said the team is focused on the marching and timing of everything. Band Director Jenn Girdlestone expressed confidence in her students, saying "I have full confidence in our students that they'll put their best foot forward, literally, and we'll rise to the occasion and represent our country well." Senior Lily Schaffer called the opportunity amazing and said it showcases how much the program has grown.
