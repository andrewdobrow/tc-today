Boca Raton High School Band to Represent Florida at National Independence Day Parade

The Boca Raton Community High School band has earned a coveted spot in Washington, D.C.'s National Independence Day Parade as the only band representing Florida. On July 4 at 10:30 a.m., the students will march a full mile along the National Mall, from Constitution Avenue to Independence Avenue. Only bands nominated by state officials receive invitations to this prestigious event.

Junior Lola Cabezas acknowledged the scale of the audience, saying "There are millions, if not billions, of people watching" and noting that while there is pressure, the team will rise to the occasion. She explained the physical demands of marching band, stating that students must play, breathe, and walk while maintaining consistent sound without hearing the steps in their music. Senior Stephin Restivo said the team is laser-focused on nailing every detail, emphasizing that everyone will be able to see what the Boca High band is capable of. Band Director Jenn Girdlestone said she has full confidence the students will put their best foot forward and represent the country well. Senior Lily Schaffer called the opportunity amazing and said it showcases how much the program has grown. The parade will be streamed live on YouTube.
