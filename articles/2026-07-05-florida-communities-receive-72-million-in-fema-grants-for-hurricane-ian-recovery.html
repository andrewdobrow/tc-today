FEMA awards $7.2 million to four Florida municipalities for Hurricane Ian infrastructure projects

FloridaCommerce is distributing $7.2 million in Hurricane Ian Hazard Mitigation Grant Match Program funds to four municipalities four years after the Category 4 storm hit Florida in September 2022. The FEMA grants fulfill local match requirements for infrastructure repairs and recovery while improving emergency preparedness and reducing the impact of future storms. Secretary of Commerce J. Alex Kelly said Florida communities are receiving the support needed to bolster critical infrastructure and storm readiness.

Charlotte County will receive $5.83 million for a public works hurricane-safe room generator and $93,392 for a leachate waste plant generator project. The Port of the Islands Community Improvement District has $750,840 for a Union Road water storage and infrastructure retrofit project. Seminole County will receive $319,437 for a mitigation reconstruction project on Shad Lane, and the city of Palatka will get $283,996 for a lift station generator. The Ian HMGMP program has already distributed more than $12 million in grants to municipalities. FEMA requires local governments to match the same amount of local funding to complete the projects.
