Florida communities receive $7.2 million in FEMA grants for Hurricane Ian recovery projects

FloridaCommerce announced $7.2 million in grants through the Hurricane Ian Hazard Mitigation Grant Match Program as part of FEMA local match requirements for infrastructure repairs and recovery. The funds are designed to provide infrastructure recovery, improve emergency preparedness and reduce the impact of future storms. Charlotte County will receive $5.83 million for a public works hurricane-safe room generator and $93,392 for a leachate waste plant generator project. The Port of the Islands Community Improvement District will receive $750,840 for a Union Road water storage and infrastructure retrofit project.

Seminole County has $319,437 earmarked for a mitigation reconstruction project on Shad Lane, while the city of Palatka will receive $283,996 for a lift station generator. Secretary of Commerce J. Alex Kelly stated that Florida communities are getting the support they need to bolster critical infrastructure and storm-readiness. Hurricane Ian made landfall near Fort Myers on September 22, 2022, as a Category 4 storm and plowed through the peninsula. The Ian HMGMP program has already distributed more than $12 million in grants to municipalities. FEMA funding is provided on the condition that local governments will match the same amount of local money to complete the projects.
