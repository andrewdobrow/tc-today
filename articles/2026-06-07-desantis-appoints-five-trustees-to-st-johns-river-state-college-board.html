DeSantis appoints five trustees to St. Johns River State College board

Governor Ron DeSantis has announced appointments and reappointments to the St. Johns River State College District Board of Trustees. Alexandria Hill and Kevin Payne are newly appointed to the board, while Richard Komando, Willard "Judd" Sapp Jr., and Tammy "Chereese" Stewart have been reappointed. The Florida Senate must still confirm these appointments. St. Johns River State College operates three campuses across Orange Park, Palatka, and St. Augustine, serving approximately 8,000 students combined.

Hill maintains an active presence in the Clay County community as a member of the Clay County Development Authority. She is a partner at the Driver, McAfee, Hawthorne and Dibenow law firm. Hill earned a bachelor's degree in finance and marketing from Stetson University before obtaining her law degree from the University of Florida. Payne serves as Senior Manager of Contracting for Imagine 360 health insurance company and holds the position of President of the Jacksonville Association of Health Underwriters. He completed a bachelor's degree in accounting at the University of Texas and earned a master's degree in business administration from Oklahoma City University.

Komando brings substantial legal and judicial experience to the board as a partner in the Bradley, Garrison and Komando law firm. He has served as Circuit Director and Statewide General Counsel for the Fourth Judicial Circuit Guardian ad Litem Office for Northeast Florida and previously worked as an Assistant State Attorney in that circuit. Komando holds a bachelor's degree in criminal justice and a master's degree in business administration from Florida State University, along with a law degree from Loyola University. Sapp is involved in railroad contracting through Sapp Son Inc., based in Baldwin, and is a Navy veteran who previously served as a member of the St. Johns County Day School Board of Trustees. He obtained his bachelor's degree from Florida State University.

Stewart currently serves as Assistant County Manager for the Clay County Commission, a position she previously held as an elected member. She is active in multiple community organizations including the Clay County Development Authority, the Clay County Cattlemen Association, the Clay County Farm Bureau, and the Florida Planning and Zoning Association. Stewart earned her bachelor's degree from Texas A&M University and completed a master's degree in educational leadership and administration from the University of North Florida. All five appointees bring experience in law, business, public service, or community leadership to the college board.
