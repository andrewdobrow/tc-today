Jupiter Island erosion threatens to split island at Peck Lake, official warns

A Jupiter Island official says the town is reapplying a Band-Aid as severe erosion threatens to split the island. The 2025 Florida Department of Environmental Protection report found Jupiter Island's 11.5-mile shoreline has eroded so severely the ocean could break through at Peck Lake.

The warning comes from a town official who characterized the response as inadequate given the scale of the threat. The environmental report documented the extent of shoreline degradation along the entire length of Jupiter Island.

Peck Lake has been identified as the most vulnerable point where the ocean could breach the island. The erosion has progressed to a critical stage according to the state environmental assessment completed last year.
