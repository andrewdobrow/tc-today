18th Annual Treasure Coast Seafood Festival Returns to Vero Beach This November

The 18th Annual Treasure Coast Seafood Festival will return to Vero Beach this November, according to Under The Sun Promotions, Inc.

The festival has become a staple event in Indian River County, drawing seafood lovers from across the Treasure Coast each year. Under The Sun Promotions organizes the annual event.

The festival typically features fresh seafood, live entertainment, and family activities at a Vero Beach waterfront location. Organizers announced the festival's return for November 2026.

Under The Sun Promotions has produced the Treasure Coast Seafood Festival for 18 consecutive years, making it one of the region's longest-running culinary events.
