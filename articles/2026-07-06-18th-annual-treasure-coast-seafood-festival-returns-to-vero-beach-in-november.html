18th Annual Treasure Coast Seafood Festival Returns to Vero Beach in November

The 18th Annual Treasure Coast Seafood Festival will return to Vero Beach this November, according to Under The Sun Promotions, Inc. The festival is scheduled to take place in Vero Beach, bringing seafood vendors and entertainment to Indian River County for the 18th consecutive year.

Under The Sun Promotions, Inc. announced the festival return on Monday. The event has become an annual tradition for the Treasure Coast region, drawing seafood lovers from across the area to sample fresh catches and local cuisine.

The festival will feature seafood from local and regional vendors. Specific dates for the November event and vendor lineup have been announced by the organizers.

Festival organizers typically host the event at Riverside Park in Vero Beach. Additional details about tickets, entertainment, and participating restaurants are expected to be released as the November dates approach.
