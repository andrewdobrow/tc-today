Pulte Homes targets St. Lucie County buyers with new development plans

Pulte is targeting South Florida bargain hunters with homes in St. Lucie County, according to a new report. The national homebuilder is focusing on the county as part of its strategy to attract buyers looking for more affordable housing options in the region.

St. Lucie County has become an increasingly attractive market for homebuilders as buyers seek alternatives to higher-priced coastal markets in South Florida. The county offers a combination of affordability and proximity to major employment centers, making it appealing to both first-time buyers and those relocating from more expensive areas.

Pulte has been expanding its presence in Florida markets in recent years, with St. Lucie County representing a key growth area. The company's focus on the local market signals continued residential development across the county, which has seen steady population growth over the past decade.

The development plans come as St. Lucie County continues to balance growth with infrastructure needs. Local officials have been working to ensure new residential construction is supported by adequate roads, schools, and utilities as the county's population expands.
