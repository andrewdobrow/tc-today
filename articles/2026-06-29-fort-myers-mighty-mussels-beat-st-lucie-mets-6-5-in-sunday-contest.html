Fort Myers Mussels defeat St. Lucie Mets in series finale as Fragoza hits two home runs

The Fort Myers Mussels secured a victory over the St. Lucie Mets in their series finale, with Fragoza providing the offensive spark by hitting two home runs in the game. The performance highlighted the Mussels' ability to generate runs at a critical moment in the series.

Fragoza's two-homer display proved to be the difference maker in the contest, as his power hitting drove in key runs for the Mussels. The series between these two Florida State League competitors concluded with this weekend matchup, capping off what had been an extended series due to scheduling adjustments earlier in the season.

The St. Lucie Mets, the Single-A affiliate of the New York Mets, play their home games at Clover Park in Port St. Lucie. The team had faced the Mussels, who are based in Fort Myers, in this series that required rescheduling due to a postponement that had pushed one contest back to late July. Despite the scheduling complications, both teams were able to complete their series competition.

The Mussels' offense took advantage of their opportunities against the Mets pitching staff in the finale, with Fragoza leading the charge. His two home runs gave Fort Myers the edge they needed to secure the series-ending victory and demonstrate their competitive standing within the league.
