Port St. Lucie to gain professional soccer team in USL League One expansion

Port St. Lucie will soon have a professional soccer team as the city joins Fort Lauderdale in welcoming new franchises to USL League One. The expansion brings professional soccer to the Treasure Coast for the first time, offering local fans a chance to watch competitive league matches without traveling to South Florida or Orlando.

USL League One operates as the third tier of American professional soccer, sitting below Major League Soccer and the USL Championship. The league features teams from across the country competing in a regular season followed by playoffs. The addition of Port St. Lucie continues Florida's growing presence in professional soccer, with teams already established in Tampa, Miami, Fort Lauderdale, and other cities.

The Port St. Lucie franchise will provide new entertainment options for residents of St. Lucie County and surrounding Treasure Coast communities. Professional soccer teams typically draw families and younger fans, creating a community gathering point during the season. The team will also offer youth soccer programs and community outreach as part of its operations.

Further information about the team name, home stadium location, ownership group, and inaugural season timeline has not yet been announced. Port St. Lucie has several sports facilities that could potentially host matches, including the New York Mets spring training complex at Clover Park. The Fort Lauderdale team is being added as part of the same expansion announcement.
