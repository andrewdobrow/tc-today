Byron Donalds surpasses $90 million in fundraising for Florida governor's race

Republican gubernatorial candidate Byron Donalds has raised more than $90 million for his campaign, solidifying his position as the frontrunner in the GOP primary race. The Naples congressman announced the fundraising totals this week, which include $10 million in his official campaign account and $80 million raised through the Friends of Byron Donalds political committee. The money comes from approximately 37,000 donors. In the most recent three-month period alone, Donalds brought in $23 million. Ryan Smith, the campaign's chief strategist, emphasized Donalds' competitive advantages in the race. "While other candidates run on fumes, Byron Donalds is running full steam ahead," Smith said. "Byron is the clear conservative choice to lead Republicans into the General Election and crush the Democrats and their radical, unaffordable agenda."

The announcement of Donalds' fundraising success coincided with new endorsements from major Florida political figures. Incoming Florida Senate President Jim Boyd and Florida House Speaker-designate Sam Garrison both endorsed Donalds on the same day the fundraising numbers were released. Attorney General James Uthmeier, a former chief of staff to Governor Ron DeSantis, also announced his support for the candidate. Additionally, Donalds has secured an endorsement from President Donald Trump, who encouraged the congressman to enter the race. With Governor Ron DeSantis unable to seek reelection due to term limits, the governorship is open for the first time in eight years.

Donalds faces ten other GOP primary opponents seeking the Republican nomination, including Lieutenant Governor Jay Collins, investment manager James Fishback, and former Florida House Speaker Paul Renner. Despite criticism from rivals for declining to participate in planned primary debates, Donalds continues to maintain a substantial lead in polling. His campaign has begun an aggressive spending strategy, announcing a $20 million statewide advertising purchase across television and digital platforms. The campaign plans to launch two new television ads that will reach voters throughout Florida.

Beyond advertising, the Donalds campaign has emphasized grassroots organizing efforts. The campaign has already knocked on more than 180,000 doors across the state as it works to mobilize voters. The GOP primary election is scheduled for August 18 and will determine which Republican nominee advances to the general election in November.
