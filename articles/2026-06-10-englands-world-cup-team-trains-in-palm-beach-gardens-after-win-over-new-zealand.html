England World Cup team trains in Palm Beach Gardens after 1-0 win over New Zealand

England's national soccer team trained at Gardens District Park in Palm Beach Gardens following a 1-0 victory over New Zealand in a pre-World Cup friendly held in Tampa over the weekend. Harry Kane scored the winning goal with a header. Both teams are set to compete in the World Cup tournament.

Manager Thomas Tuchel discussed the team's preparation and training approach during their time in South Florida. "We expect to push tomorrow physically and from intensity and style of play, ball speed and everything. We want to take the next step," Tuchel said. He highlighted the quality of the training facilities available in Palm Beach Gardens as an important asset for the team's preparation.

Defender Marc Guehi expressed his satisfaction with both the accommodations and the training venue. "Great, fantastic. Everyone is super friendly and welcoming to us, felt at home straight away here, and in West Palm Beach. The quality of the pitch, really good so far," Guehi said. However, he acknowledged the difficulty of adjusting to Florida's summer heat during their first days of training. "First day was extremely hot. We've been doing better getting used to the weather, getting used to the conditions, definitely a big step for us," Guehi said.

Andrew Keller, Palm Beach Gardens Director of Agronomy, explained the groundskeeping practices used before England's training sessions. The fields were watered just before practice to improve playing conditions. "They get a little bit of moisture on the leaf blade and it helps the ball skip across the field rather than the ball catching on the grass and rolling. It helps them play faster so they can replicate what happens in the stadium. Each stadium waters before a game," Keller said. England is scheduled to depart South Florida this weekend to establish their base camp in Kansas City. The team's first World Cup match is scheduled for next Wednesday against Croatia. Before leaving the region, England has another warm-up match planned in Orlando. Following England's departure, Portugal's national team will use the same training fields in Palm Beach Gardens for their World Cup preparations.
