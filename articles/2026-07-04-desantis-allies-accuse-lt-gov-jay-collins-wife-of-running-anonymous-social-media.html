DeSantis allies accuse Lieutenant Governor's wife of running anonymous accounts attacking Governor

Two prominent members of Ron DeSantis' communications team have publicly alleged that Layla Collins, wife of Lieutenant Governor Jay Collins, operated anonymous social media accounts to promote her husband's gubernatorial campaign while attacking the Governor. Christina Pushaw, a Communications Specialist in the Governor's Office, and Jeremy Redfern, Deputy Chief of Staff to Attorney General James Uthmeier, both presented evidence linking the accounts to the Second Lady.

Pushaw shared information from Promota CEO Robert Salvador indicating that the account @FSUdude01670 was connected to a phone number and email address matching ones known to be associated with Layla Collins. Florida Politics obtained screenshots of two-factor authentication messages sent to an email address with letters concealed and a phone number showing only the final digits. Pushaw identified a second account, @bobnamy1, which she also said appeared tied to Layla Collins. According to Pushaw's posts, the account's content focused entirely on promoting Jay Collins and attacking individuals who did not endorse him, including the Governor. Pushaw noted that someone serving on an important state board, as Layla Collins does on the Board of Education, would typically lack time to maintain burner accounts so actively.

Redfern corroborated the findings, stating that the FSUDude account was tied directly to Layla Collins' personal cell phone number. Both accounts were deactivated shortly after Pushaw and Redfern made their accusations public, suggesting the individuals behind them were aware of the exposure. The sudden removal of the accounts came after the criticism appeared online.

The controversy creates a significant challenge for the final six months of DeSantis' gubernatorial term. DeSantis appointed Jay Collins as Lieutenant Governor last year and appointed Layla Collins to the state Board of Education. Jay Collins is competing in the Republican primary for Governor against U.S. Representative Byron Donalds, who has received the endorsement of Attorney General Uthmeier. As of late June, Collins had raised less than 3.5 million dollars, considerably trailing Donalds' fundraising efforts. Both Pushaw and Redfern previously served as Press Secretary for DeSantis before their current positions. Florida Politics has requested comment from both Jay and Layla Collins.
