DeSantis allies accuse Lieutenant Governor's wife of running anonymous accounts attacking Governor

Multiple X accounts were deactivated after allegations surfaced that they were tied to Layla Collins, wife of Lt. Gov. Jay Collins. Christina Pushaw, a Communications Specialist in the Governor's Office, and Jeremy Redfern, Deputy Chief of Staff to Attorney General James Uthmeier, both pointed to online research tying the accounts to the Second Lady. Pushaw shared information posted by Promota CEO Robert Salvador showing the account @FSUdude01670 appeared connected to a phone number and email similar to ones known to be used by Layla Collins. Florida Politics obtained screenshots verifying this through two-factor authentication, which showed an access attempt triggered a message to an email address with letters starred out and to a phone number with only the last digits showing. Pushaw wrote that Layla Collins was appointed to a state board by the same governor she was calling a coward. Pushaw also identified a second account, @bobnamy1, which she said appeared tied to Layla Collins as well.

Pushaw said the account content was entirely comprised of two types of posts: promoting Jay Collins and attacking people who didn't endorse Jay, including the governor. Redfern posted that the FSUDude account was tied directly to her personal cell phone number. Both accounts were deactivated after Pushaw and Redfern leveled their criticisms publicly. Florida Politics requested comment from Jay and Layla Collins. Pushaw and Redfern have both previously served as Press Secretary for DeSantis. Redfern now works for Uthmeier, who has endorsed U.S. Rep. Byron Donalds for Governor. DeSantis appointed Jay Collins as Lieutenant Governor last year.
