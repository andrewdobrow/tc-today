DeSantis allies accuse Lieutenant Governor Jay Collins' wife of operating anonymous social media accounts attacking Governor

Multiple social media accounts were shut down after prominent members of Governor Ron DeSantis' communications team claimed that Layla Collins, wife of Lieutenant Governor Jay Collins, had been operating them to attack the Governor and promote her husband's campaign for the Republican gubernatorial nomination. Christina Pushaw, a Communications Specialist in the Governor's Office, and Jeremy Redfern, Deputy Chief of Staff to Attorney General James Uthmeier, both publicly presented evidence linking the accounts to the Second Lady.

Robert Salvador, CEO of Promota, conducted research showing that the account @FSUdude01670 appeared connected to a phone number and email address similar to ones associated with Layla Collins. Florida Politics obtained screenshots through two-factor authentication documentation, which displayed an access attempt that triggered messages to a partially concealed email address and phone number showing only the final digits. Pushaw identified a second account, @bobnamy1, which she also claimed was tied to Layla Collins. According to Pushaw, the content of these accounts focused exclusively on two activities: supporting Jay Collins and attacking individuals who refused to endorse him, including Governor DeSantis.

Redfern corroborated these allegations, confirming that the FSUDude account connected directly to Layla Collins' personal cell phone number. Both accounts were deactivated shortly after Pushaw and Redfern made their accusations public. Pushaw questioned the appropriateness of a state board member, appointed by the same Governor being criticized in the posts, maintaining such accounts. Layla Collins serves on the state Board of Education, having been appointed by DeSantis. Florida Politics requested comment from both Jay and Layla Collins.

Both Pushaw and Redfern have prior experience in DeSantis' communications operation, having previously served as Press Secretary to the Governor. Redfern currently works for Uthmeier, who has endorsed U.S. Representative Byron Donalds for Governor. DeSantis appointed Jay Collins as Lieutenant Governor last year. Collins is now competing for the Republican gubernatorial nomination alongside Donalds, former Florida House Speaker Paul Renner, and investment manager James Fishback. The timing of these accusations coincides with Collins' active campaign efforts within the Republican primary race.
