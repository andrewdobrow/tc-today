DeSantis Allies Accuse Lieutenant Governor's Wife of Running Anonymous Attack Accounts

Christina Pushaw, a communications specialist in the Governor's Office, and Jeremy Redfern, Deputy Chief of Staff to Attorney General James Uthmeier, accused Layla Collins of operating anonymous social media accounts attacking Governor Ron DeSantis. Pushaw shared information from Promota CEO Robert Salvador showing that the account @FSUdude01670 appeared connected to a phone number similar to one known to be associated with Layla Collins. Florida Politics obtained screenshots verifying the connection through two-factor authentication, which displayed an attempt to access the account that triggered a message to an email address with letters starred out and a phone number showing only the last digits. Pushaw also identified a second account, @bobnamy1, that appeared tied to Layla Collins. She noted the account content consisted entirely of two types of posts: promoting Jay Collins and attacking people who did not endorse him, including the governor. Redfern stated that the FSUDude account was tied directly to her personal cell phone number.

Both accounts were deactivated after Pushaw and Redfern publicly leveled their criticisms. Both previously served as Press Secretaries for DeSantis. Redfern now works for Uthmeier, who has endorsed U.S. Representative Byron Donalds for Governor. DeSantis appointed Jay Collins as Lieutenant Governor last year but has declined to endorse a preferred successor in the Governor's race. DeSantis cannot seek a third term because of term limits. Florida Politics requested comments from Jay and Layla Collins.
