DeSantis allies accuse Lieutenant Governor's wife of running anonymous accounts attacking Governor

Multiple X accounts were suddenly deactivated after allegations emerged that they were tied to Layla Collins, wife of Lieutenant Governor Jay Collins. Christina Pushaw, a communications specialist in the Governor's Office, and Jeremy Redfern, Deputy Chief of Staff to Attorney General James Uthmeier, both identified online evidence connecting the accounts to the Second Lady. Pushaw shared information from Promota CEO Robert Salvador showing the account @FSUdude01670 appeared connected to a phone number and email used by Layla Collins. Florida Politics obtained screenshots verifying this through two-factor authentication, triggered when someone attempts to access an account without the preset password. The screenshots showed an attempt to access the account triggered a message to an email address with letters starred out and to a phone number with only the last digits showing. Pushaw also identified a second account, @bobnamy1, that she said appeared tied to Layla Collins.

Pushaw said the account content suggested it existed entirely to advance Jay Collins' political ambitions in the Governor's race. The account posted only two types of content: promoting Jay Collins and attacking people who did not endorse him, including the Governor. Redfern confirmed the allegations, stating the FSUDude account was tied directly to Layla Collins' personal cell phone number. Both accounts were deactivated after the public accusations. Florida Politics has requested comments from Jay and Layla Collins.
