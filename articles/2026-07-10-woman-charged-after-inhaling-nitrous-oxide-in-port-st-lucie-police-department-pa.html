Woman charged with inhaling nitrous oxide in parking lot of Port St. Lucie Police Department

A woman was arrested Thursday after police say she was inhaling nitrous oxide in the parking lot of the Port St. Lucie Police Department. The incident occurred at the police department facility in Port St. Lucie.

Authorities charged the woman with inhaling nitrous oxide, also known as huffing, which is illegal in Florida. The arrest took place in the parking lot of the police headquarters, according to reports.

Port St. Lucie police officers responded to the scene and took the woman into custody. The woman faces criminal charges related to the inhaling of nitrous oxide.

The arrest highlights ongoing concerns about substance abuse in St. Lucie County. Port St. Lucie Police Department has not released additional information about the case.
