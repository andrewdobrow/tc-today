Woman charged with inhaling nitrous oxide in parking lot of Port St. Lucie Police Department

A woman faces charges after police say she was inhaling nitrous oxide in the parking lot of the Port St. Lucie Police Department on Thursday. The incident occurred at the police headquarters in Port St. Lucie.

Officers discovered the woman in the parking lot with nitrous oxide, commonly known as laughing gas. The woman was taken into custody at the scene.

Port St. Lucie police charged the woman in connection with the incident. The arrest took place at the department's parking lot on Thursday.

Nitrous oxide inhalation is illegal in Florida. The woman now faces criminal charges related to the incident at the police facility.
