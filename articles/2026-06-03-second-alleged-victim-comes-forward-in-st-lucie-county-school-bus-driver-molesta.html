Serious crash on I-95 near Kanner Highway sends one person to trauma center by helicopter

A multi-vehicle collision involving an SUV and a semi-truck closed all northbound lanes of Interstate 95 near Kanner Highway in Martin County on Thursday morning. One person suffered traumatic injuries in the crash and was airlifted from the scene for emergency medical treatment.

The crash forced a complete shutdown of northbound traffic on I-95 in the area, creating significant delays for morning commuters traveling through Martin County. Emergency responders worked at the scene to clear the wreckage and assist the injured.

Kanner Highway serves as a major crossroads in Stuart, connecting to I-95 and serving as a gateway to western Martin County communities. The interstate closure would have forced drivers to seek alternate routes during the morning rush.

The condition of the person who was airlifted has not been released. The Florida Highway Patrol typically investigates major crashes on Interstate 95 to determine the cause and whether any citations will be issued.
