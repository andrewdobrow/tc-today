Treasure Coast travel writer names favorite town after 20 years of Florida visits

A travel writer who has visited Florida for more than 20 years has identified a Treasure Coast town as a personal favorite, citing its lively arts scene and uncrowded beaches. The endorsement appears in Travel + Leisure magazine.

The article highlights the town's combination of cultural offerings and beach access that sets it apart from more crowded Florida destinations. The writer emphasized the arts scene as a particular draw alongside the beaches.

The recognition comes as the Treasure Coast continues to attract visitors seeking alternatives to busier coastal areas in South Florida. The region encompasses Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River counties along Florida's Atlantic coast.

The full article detailing the writer's experiences and specific recommendations for the town appears in Travel + Leisure. The piece is part of ongoing travel coverage highlighting lesser-known Florida destinations.
