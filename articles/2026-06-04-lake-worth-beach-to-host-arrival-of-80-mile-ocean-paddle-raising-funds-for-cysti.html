80-Mile Ocean Paddle From Bimini to Lake Worth Beach Set for June 13 to Raise Cystic Fibrosis Funds

Participants will launch from Bimini at midnight on June 13 and arrive at Lake Worth Beach after 12 to 16 hours of paddling. The endurance trek benefits the Pipers Angels Foundation, which event founder Travis Suit established in honor of his daughter Piper, who was diagnosed with cystic fibrosis at age 4.

The crossing is part of a four-day schedule beginning June 10 with arrivals and a poolside social at Bimini Cove. On June 11, participants take part in a 4Ocean beach cleanup and a community buffet dinner. June 12 brings a Paddle Out Rose Ceremony to honor cystic fibrosis warriors, followed by rest and safety checks before launch night.

All paddlers must wear a U.S. Coast Guard-approved personal flotation device, waterproof whistle, and reflective gear during dark hours. GPS trackers provided by the event must be worn and active at all times, and mandatory safety meetings will be held. Paddlers must raise at least $2,000 for the cause, with crew or virtual participants having a $500 minimum. Final contributions are due July 31. Spectators are invited to Lake Worth Beach on June 13 for a daylong beach celebration marking the paddlers' arrival.
