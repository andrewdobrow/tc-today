Secretary of State Byrd moves to block Paula Stark from House District 47 ballot

Secretary of State Cord Byrd filed a response in Leon Circuit Court seeking to keep St. Cloud Republican Paula Stark off the ballot for House District 47 after she failed to properly submit Form 6, a public disclosure of financial interests. Stark's incomplete qualifying materials were timestamped less than 30 minutes before the noon deadline on June 12. Five state employees gave sworn written declarations that Stark's treasurer and partner, Joel Davis, never submitted Form 6 nor was he accidentally handed it back when he submitted the rest of the paperwork. Byrd stated that Stark and Davis knew Form 6 was a required filing and should have exercised care by questioning why it was allegedly handed back or by submitting documents before the deadline.

Election Records Division Bureau Chief Erica Ikay Reeve said Stark was one of 12 candidates who didn't properly turn in Form 6. Half of them corrected the problems and still qualified. Three weeks after the qualifying deadline passed, Stark had not submitted Form 6. Reeve noted that Stark's campaign filed a campaign finance report over one month late, reported excessive contributions, and has an incorrect address on file. The state revoked Stark's Friends of Paula political committee in November over 16,000 dollars in unpaid fines from missed campaign finance deadlines. If Stark is not on the ballot, the August Primary for House District 47 would be open to all voters to decide between Democrats Jorge Figueroa and Anthony Nieves.
