Secretary of State Cord Byrd seeks to keep Paula Stark off House District 47 ballot

Secretary of State Cord Byrd is asking courts to keep St. Cloud Republican Paula Stark off the House District 47 ballot, arguing she failed to properly file required paperwork. Stark submitted incomplete qualifying materials less than 30 minutes before the noon deadline on June 12. Five state employees gave sworn written court declarations stating that Stark's treasurer and partner, Joel Davis, never submitted Form 6, a public disclosure of financial interests, nor was he accidentally handed it back when he submitted other paperwork. Byrd contended that if Stark or Davis had questioned why the Form 6 was allegedly handed back or had not waited until the final hour, the mistake could have been discovered in time.

Three weeks after the qualifying deadline, Stark still has not submitted Form 6, according to Election Records Division Bureau Chief Erica Ikay Reeve. Stark was one of 12 candidates who did not properly turn in Form 6, but half of them corrected the problems and still qualified. Reeve also cited Stark's history of campaign finance violations, noting Davis filed reports over one month late, reported excessive contributions, and maintains an incorrect address on file. The state revoked Stark's Friends of Paula political committee in November over $16,000 in unpaid fines. If Stark is removed from the ballot, the August Primary for House District 47 would feature only Democrats Jorge Figueroa and Anthony Nieves, representing a significant advantage for Democrats in the traditionally blue district covering Osceola and Orange counties.
