Secretary of State Byrd seeks to keep GOP candidate Paula Stark off House District 47 ballot

Secretary of State Cord Byrd is asking courts to keep Republican Paula Stark off the House District 47 ballot, arguing she failed to submit required paperwork by the June 12 deadline. Stark's incomplete qualifying materials were timestamped less than 30 minutes before noon on June 12. In his court response, Byrd said Stark's treasurer and partner, Joel Davis, never submitted Form 6, a public disclosure of financial interests. Five state employees gave sworn written declarations stating Davis never submitted the form nor was it accidentally handed back when he submitted other paperwork in the final qualifying hour. Three weeks after the deadline passed, Stark still had not submitted Form 6, according to Election Records Division Bureau Chief Erica Ikay Reeve.

Reeve said Stark was one of 12 candidates who did not properly turn in Form 6. Half of those candidates corrected the problems and still qualified. Reeve also noted Stark's treasurer filed a campaign finance report over one month late, reported excessive contributions, and has an incorrect address on file. The state revoked Stark's Friends of Paula political committee in November over $16,000 in unpaid fines from missed campaign finance deadlines. Byrd said ballots must be mailed to overseas military by July 4 and vote-by-mail ballots are due out July 9-16. If Stark is removed from the ballot, the August Primary for House District 47, representing Osceola and Orange counties, would feature Democrats Jorge Figueroa and Anthony Nieves, with no other Republicans having filed.
