Florida 2026-07-04

Secretary of State Cord Byrd moves to keep St. Cloud Republican Paula Stark off ballot

Secretary of State Cord Byrd is asking Leon Circuit Court to keep St. Cloud Republican Paula Stark off the ballot, arguing she failed to submit required Form 6 paperwork before the June 12 deadline. Five state employees submitted sworn written declarations stating that Stark's treasurer and partner, Joel Davis, never submitted Form 6 nor received it back when he submitted remaining paperwork in the final 30 minutes before the noon deadline. Election Records Division Bureau Chief Erica Ikay Reeve said Stark was one of 12 candidates who did not properly turn in Form 6, a public disclosure of financial interests. Half of those candidates corrected their problems and qualified. Three weeks after the deadline passed, Stark still has not submitted the form.

Reeve also cited Stark's history of paperwork violations, including a campaign finance report filed over one month late, reported excessive contributions, and an incorrect address on file. The state revoked Stark's Friends of Paula political committee in November over $16,000 in unpaid fines from missed campaign finance deadlines. If Stark is removed from the ballot, the August Primary for House District 47 will feature Democrats Jorge Figueroa and Anthony Nieves with no other Republican candidates, though the district traditionally leans Democratic and covers Osceola and Orange counties. Byrd noted that ballots must be mailed to overseas military by July 4 and vote-by-mail ballots must be sent out July 9-16.
