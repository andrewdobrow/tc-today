Secretary of State Cord Byrd asks court to keep Paula Stark off House District 47 ballot

Secretary of State Cord Byrd has asked a Leon Circuit Court to keep Republican Paula Stark off the House District 47 ballot, disputing her account of a paperwork mix-up. Stark's qualifying materials were timestamped less than 30 minutes before the noon deadline on June 12, missing the required Form 6 disclosure of financial interests. Five state employees submitted sworn written declarations stating that Stark's Treasurer and partner, Joel Davis, never submitted the Form 6 and was not accidentally handed it back when he submitted the rest of the paperwork. Election Records Bureau Chief Erica Ikay Reeve said in a court declaration that three weeks after the qualifying deadline, Stark still had not submitted the Form 6. Byrd argued that had Stark or Davis questioned why the form was allegedly handed back, the mistake would have been discovered while Davis was at the Division.

Reeve detailed Stark's history of paperwork problems, including campaign finance reports filed over one month late, reported excessive contributions, and an incorrect address on file. The state revoked Stark's Friends of Paula political committee in November over $16,000 in unpaid fines from missed campaign finance deadlines. If Stark remains off the ballot, the August Primary for House District 47 will open to all voters choosing between Democrats Jorge Figueroa and Anthony Nieves. No other Republican filed to run in the traditionally blue seat covering parts of Osceola and Orange counties. Ballots are required to be mailed to overseas military members by July 4, with vote-by-mail ballots going out July 9-16.
