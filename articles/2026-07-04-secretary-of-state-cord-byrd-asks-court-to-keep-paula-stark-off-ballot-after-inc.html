Florida 2026-07-04

Universal primary in House District 47 underway as ballot dispute continues

Paula Stark is suing to get on the ballot for House District 47 after failing to submit required Form 6 paperwork by the June 12 qualifying deadline. Secretary of State Cord Byrd is asking the courts to keep Stark off the ballot, arguing in court filings that five state employees gave sworn declarations stating that Stark's treasurer and partner, Joel Davis, never submitted the Form 6. Byrd said that had Stark or Davis exercised care by questioning why the form was allegedly handed back, "the mistake would have been discovered" while Davis was at the Division. Three weeks after the deadline, Stark still has not submitted Form 6, according to Election Records Division Bureau Chief Erica Ikay Reeve. Reeve noted that Stark was one of 12 candidates who didn't properly submit Form 6, but half of them corrected the problems and qualified. Form 6 is a public disclosure of financial interests.

Reeve also cited Stark's history of paperwork problems, including late campaign finance reports, excessive contributions, and audits finding inconsistent fund transfers in 2022 and 2024. The state revoked Stark's Friends of Paula political committee in November over 16,000 dollars in unpaid fines from missed campaign finance deadlines. The Universal Primary Contest for House District 47 is already underway with ballots scheduled to mail to overseas military by July 4 and vote-by-mail ballots due out July 9 to 16. If Stark remains off the ballot, the August Primary would be open to all voters to decide between Democrats Jorge Figueroa and Anthony Nieves. No other Republicans filed to run in the traditionally Democratic district representing Osceola and Orange counties.
