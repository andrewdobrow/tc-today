Child killed, another injured after bicycle collides with FedEx truck in Fort Pierce

A child died and another was hospitalized after a bicycle collided with a FedEx truck Monday afternoon in Fort Pierce, according to the Florida Highway Patrol. The crash happened around 3:07 p.m. in the 600 block of East Midway Road. Two juveniles were riding a bicycle when it collided with a FedEx truck.

One child was pronounced dead at the scene. The second child was taken to a hospital in stable condition.

Officials have not released the identities or ages of the children involved in the collision. The Florida Highway Patrol has not provided additional details about what led up to the crash. The incident remains under investigation.

Neighbors who live near the scene said the area is commonly used by people riding bikes. Megan Sharma told WPTV that she sees kids going up and down the sidewalk all the time on both sides of the road. "It's tragic, it's horrible.

We see kids going up and down the sidewalk all the time. Both sides, our side and the opposite side. It's unfortunate that it happened," Sharma said. "It's really tragic that a child died."

Sharma also recalled that tragedy has struck along East Midway Road before. She said that several years ago in 2020, a young adult on a motorcycle was hit by a car in the area. "Look both ways before you cross the street.

Be weary of other vehicles, it's a dangerous, very dangerous highway," Sharma said. The Florida Highway Patrol indicated that this is a developing story and more information will be released as it becomes available.
