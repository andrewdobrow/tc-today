Saint Andrew's Scots football targets state title after 10-2 season and district championship

The Saint Andrew's Scots football program is targeting a state title in the 2026 fall season after finishing last year with a 10-2 record and a district championship. Despite the successful regular season, a second-round playoff exit has motivated the team to pursue greater postseason success. Head coach Jimmy Robertson is building the team around a veteran senior class, with most seniors having played together since middle school. "Most of these seniors are four-year seniors. I mean, if not the majority of them came from the middle school as well. So they know how things are run. They had a lot of success in their career, and hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had," Robertson said.

To elevate their performance, the Scots have scheduled more demanding opponents for the 2026 season. Senior Jack Williams said the tougher schedule is designed to help the team compete at a higher level and attract new talent. "We knew that we wanted to take our program to the next level. So we really have a challenging schedule for this year because we wanna compete," Williams said. For the Scots, success this season is measured by postseason advancement rather than regular season record alone. Senior Jack Whelche emphasized that going farther than the second round of playoffs is the primary goal, adding "that's all that matters."
