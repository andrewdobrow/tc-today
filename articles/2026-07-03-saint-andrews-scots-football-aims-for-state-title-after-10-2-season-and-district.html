Saint Andrew's Scots football eyes state title run after 10-2 season and district championship

The Saint Andrew's Scots are targeting a state title run after finishing last season with a 10-2 record and a district championship. A second-round playoff exit, however, left the team believing more was achievable. Head football coach Jimmy Robertson said most of the senior class are four-year players who came through the middle school program. "They know how things are run. They had a lot of success in their career, and hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had," Robertson said.

With a veteran senior class leading the way, Saint Andrew's is embracing a more demanding schedule for the 2026 fall season. Senior Jack Williams said the program wanted to elevate to the next level by competing against quality teams. For the Scots, measuring success comes down to one metric: advancing further in the postseason. Senior Jack Whelche emphasized that going undefeated in the regular season means nothing if the team continues losing in the second round of the playoffs. "We just gotta go farther than that, that's all that matters," Whelche said. Senior Edmyer St. Jean said the team is using summer workouts as motivation, with daily effort building toward positive results when the season begins.
