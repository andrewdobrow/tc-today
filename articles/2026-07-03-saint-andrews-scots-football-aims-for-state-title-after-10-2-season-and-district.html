Saint Andrew's Scots football targets state championship after 10-2 season and district title

The Saint Andrew's Scots football team is preparing for the 2026 fall season with their sights set on a state championship. After finishing last season with a 10-2 record and a district title, a second-round playoff exit has motivated the program to pursue deeper postseason success. Head coach Jimmy Robertson highlighted the strength of his senior class, noting that most are four-year seniors who came through the middle school program. "They know how things are run. They had a lot of success in their career, and hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had," Robertson said.

The Scots are taking a deliberate approach to improvement by scheduling more challenging opponents this fall. Senior Jack Williams explained that the demanding schedule is designed to elevate the program's competitiveness. "We knew that we wanted to take our program to the next level. So we really have a challenging schedule for this year because we wanna compete," Williams said. The team's focus has shifted from regular season success to advancing further in the playoffs. Senior Jack Whelche emphasized this priority, stating that going undefeated in the season matters less than avoiding another second-round playoff exit. Senior Edmyer St. Jean credited the summer workout program as crucial preparation, saying the daily grind builds momentum toward breaking the program's record.
