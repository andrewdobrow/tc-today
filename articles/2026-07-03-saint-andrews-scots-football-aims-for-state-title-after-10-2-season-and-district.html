Saint Andrew's Scots football sets sights on state title after 10-2 season and district championship

The Saint Andrew's Scots football team is preparing for a state title run in the 2026 fall season after finishing last year with a 10-2 record and a district championship. A second-round playoff exit left the team believing more was achievable. Head coach Jimmy Robertson highlighted the experience of his senior class, noting that most are four-year players who started at the middle school level. "Most of these seniors are four-year seniors. I mean, if not the majority of them came from the middle school as well. So they know how things are run. They had a lot of success in their career, and hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had," Robertson said.

To reach the next level, the Scots are scheduling significantly tougher opponents this fall. Senior Jack Williams explained the strategy: "We knew that we wanted to take our program to the next level. So we really have a challenging schedule for this year because we wanna compete. That's what we wanna do and we wanna take that next level, and it's by playing against really good teams that we do that and attract new people." For the team, playoff advancement matters most. Senior Jack Whelche stressed that regular season success is secondary. "A better year is just gonna be going farther in the playoffs. Obviously we wanna go undefeated in the season, but that doesn't matter if we keep losing in the second round of the playoffs," Whelche said. Senior Edmyer St. Jean said summer workouts are driving the team forward.
