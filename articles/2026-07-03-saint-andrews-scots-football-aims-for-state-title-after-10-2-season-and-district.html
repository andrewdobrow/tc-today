Saint Andrew's Scots football targets state title after 10-2 season and district championship

The Saint Andrew's Scots football team is working hard during the summer months as they prepare for a state title push in the 2026 fall season. Last year, the team finished with a 10-2 record and won the district championship, but a second-round playoff loss has motivated them to achieve even more this year. The players and coaching staff believe they can go further in the postseason than they did in their previous campaign.

Head football coach Jimmy Robertson highlighted the strength of the senior class, noting that most of them have been with the program since middle school. This experience and continuity has given the team stability and success throughout their years at Saint Andrew's. Robertson expressed optimism about the senior class's final year, saying "Most of these seniors are four-year seniors. I mean, if not the majority of them came from the middle school as well. So they know how things are run. They had a lot of success in their career, and hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had." Because of this veteran leadership, the program made the decision to schedule more difficult opponents this fall to challenge themselves and attract attention to the program.

Senior Jack Williams explained the team's reasoning for taking on tougher competition. "We knew that we wanted to take our program to the next level. So we really have a challenging schedule for this year because we wanna compete. That's what we wanna do and we wanna take that next level, and it's by playing against really good teams that we do that and attract new people." Senior Jack Whelche emphasized that advancing in the playoffs matters more than winning every regular season game. "A better year is just gonna be going farther in the playoffs. Obviously we wanna go undefeated in the season, but that doesn't matter if we keep losing in the second round of the playoffs. We just gotta go farther than that, that's all that matters."

The team is using summer workouts as preparation and motivation for the upcoming season. Senior Edmyer St. Jean reflected on how the daily training sessions provide purpose as the team works toward their goals. "It just adds up every day as something like to look forward to. Work harder so we can see the results when we come off the season positive and break our record." The Scots are committed to the demanding summer program as they aim to have their best season in program history while competing for a state championship.
