Saint Andrew's Scots Football Eyes State Title After 10-2 Season and District Championship

The Saint Andrew's Scots football team finished last season with a 10-2 record and a district championship, but a second-round playoff exit has left them hungry for more. This offseason, the team is preparing for the 2026 fall season with their sights set on a state title. Head football coach Jimmy Robertson noted that most of the seniors are four-year veterans who came up through the middle school program and have experienced success throughout their careers. "Hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had," Robertson said.

To reach that goal, the Scots are embracing a more demanding schedule this fall. Senior Jack Williams explained the reasoning behind the tougher slate of opponents: "We wanna compete and take that next level, and it's by playing against really good teams that we do that." For the team, success this season will be measured by playoff performance. Senior Jack Whelche emphasized that going undefeated in the regular season means little if the team continues losing in the second round. Senior Edmyer St. Jean said the daily work in summer training serves as motivation, adding that the hard work "adds up every day as something like to look forward to" as they aim to "see the results when we come off the season positive and break our record."
