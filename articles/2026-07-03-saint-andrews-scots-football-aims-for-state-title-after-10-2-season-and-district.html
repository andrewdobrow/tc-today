Saint Andrew's Scots football targets state title after 10-2 season

The Saint Andrew's Scots finished last season with a 10-2 record and a district title, but a second-round playoff exit has the team focused on a state title run this fall. Head football coach Jimmy Robertson said most of the seniors are four-year players, with many coming from the middle school program. "Most of these seniors are four-year seniors. I mean, if not the majority of them came from the middle school as well. So they know how things are run. They had a lot of success in their career, and hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had," Robertson said.

To compete at the next level, the Scots have scheduled more challenging opponents for the 2026 fall football season. Senior Jack Williams said playing stronger teams will help the program advance. "We knew that we wanted to take our program to the next level. So we really have a challenging schedule for this year because we wanna compete," Williams said. Senior Jack Whelche emphasized that advancing further in the playoffs is the priority. "A better year is just gonna be going farther in the playoffs. Obviously we wanna go undefeated in the season, but that doesn't matter if we keep losing in the second round of the playoffs," Whelche said. Senior Edmyer St. Jean said summer workouts are building momentum for the season ahead.
