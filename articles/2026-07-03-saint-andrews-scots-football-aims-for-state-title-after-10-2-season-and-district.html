Saint Andrew's Scots Football Targets State Title After 10-2 Season

The Saint Andrew's Scots are preparing for the 2026 fall football season with their sights set on a state title. After finishing last season with a 10-2 record and a district title, the team fell short in the second round of playoffs, leaving them feeling like more was within reach. Head football coach Jimmy Robertson said most of the seniors are four-year seniors who came up through the middle school program and know how the program operates. "Most of these seniors are four-year seniors. I mean, if not the majority of them came from the middle school as well. So they know how things are run. They had a lot of success in their career, and hopefully their senior year is the best season that we've had," Robertson said.

The Scots have scheduled more challenging opponents this fall to compete at a higher level. Senior Jack Williams explained the reasoning behind the tougher schedule, saying the program wants to take itself to the next level by playing against really good teams. For the team, improvement this season is measured by one metric: advancing further in the postseason. Senior Jack Whelche said going undefeated in the regular season does not matter if the team keeps losing in the second round of the playoffs. The team is embracing summer workouts as motivation for the upcoming season.
