Fort Pierce pastor arrested on child abuse charges after police find starvation, filth in foster home

Fort Pierce police arrested a local pastor on child abuse charges after discovering alleged starvation and unsanitary conditions inside his foster home. The investigation uncovered what authorities describe as severe neglect and abuse of children in the pastor's care.

Police responded to the Fort Pierce residence and found conditions they characterized as filthy, with evidence suggesting children were being denied adequate food and living in deplorable circumstances. The pastor operated the home as a licensed foster care facility, housing children placed in his care through the state system.

Authorities have not released the pastor's name or the exact number of children affected. The investigation revealed enough evidence for police to file formal child abuse charges against the religious leader, who allegedly violated his duty to protect and care for vulnerable children.

The case remains under active investigation as authorities work to determine the full extent of the alleged abuse and neglect. Child welfare officials are working to ensure all children formerly in the pastor's care are placed in safe environments.
