Fort Pierce pastor charged with child neglect after foster children found without food, functioning bathroom

A Fort Pierce pastor faces five counts of child neglect after authorities discovered five foster children living in severe conditions without proper food access or bathroom facilities. Gwendolyn Rolle, 59, had been caring for the children, ages 4 to 9, after their father placed them in her custody. On June 2, police responded to her home following reports of abuse.

When officers arrived, Rolle's daughter opened the door and confirmed that abuse was occurring in the home. According to the arrest affidavit, the daughter had hesitated to speak out earlier because of Rolle's standing in the community. Investigators found that the foster children were denied access to the home's working bathroom, which was kept locked. Instead, the children were forced to use a bucket for their bathroom needs and bathed outdoors using a garden hose on the front porch.

Police documented physical abuse during their investigation. One child had visible bruising on their chest, and multiple children reported being struck in the face or head during discipline. While officers were still at the home, one child said Rolle threatened them after arriving, telling the child that once police left, they would "get it." Beyond physical abuse, the children described inadequate meals. They reported frequently missing dinner or going to bed hungry. Major Michael Santiago of the Fort Pierce Police Department noted that the foster children depended on meals provided by neighbors to supplement their diet. Additionally, two of the children are non-verbal and attended a special needs school, but Rolle could not identify which school they attended or provide basic information about their education.

Following the investigation, the Department of Children and Families took custody of all five foster children. Rolle was released on bond pending trial. Major Santiago characterized the case as exhibiting unmistakable signs of neglect, describing the situation as "absolutely disgusting and tragic" for the children involved.
