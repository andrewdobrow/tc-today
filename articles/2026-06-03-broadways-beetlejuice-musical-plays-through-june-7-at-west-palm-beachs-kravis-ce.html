Beetlejuice musical runs through Saturday at Kravis Center in West Palm Beach

Beetlejuice the musical is playing at the Kravis Center in West Palm Beach through Saturday, June 7. The Broadway hit brings its spooky humor, over-the-top visuals, and heartwarming moments to the Alexander W. Dreyfoos Concert Hall. Actor Ryan Stajmiger steps into the iconic striped suit to play the mischievous demon beloved from Tim Burton's 1988 film. The musical follows Lydia Deetz, a strange and unusual teen, whose life changes after meeting two recently deceased neighbors and a demon with a flair for trouble.

Stajmiger says one of his favorite parts is laughing with the audience. He breaks the fourth wall frequently to communicate directly to the audience. Early in the run, he experienced an unexpected moment when a wig change on stage went wrong. He was wearing two wigs, and the second wig came off with the first wig, leaving him bald for an entire scene.

Performances include 7:30 p.m. shows on June 3 and June 4, 8 p.m. on June 5, matinee and evening performances on June 6, and a closing matinee on June 7. Tickets start at $34.50 and are available at kravis.org. The show is recommended for ages 13 and up, with parental discretion advised.
