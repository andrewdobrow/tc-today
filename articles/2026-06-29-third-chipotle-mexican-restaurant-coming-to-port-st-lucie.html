Third Chipotle Mexican restaurant coming to Port St. Lucie

The new location will join two existing Chipotle restaurants already operating in the city. Port St. Lucie has seen continued commercial expansion as the population grows across St. Lucie County.

Chipotle is a fast-casual Mexican restaurant chain that offers burritos, bowls, tacos, and salads. The company operates multiple locations throughout Florida.

The announcement comes as Port St. Lucie continues to attract new restaurant and retail development. The specific location and opening date for the third Chipotle have not been disclosed.
