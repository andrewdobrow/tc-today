St. Lucie County Sheriff's Office Marine Unit rescues 2 after boat sinks in Intracoastal Waterway

The St. Lucie County Sheriff's Office Marine Unit responded to reports of a small motorized boat taking on water and becoming submerged in the Intracoastal Waterway on July 1. Both occupants ended up in the water when their boat went down, but deputies say they were wearing life jackets that kept them afloat while help arrived. Body-worn camera footage captured the rescue as it unfolded.

Marine Unit deputies quickly located the pair in the water and safely brought them aboard their patrol vessel. The boaters were then transported back to the boat ramp without any injuries reported. The sheriff's office said in a Facebook post that this incident serves as an important reminder that wearing a life jacket can save your life. The sheriff's office added that they are grateful this call ended safely and proud of the swift response by their Marine Unit deputies who are always ready to answer the call.
