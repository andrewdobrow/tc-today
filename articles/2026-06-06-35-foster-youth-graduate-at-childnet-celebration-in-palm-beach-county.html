35 foster youth graduate at ChildNet celebration

ChildNet marked a significant milestone by hosting its annual graduation celebration at Palm Beach State College, where 35 students participated in the ceremony. The nonprofit organization serves foster youth and young adults across Broward and Palm Beach counties, providing support through education programs, adoption services and independent living resources designed to help young people transition to adulthood.

Among the graduates were Sabrina Diaz and Brian Saintfort, two students who have overcome substantial challenges with ChildNet's assistance. Both lost a parent during their time in the foster care system and relied on the organization to provide the resources they needed to move forward with their lives. Diaz is currently enrolled in pre-med classes at Palm Beach State College with aspirations of becoming a doctor. Saintfort has chosen a different path, planning to pursue a vocational program to become a plumber. Their diverse goals reflect the range of opportunities available to graduates supported by the organization.

During the celebration, both graduates shared messages of encouragement for other young people in similar circumstances. Diaz emphasized the importance of maintaining hope and perseverance through difficult times. "Belief that you're going to be big one day and just put effort into it, never give up on school and never give up on dreams. If you like push past with the pain and just keep trying hard," she said. Saintfort similarly stressed the power of self-belief in achieving success. "Believe that you'll make it, then you're gonna make it and stuff," he said.

ChildNet CEO Larry N. Rein used the occasion to acknowledge the collective effort that made the evening possible. He highlighted the contributions of case workers from both ChildNet and the Department of Children and Family Services, while particularly praising the graduates themselves for their hard work and determination. "This evening is the fruition of great work by everyone in the community, including case workers at ChildNet, DCF and especially the kids who have worked so hard. We are to celebrate them!" Rein said. The graduation celebration demonstrated how comprehensive support systems, combined with the resilience of young people in foster care, can help overcome obstacles and create pathways to successful futures.
