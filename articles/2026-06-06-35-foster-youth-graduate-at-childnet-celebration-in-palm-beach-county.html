35 foster youth graduate at ChildNet celebration in Palm Beach County

Thirty-five students reached a significant milestone by graduating at ChildNet's annual celebration held at Palm Beach State College. The event recognized the achievements of foster youth and young adults supported by the nonprofit organization, which operates in both Broward and Palm Beach counties. ChildNet provides comprehensive services to foster children and young adults, including educational support, adoption services, and resources for independent living.

Two graduates who stood out at the ceremony were Sabrina Diaz and Brian Saintfort. Both had experienced the loss of a parent and sought support from ChildNet during their high school years. The organization provided them with the resources and guidance they needed to overcome their challenges and move toward their futures. During the celebration, both graduates shared their experiences and offered encouragement to other young people facing similar hardships.

Diaz spoke directly to others in comparable situations, emphasizing the importance of maintaining hope and persistence. She encouraged her peers to believe in their potential for success, invest effort into their education, and never abandon their goals despite difficulties. She stressed the value of pushing through pain and continuing to work hard toward one's objectives. Saintfort delivered a similar message, highlighting the power of believing in oneself as a foundation for achievement. Both young adults demonstrated a commitment to motivating others who might be struggling with loss and uncertainty.

Larry N. Rein, CEO of ChildNet, used the occasion to acknowledge the collective effort behind the graduates' success. He recognized the contributions of case workers employed by ChildNet and the Department of Children and Families, as well as the dedication of the students themselves. Rein emphasized that the evening represented the culmination of hard work from everyone involved in supporting these young people. Looking ahead, Diaz has enrolled in pre-med classes at Palm Beach State College with aspirations to become a doctor. Saintfort is pursuing a vocational program to train as a plumber. Both are positioning themselves for stable, rewarding careers after graduation.
