35 foster youth graduate at ChildNet celebration at Palm Beach State College

Thirty-five foster youth and young adults celebrated a significant milestone as they walked across the stage at ChildNet's annual graduation event held at Palm Beach State College. The ceremony honored students served by the nonprofit organization, which operates across Broward and Palm Beach counties. ChildNet provides comprehensive support to foster children and young adults through education programs, adoption services, and independent living resources designed to help them build successful futures.

Among those recognized were Sabrina Diaz and Brian Saintfort, two graduates who each experienced the loss of a parent and sought assistance from ChildNet during difficult times. The organization provided them with essential resources and guidance that enabled them to overcome their challenges and move forward. Diaz offered encouragement to others facing similar circumstances, sharing her perspective on perseverance. "Belief that you're going to be big one day and just put effort into it, never give up on school and never give up on dreams. If you like push past with the pain and just keep trying hard," she said. Saintfort reinforced this message of determination, stating, "Believe that you'll make it, then you're gonna make it and stuff."

Larry N. Rein, CEO of ChildNet, emphasized the importance of the evening's celebration and acknowledged the contributions of everyone involved in supporting these young people. "This evening is the fruition of great work by everyone in the community, including case workers at ChildNet, DCF and especially the kids who have worked so hard. We are to celebrate them!" Rein said. His remarks highlighted the collaborative effort required to help foster youth succeed, recognizing both the dedicated professionals and the resilience of the graduates themselves.

The two featured graduates are already pursuing their professional ambitions. Diaz has enrolled in pre-med courses at Palm Beach State College as she works toward her goal of becoming a physician. Saintfort is pursuing vocational training to become a plumber. Their clear career paths and continued educational engagement demonstrate the positive outcomes that ChildNet's support has helped facilitate for young people in the foster care system.
