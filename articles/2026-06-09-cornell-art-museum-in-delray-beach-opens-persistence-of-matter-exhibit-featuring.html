Cornell Art Museum in Delray Beach Opens 'Persistence of Matter' Upcycled Art Exhibition

The Cornell Art Museum at Old School Square in Delray Beach has launched Persistence of Matter, a summer exhibition celebrating artists who repurpose discarded materials into compelling artwork. The show aligns with the museum's commitment to blending artistic creativity with meaningful community engagement, according to Marusca Gatto, Cultural Arts Director for the Delray Beach Downtown Development Authority. Guest curators Mavis Benson and Pamela Lambie assembled a diverse group of artists working across multiple mediums, all using reclaimed objects as their primary materials.

The exhibition features works ranging from sculptures to installations to mixed media pieces. Among the highlighted artists is Autumn Kioti, whose creations draw inspiration from both natural and urban environments. Another featured artist, Mike Silverman, has created the Self-Reflection series using salvaged hard drive platters and printed circuit boards to construct reflective sculptures. Silverman explained that his work encourages viewers to examine their own relationship with technology and waste in fresh ways. "Every piece invites viewers to see themselves and their relationship to technology and waste in a new light," he said.

As sustainability becomes an increasingly urgent concern for communities, Persistence of Matter serves as an artistic exploration of how discarded materials maintain value and purpose. The exhibition demonstrates how everyday waste can be transformed into beautiful and thought-provoking art, fostering important conversations about environmental responsibility through innovative creative practice. The show remains on view through September 10, 2026, and includes a range of public programming designed to deepen visitor engagement.

The museum offers several ways to participate in the exhibition beyond simply viewing the artwork. Visitors can attend artist talks, participate in salon discussions, and watch film screenings throughout the run. The Cornell Art Museum maintains regular hours Wednesday through Sunday, opening at noon on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday until 5 p.m., 7 p.m., and 7 p.m. respectively. Saturday hours run from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Sunday from noon to 5 p.m. For more information about the exhibition or special programs, interested visitors can call 561-654-2220.
