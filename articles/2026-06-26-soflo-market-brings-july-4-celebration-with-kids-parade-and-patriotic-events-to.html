SoFlo Market hosts Fourth of July celebration at Boca Raton City Hall with kids parade, local vendors

The SoFlo Market is transforming Boca Raton City Hall into a patriotic gathering space this Independence Day weekend. The popular monthly event takes place Saturday, July 4, from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. at 201 West Palmetto Park Road, drawing families, food lovers and local shopping enthusiasts from across Palm Beach County. Admission is free. Founder and organizer Tatiana Freitas told WPTV the market's core mission is to uplift small local businesses while bringing the community together. For the holiday celebration, organizers are emphasizing patriotic themes alongside their regular offerings.

The event features a robust lineup of activities designed for families. Kids will participate in a kids' parade, while a DJ keeps the energy high throughout the day. The first 20 children through the gate receive a free gift. Entertainment options include face painting, a foggy bubble station, mini golf, and balloon twisting. The market's signature handmade crafts and gourmet desserts will be available from local vendors. A live appearance by the SoFlo Market's flamboyant flamingo mascot is expected to be a crowd favorite.

Community partnerships are central to the event's vision. Boca Raton firefighters and police officers have been invited to participate, reinforcing a family-first atmosphere. Freitas works alongside her husband Eddie and son Kyle on the organizing team to ensure all activities run smoothly. On-site generators will power the event, while strong cell service allows visitors to share moments instantly on social media. The market's Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube channels will feature behind-the-scenes clips and photos throughout the day.

For Freitas, this Independence Day edition represents more than just a market. "We want to make people proud to call this place home," she said. The SoFlo Market runs monthly but the Fourth of July edition is always a standout. By combining economic support for small businesses with cultural tradition, the event becomes a statement of community pride. Independent markets like SoFlo play an increasingly vital role in local economies, offering exposure for entrepreneurs while creating spaces where neighbors can connect. The holiday celebration exemplifies this dual purpose, turning a regular market day into a patriotic community gathering that strengthens local ties and celebrates entrepreneurship.
