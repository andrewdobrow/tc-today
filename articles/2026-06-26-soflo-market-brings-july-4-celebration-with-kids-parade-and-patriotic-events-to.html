SoFlo Market brings free Independence Day celebration to Boca Raton City Hall July 4

Founder and organizer Tatiana Freitas is preparing the SoFlo Market to transform Boca Raton City Hall into a patriotic celebration hub this Fourth of July. The free event takes place Saturday, July 4, and will draw families, food lovers and local shopping enthusiasts throughout Palm Beach County. According to Freitas, the market's core mission centers on supporting small local businesses while bringing the community together. For the holiday edition, organizers are layering in extra patriotic touches that promise to make the celebration memorable.

The day's activities blend family entertainment with Independence Day spirit. Attendees can enjoy face painting, a foggy bubble station, mini golf and balloon twisting alongside the market's collection of handmade crafts and desserts. A kids' parade and DJ performances will keep the energy high, while the first 20 children through the gate receive a free gift. The SoFlo Market's flamboyant flamingo mascot will make a live appearance that organizers expect will be a crowd draw. Unique vendors and gourmet food options will round out the offerings throughout the afternoon.

Community partnerships strengthen the event's family-focused atmosphere. Boca Raton firefighters and police officers have been invited to participate and connect with residents. Kyle Freitas, Tatiana's son, and Eddie, her husband, serve as part of the organizing team coordinating the day's activities. Generators will provide on-site electricity for the event. Strong cell service will allow visitors to share their experiences instantly across social media, with behind-the-scenes coverage planned for the market's Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube channels.

The Independence Day market represents more than just shopping and entertainment. Independent markets like SoFlo play an increasingly important role in local economies by providing exposure for small businesses and creating gathering spaces where neighbors connect. By combining the market with Fourth of July traditions, organizers blend economic support with cultural celebration. Freitas emphasized the deeper purpose: "We want to make people proud to call this place home." While SoFlo Market operates monthly, Freitas notes that the Independence Day edition consistently stands out as a highlight. The event runs from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. at Boca Raton City Hall, located at 201 West Palmetto Park Road. Admission is free.
