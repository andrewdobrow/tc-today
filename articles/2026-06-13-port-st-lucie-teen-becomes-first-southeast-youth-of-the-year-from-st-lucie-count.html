Port St. Lucie teen makes history as first Southeast Youth of the Year from St. Lucie County

Enrique Rosario Liz, the valedictorian of Treasure Coast High School, has achieved a significant milestone by becoming the first person from Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Lucie County to win the Southeast Region Youth of the Year award. This recognition honors outstanding teens who demonstrate strong academics, solid values, and commitment to serving others. Enrique will travel to New York City on October 1 to compete for the national Youth of the Year title.

Enrique's path to this honor reflects remarkable personal growth. He arrived in Port St. Lucie from the Dominican Republic eight years ago without speaking English. With support from his school and the local Boys & Girls Club, he pursued opportunities to develop his skills and knowledge. Now in his senior year, he has been named both the county's Youth of the Year and Florida Youth of the Year. This fall, he will begin studies at the University of Florida. "I could never have imagined this moment when I was 8 years old," Enrique reflected after receiving the award. "It's a privilege to share my journey, and I hope it inspires belief in every young person who needs a helping hand and an opportunity."

Enrique's accomplishment has earned praise from community leaders and mentors. Will Armstead, CEO of Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Lucie County, highlighted Enrique's character as his most impressive quality. "He's turned challenges into opportunities and advocated for others—all with humility and determination," Armstead said. Travis Leonard, owner of A&G Concrete Pools and a longtime Youth of the Year judge, noted that Enrique distinguished himself throughout the selection process. "From the start, Enrique stood out. Thanks to his hard work—and all those who support him—we finally have a Southeast Region Youth of the Year," Leonard said. "I believe he's ready for the national competition, and I'm proud of him and our whole community."

Enrique's family has expressed deep gratitude for the opportunities he has received. His parents credited their faith and the support of Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Lucie County in helping their son develop as both a leader and a person. The Youth of the Year program represents Boys & Girls Clubs of America's premier recognition for teenagers who serve as role models and change-makers in their communities. As Enrique prepares to compete on the national stage, his journey from newcomer to valedictorian continues to inspire young people throughout St. Lucie County.
