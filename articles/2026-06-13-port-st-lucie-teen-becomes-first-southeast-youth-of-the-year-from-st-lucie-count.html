Port St. Lucie teen becomes first from St. Lucie County named Southeast Youth of the Year

Enrique Rosario Liz has achieved a historic milestone as the first member of Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Lucie County to receive the Southeast Region Youth of the Year award. The Port St. Lucie High School valedictorian will now travel to New York City on October 1 to compete for the national title. The Southeast Region Youth of the Year award honors outstanding teenagers who demonstrate strong academics, solid values, and a commitment to serving their communities.

Enrique's path to this recognition spans eight years. He arrived in the United States from the Dominican Republic as an 8-year-old who did not speak English. With support from his school and the local Boys & Girls Club, he transformed himself into a capable student and leader. Reflecting on his achievement, Enrique said, "I could never have imagined this moment when I was 8 years old. It's a privilege to share my journey, and I hope it inspires belief in every young person who needs a helping hand and an opportunity." His rise from newcomer to valedictorian demonstrates his determination and work ethic. This fall, he will enroll at the University of Florida.

Will Armstead, CEO of Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Lucie County, highlighted Enrique's character and resilience. "Enrique's achievement is historic, but his character impresses me most. He's turned challenges into opportunities and advocated for others, all with humility and determination," Armstead said. Travis Leonard, owner of A&G Concrete Pools and a longtime Youth of the Year judge, also praised the teenager's performance. "From the start, Enrique stood out. Thanks to his hard work and all those who support him, we finally have a Southeast Region Youth of the Year. I believe he's ready for the national competition, and I'm proud of him and our whole community," Leonard said.

Enrique's parents expressed gratitude for the opportunities their son received. "We're grateful to God for every opportunity and to Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Lucie County for helping Enrique grow as a leader and a person," they said. The Youth of the Year program represents Boys & Girls Clubs of America's highest recognition for teenagers who serve as role models and change-makers in their communities. As Enrique prepares for the national stage, his accomplishments continue to inspire young people throughout St. Lucie County.
