George Snow Scholarship Fund awards record $7.5 million to 470 Palm Beach, Broward students

The George Snow Scholarship Fund has announced a major achievement, awarding more than $7.5 million in scholarships and support services to 470 students across Palm Beach and Broward counties in 2026. According to President Tim Snow, who spoke with WPTV, this record-breaking distribution reflects the nonprofit's continued expansion of financial and educational support for students in South Florida.

Since its founding over 40 years ago, GSSF has distributed more than $45 million to over 4,100 students, establishing itself as the largest scholarship provider in Palm Beach County. Snow explained that the program bridges the gap between traditional financial aid and what students and families can actually afford to pay. The nonprofit's approach includes four-year scholarship commitments that provide stability and predictability as students work through their college education. Beyond monetary assistance, Vice President of Development Jay Brandt noted that GSSF offers mentorship, professional development opportunities, and educational resources including the College Compass podcast to help scholars succeed both academically and professionally.

One recipient benefiting from the fund's support is Zya Chow, a Wellington Community High School graduate heading to Georgetown University. Chow earned both the Royal Palm Hearing Aid Scholarship and the Davidowitz Family Caregiving Youth Scholarship. Her selection highlights how GSSF's diverse funding sources allow the organization to support students with varying backgrounds and circumstances.

The scholarship fund has also expanded its services beyond financial aid. The Doyle-Whelchel Scholars Boutique, led by Scholar Services Coordinator Garvey Antoine, provides professional, formal, and casual clothing to help students prepare for interviews, internships, and college life. The boutique accepts donations of new or gently used clothing, footwear, and accessories from the community. This comprehensive approach to student support reflects GSSF's understanding that removing barriers to success involves more than tuition assistance. As higher education costs continue to rise across the nation, organizations like the George Snow Scholarship Fund play an increasingly important role in making college accessible to underserved students in South Florida.
