Indian River County 2026-06-12

Indian River County rental costs reach $1,222 for one-bedroom apartments according to federal housing data

Fair Market Rent for one-bedroom apartments in Indian River County has reached $1,222 according to 2026 data from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. The county's rental market sits in the middle range across South Florida, with Belle Glade offering the region's most affordable options at $890 and Wellington in Palm Beach County commanding the highest rates at $2,850 for comparable housing.

HUD's Fair Market Rent figures are calculated using surveys, rental listings and market adjustments to reflect current rates for similar properties in each area. These benchmarks serve an important function in the housing assistance landscape, helping to determine eligibility and payment levels for programs that support lower-income renters. However, the data does not establish rent control limits for landlords or restrict what they can charge tenants.

The substantial variation in rental costs across South Florida reveals significant affordability challenges facing the region. Real estate broker Talbot Sutter, president of Sutter and Nugent Real Estate, attributes high costs in desirable areas to multiple factors. "A lot of it has to do with school districts, especially Wellington. They have like top A-rated schools, lifestyle—they have so many aspects from equestrian to outdoor activities," Sutter said. He notes that demand for properties with more acreage and land has increased across the rental market. For those searching for housing, Sutter recommends thorough research: "Know the market, do your comps. You have to really, really know the area if you don't want to get ripped off paying rent."

The rental market continues to create hardship for many South Florida residents balancing stagnant income growth against rising living costs. One Section 8 housing resident described the situation plainly: "It's not easy here. You have to budget—there's no way out of it." As renters, policymakers and housing advocates work to address the region's growing affordability gaps, understanding current market rates provides essential context for these ongoing discussions. The HUD data captures a snapshot of how dramatically rental costs vary even within the same county, underscoring the importance of location when searching for housing in South Florida.
