Palm Beach County Library launches summer reading challenge through July 31

The Palm Beach County Library System is running its 2026 Summer Reading Challenge, themed "Unearth a Story," through July 31. According to Community Engagement Manager Lindsey McMullen, the program provides free activities, incentives and grand prizes designed to help residents reconnect with reading. Anyone can participate by registering at any library branch, completing an activity log and returning it to claim a prize while supplies last. Those who submit their logs also enter an automatic drawing for one of several grand prizes.

The program offers age-appropriate rewards for different groups. Children up to age 4 receive a rubber duck, while kids ages 5 to 11 can choose between a fidget spinner, dinosaur-themed pop keychain or dino pencil. Teenagers ages 12 to 17 and adults have the option of selecting a fidget spinner, chip clip or notebook. All prizes are distributed on a while supplies last basis.

Beyond the core reading challenge, each library branch is hosting special events throughout the summer. These include children's story times and craft sessions alongside adult workshops and wellness programs. Families will also have opportunities to meet Dewey the Library Owl and Penelope Rex, a dinosaur character created for the challenge, at various branch locations and special appearances.

Summer reading initiatives like this one serve an important educational purpose. According to the American Library Association, these programs help prevent the "summer slide," which refers to the decline in student learning that can occur during extended school breaks. Activity logs can be picked up and submitted at any library location. The challenge welcomes all county residents and library cardholders to participate.
