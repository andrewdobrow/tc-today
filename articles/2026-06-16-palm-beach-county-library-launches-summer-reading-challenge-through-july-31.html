Palm Beach County Library launches summer reading challenge through July 31

The Palm Beach County Library System is encouraging readers of all ages to participate in its 2026 Summer Reading Challenge, themed "Unearth a Story," running through July 31. According to Community Engagement Manager Lindsey McMullen, the program provides free activities, incentives and grand prizes designed to inspire residents to reconnect with reading. To participate, people can register at any library branch, complete an activity log and return it to receive a prize while supplies last. Those who submit their logs also enter an automatic drawing for one of several grand prizes.

The library has created age-appropriate reward options for different groups. Children up to age 4 receive a rubber duck, while kids ages 5 to 11 can choose between a fidget spinner, dinosaur-themed pop keychain or dinosaur pencil. Teens ages 12 to 17 and adults have the option of selecting a fidget spinner, chip clip or notebook. Activity logs can be picked up and returned at any branch location.

Beyond the reading challenge itself, each library branch is hosting special events throughout the summer. These activities range from children's story times and craft sessions to adult workshops and wellness programs. The library is also bringing characters to various locations, including Dewey the Library Owl and Penelope Rex, a dinosaur character created specifically for this summer's challenge.

The summer reading initiative aligns with educational research showing that such programs help prevent the summer slide, the learning loss that students can experience during extended breaks from school, according to the American Library Association. The challenge welcomes all county residents and library cardholders. For more information about this summer's reading program or to learn about the special events happening at your local branch, residents can visit their nearest Palm Beach County Library location or contact the library system directly.
