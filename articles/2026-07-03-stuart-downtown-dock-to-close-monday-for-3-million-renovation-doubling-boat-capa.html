Stuart downtown dock closes Sunday for $3 million renovation to expand to 44 slips

Stuart's downtown public dock will close July 6 for a $3 million renovation projected to be complete by next Fourth of July. The project will nearly double the dock's capacity, expanding from 18 to 44 slips for both motorized and non-motorized vessels. The renovation will include a 7,500 square foot fixed dock, a 1,400 square foot floating dock and a smaller floating platform. Funding comes from a state grant and transportation impact fees. Stuart Mayor Sean Reed said the upgrades will prevent storm damage during hurricanes and major storms.

More than 1,400 boaters use the dock annually. During the closure, Tiki Taxi & Cruises will be able to continue operations. Austin Kuyrkendall, director of operations for Tiki Taxi & Cruises, said the city is allowing the company to build a gate at the non-floating section of the dock for passenger boarding during construction. Kuyrkendall said the dock is central to Stuart's identity and economy. Boat captain Parker Fisher said the dock has needed work over the years and he is glad the city is investing money into it.
