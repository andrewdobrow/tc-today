School bus driver arrested in Florida after second student allegation

A Florida school bus driver has been arrested following a second allegation from a student. Law enforcement took action after receiving multiple reports concerning the driver's conduct with students.

The arrest represents the second complaint against the driver, prompting authorities to intervene. School transportation officials are working with law enforcement as the investigation continues.

The case highlights ongoing concerns about student safety during school transportation. Parents and school officials are reviewing protocols to ensure children remain protected while riding school buses.

School district administrators are expected to address transportation safety measures in response to the allegations. The investigation remains active as authorities work to determine the full scope of the incidents involving students.
