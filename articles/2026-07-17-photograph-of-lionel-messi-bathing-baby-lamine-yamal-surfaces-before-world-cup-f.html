Lionel Messi photographed bathing Lamine Yamal as baby nearly two decades before World Cup final matchup

Almost two decades ago, photographer Joan Monfort took photos of a teenage Lionel Messi bathing a cute baby boy in a plastic bathtub as part of a charity calendar produced by local newspaper Sport and UNICEF. That infant was Lamine Yamal. Now those images of the longhaired Messi, his hands covered in soap suds, have become the most talked about in the runup to Sunday's World Cup final, when Messi's Argentina will play Yamal's Spain. Monfort, who works as a freelance photojournalist for the Associated Press from his home in Barcelona, said he has never been a believer or thought that anything was destined to occur, but he is beginning to have his doubts. Luck dictated that Yamal's mother, who appears in the calendar photo, won a raffle of families in the city of Mataró, near Barcelona, who wanted to participate in the 2007 photo shoot. A tearful Messi left Barcelona in 2021 when the club was in financial trouble. Yamal erupted at the club two years later. The 19-year-old Yamal will face a Messi who is 20 years his senior.

Monfort had no recollection of the photos until Yamal's father posted one on social media during the 2024 European Championship, when a teenage Yamal was enjoying his international breakout and led Spain to the title. Monfort, 58, said interest in his photos has skyrocketed and that this has exploded all over the world. He is considering traveling to see the final in New Jersey. Monfort said his heart is split and he does not know if he wants Messi or Yamal to win, as he has an everlasting love for the best player of all time but Yamal has broken the mold and represents a new, diverse Spain thanks to his parents from Morocco and Equatorial Guinea.
