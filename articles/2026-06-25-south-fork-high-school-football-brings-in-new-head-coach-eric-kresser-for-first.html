South Fork High School football brings in new head coach Eric Kresser for first winning season since 2015

South Fork High School's football program is gearing up for the upcoming fall season with fresh leadership. New head coach Eric Kresser, who previously served as head coach at The Benjamin School, has taken the reins of a Bulldogs program that has struggled to find success in recent years. The team has not recorded a winning season since 2015, making this coaching change a significant moment for the Stuart-based program.

The Bulldogs are currently in the middle of their summer preparation, engaging in conditioning work and participating in 7 on 7 tournaments. These summer activities are designed to build team cohesion and prepare players for the challenges ahead. Kresser has emphasized the importance of investment in meetings and fundamental football knowledge during this critical development period. "I'm really excited about what I'm seeing," Kresser told WPTV's John Barron, expressing his optimism about the team's progress and work ethic during the offseason.

Senior Jaahad Henry, a key player for the Bulldogs, is among those energized by the direction the program is heading. Henry highlighted the quality of both players and coaches on the roster, expressing confidence that the combination will lead to success this season. The senior expressed his excitement about playing football and moving beyond just surviving summer conditioning. Henry's positive outlook reflects the broader sentiment on the team that this coaching change represents a genuine opportunity for improvement.

Kresser's vision for turning the program around centers on commitment, preparation, and building a winning culture. He has stated that "where there's a will, there's a way," suggesting that the determination and effort he's seeing from his players will be the foundation for success. With the season set to begin in August, the coaching staff will have their first opportunity to demonstrate whether these summer improvements translate into wins on the field. For a program searching for its first winning season in nearly a decade, the stakes are high and expectations are rising as the fall approaches.
