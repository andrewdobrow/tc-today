Delray Beach golf training center Tee Box opening July 17 after $90,000 contractor setback

Tom Molen, owner of Tee Box, a virtual golf training and performance center near Delray Beach along Jog Road south of Linton Boulevard, faced a three-month delay after problems with his licensed contractor. The work ultimately had to be redone, costing Molen approximately $90,000. This estimate does not include revenue lost during the delays or expenses for bringing in another contractor to complete the repairs. "It was double pay on a lot of things from electricians to HVAC to drywall," Molen said.

David Allan, founder of Code Pro of the Palm Beaches, said contractor-related issues are impacting a significant number of people across Palm Beach County, with an estimated 25% to 35% of homeowners and property owners facing major issues with unvetted contractors. Allan recommends using Palm Beach County's online portal to verify whether contractors have proper licenses and insurance before work begins. The most common contractor complaints in Palm Beach County involve roofing, windows, and air conditioning work. Despite the setbacks, Molen said he is moving forward with the grand opening for Tee Box set for July 17.
