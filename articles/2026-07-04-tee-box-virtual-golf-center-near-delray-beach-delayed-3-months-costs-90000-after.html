Delray Beach golf training center delayed 3 months and costs owner $90,000 after contractor issues

Tom Molen, owner of Tee Box, a new virtual golf training and performance center preparing to open near Delray Beach along Jog Road south of Linton Boulevard, said the project was delayed by three months after problems with the contractor hired to prepare the space. Instead of opening on schedule in April, Molen said the project was constantly pushed off by the contractor. Even though the contractor was licensed, the work ultimately had to be redone, creating a costly domino effect. Molen estimates the issues cost him about $90,000, not including revenue lost during the delays or the added expense of bringing in another contractor to fix the problems. "It was double pay on a lot of things from electricians to HVAC to drywall," Molen said.

Molen worked with Code Pro of the Palm Beaches, which is warning property and business owners to thoroughly vet contractors before hiring them. Code Pro founder David Allan estimates 25% to 35% of homeowners and property owners face major issues with unvetted contractors. He recommends using Palm Beach County's online portal to verify whether contractors have the proper licenses and insurance before any work begins. According to Allan, the most common contractor complaints in Palm Beach County involve roofing, windows and air conditioning work. Despite the setbacks, Tee Box is preparing for its grand opening set for July 17.
