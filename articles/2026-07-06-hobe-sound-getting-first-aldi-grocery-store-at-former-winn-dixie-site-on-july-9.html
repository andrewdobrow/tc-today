Aldi to open first Hobe Sound grocery store July 9 at former Winn-Dixie location

Hobe Sound is getting its first Aldi store when the grocery chain opens July 9 at 8867 SE Bridge Road. The new store occupies the space where Winn-Dixie previously operated.

Aldi has converted multiple Winn-Dixie stores across South Florida over the past few years. The Hobe Sound location marks the latest expansion for the discount grocery chain in the region.

A grand opening and ribbon-cutting ceremony will take place July 9 at 8:30 a.m. The first 200 customers will receive a gift bag with Aldi products and a gift card.

The Hobe Sound Aldi will be open daily from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. The store brings a new grocery option to the community at the SE Bridge Road location.
