Port St. Lucie 9-year-old launches junior chess club at community library

A 9-year-old Port St. Lucie boy has started a junior chess club to introduce his community to the game he loves. Orion Jean Baptiste began playing chess at age 5 with his family members and now wants to share that passion with other young people in the area. "I just wanted kids to learn about chess," Baptiste said. He believes chess deserves more respect in a world focused on technology. "Everybody's more focused on like tech stuff," Baptiste said. "I'm hoping that more people have respect for board games."

Diana Cowan Kempton, the club's program director, supports Baptiste's vision for what chess can accomplish. She believes the game will help build the next generation of thoughtful leaders. "It will help to build leaders or members that are more focused, more patient, more strategic and thoughtful in their decision making," Kempton said. The Atlantis Chess Club meets every Friday at the Port St. Lucie library and welcomes players at all skill levels, from beginners to experienced competitors.

The club has already grown to more than 36 junior members since its launch. Families like the Furlongs have been drawn to the program as a structured summer activity where children can learn together. Thomas Furlong brought his children to participate, seeing it as an opportunity for both his daughter to learn chess and his son to improve his skills. "This is like the perfect chance for her to learn chess, my son to get better at chess and then them to have a little sibling rivalry competition," Furlong said. For Francesca Furlong, one of the newest members, the appeal is straightforward. "I like playing with other kids," she said.

The club's success at the Port St. Lucie library has led to expansion plans. The Atlantis Chess Club will soon launch two additional programs at the Fenn Center in Fort Pierce, bringing chess opportunities to even more young people in the area. Through these efforts, Baptiste is achieving his goal of helping children appreciate board games while developing important skills like focus, patience and strategic thinking.
