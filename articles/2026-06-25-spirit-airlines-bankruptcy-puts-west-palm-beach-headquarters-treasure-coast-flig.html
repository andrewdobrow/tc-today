Spirit Airlines bankruptcy puts Treasure Coast entrepreneur's $250 million bid in spotlight

LeRoy Gillead II, a Treasure Coast entrepreneur, has submitted a proposal to purchase Spirit Airlines in its entirety, rehire its workforce, and rebrand the carrier as Florida Air Express, a low-cost airline based in West Palm Beach. According to court documents, Gillead is attempting to secure up to $250 million from private investors to fund the acquisition. The deadline for all bids is scheduled for July.

Spirit Airlines is currently in bankruptcy court liquidating its assets, which include aircraft, airport gates, and potentially its headquarters. These physical assets represent significant value in the aviation industry. The airline's closure removes another budget-friendly option for cost-conscious travelers at a time when airfare prices remain high. Many passengers relied on Spirit's low fares to make air travel affordable.

Clint Henderson, managing editor of news at The Points Guy travel website, expressed skepticism about Gillead's proposal becoming reality. "This seems like a pie in the sky thing," Henderson said. While acknowledging that money could potentially be raised to establish a new low-cost carrier, he noted that such an operation currently has no routes or operational infrastructure. Henderson believes it is far more probable that Spirit's assets will be distributed among multiple bidders, likely major airlines. Those major carriers would acquire the valuable planes and gates rather than seeing them consolidated under a single new operator.

The disappearance of Spirit Airlines reflects a larger trend affecting the budget airline sector. Henderson noted that low-cost carriers are struggling nationwide, with the business model facing significant challenges. Only Frontier and Allegiant remain operating in the true low-cost category. For travelers seeking affordable fares, Henderson recommends a flexible approach. "Be airline agnostic," he advised consumers. "Go with the cheapest airline you can find. Use Google Flights and don't try to be loyal to one airline." This strategy requires more effort to find bargains but offers the best chance of securing discounted tickets in the current market. The iconic yellow aircraft Spirit was known for will likely be repainted and operated under different airline brands if purchased by larger carriers.
