Florida 2026-06-06

Former Lake County Property Appraiser Carey Baker enters race for CD 11

Carey Baker, 63, a lifelong Eustis resident and former Lake County Property Appraiser, is running for Congress in Florida's 11th Congressional District. He will face Lake County Commissioner Anthony Sabatini in the Republican primary. Sabatini, once considered Baker's protégé, has already run for the seat previously.

Baker represented much of Central Florida in the state legislature, serving in both the House and Senate before winning election as a constitutional officer. He was first elected to the Florida House in 2000 and later advanced to the Senate. His father also served the area in the Florida House. Most recently, Baker spent multiple terms as Lake County Property Appraiser before losing his re-election bid in 2024. He had filed as a write-in candidate but was defeated by Republican David Jordan by a three-to-one margin in the general election. Despite the loss, Baker's campaign raised $233,000, demonstrating the fundraising ability that will be critical in the primary race. After his defeat, Baker worked unsuccessfully with Sabatini in an effort to become Lake County Republican Party Chair.

The 11th Congressional District encompasses all of Sumter County, portions of western Orange County, and most of Lake County following the latest congressional redistricting. The district leans heavily Republican, with more than 57 percent of voters supporting President Donald Trump in the 2024 presidential election, making it likely to remain in Republican hands in the general election.

On the Democratic side, repeat candidate Barbie Harden Hall announced on Saturday that she was suspending her campaign due to health issues. Hall had been the leading fundraiser among Democrats and appeared to be the frontrunner for the party's nomination for the second consecutive election cycle. Three other Democratic candidates remain in the race: Royal Webster, Shawn Bettis, and Dan Williams.
