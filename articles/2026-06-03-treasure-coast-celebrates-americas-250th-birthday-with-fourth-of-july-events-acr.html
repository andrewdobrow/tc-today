North Palm Beach celebrates Fourth of July with fireworks and America 250 events at country club

North Palm Beach will host a full day of Independence Day celebrations on July 4 at the North Palm Beach Country Club and The Clubhouse. The free community event combines family-friendly activities, live entertainment and fireworks to mark both the nation's birthday and America's 250th anniversary. According to event organizers, recognizing the milestone adds special significance to this year's annual holiday tradition in Palm Beach County.

The day begins at 11 a.m. with a community pool party at the country club pool area. Attendees can enjoy a water slide, DJ entertainment, games, activities, food and beverages, and a poolside satellite bar. Event organizers note that the pool deck and lawn areas will create a festive backdrop throughout the day. As the afternoon progresses, families can participate in lawn games and activities starting at 4:30 p.m., including cornhole, Connect 4, giant Jenga and face painting.

The Clubhouse will host a Fourth of July barbecue buffet beginning at 4:30 p.m., providing dinner options for guests. Live music from the band Solstice Reign is scheduled to begin around 6 p.m., adding entertainment to the evening festivities. Ashley Shipman, director of parks and recreation, highlighted that the golf course offers one of the best vantage points for viewing the fireworks display.

The celebration concludes with a fireworks finale at 9 p.m. The combination of activities, dining and entertainment makes the event accessible to visitors of all ages. With no admission fee and multiple entertainment options spread throughout the day, North Palm Beach's Fourth of July celebration provides a traditional way for residents and visitors to mark the holiday while also commemorating the country's 250-year milestone.
