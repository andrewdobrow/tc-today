North Palm Beach celebrates Fourth of July with fireworks, pool party at North Palm Beach Country Club

North Palm Beach will host a full day of Independence Day festivities on July 4 at North Palm Beach Country Club and The Clubhouse. The free community event recognizes America's 250th anniversary while offering residents and visitors family-friendly entertainment, live music, games and a fireworks display at 9 p.m. The celebration combines a pool party, lawn activities and a barbecue buffet into one comprehensive holiday gathering for Palm Beach County.

The day begins at 11 a.m. with a community pool party at the country club pool area. Attendees can enjoy a water slide, DJ entertainment, various games and activities, food and beverages, and a poolside satellite bar. The pool deck and lawn areas will provide a festive backdrop as guests spend time in the water and around the grounds throughout the afternoon and evening hours.

Starting at 4:30 p.m., families can participate in lawn games and activities including cornhole, Connect 4, giant Jenga, face painting and other yard games. The Clubhouse will begin serving a Fourth of July barbecue buffet at the same time, allowing families to gather for dinner. Live music from the band Solstice Reign is scheduled to start around 6 p.m., providing entertainment as the sun sets and the evening progresses toward the fireworks finale.

According to Ashley Shipman, director of parks and recreation, the golf course will offer one of the best vantage points for viewing the fireworks display. Whether visitors choose to watch from the golf course, the pool area or the lawn, the venue provides multiple locations to enjoy the evening's entertainment. The event combines dining, recreation and entertainment into a comprehensive celebration designed for all ages and interests.
