Dave & Buster's employee stops suspected impaired driver in Port St. Lucie

A Dave & Buster's employee in Port St. Lucie intervened to stop a suspected impaired driver. The employee's actions are being praised by local authorities.

The incident occurred at the Dave & Buster's location in Port St. Lucie. The employee recognized signs of impairment and took action to prevent the suspected impaired driver from leaving the premises.

Local law enforcement responded to the scene after the employee's intervention. The employee is being hailed for the quick thinking that potentially prevented a dangerous situation on local roads.
