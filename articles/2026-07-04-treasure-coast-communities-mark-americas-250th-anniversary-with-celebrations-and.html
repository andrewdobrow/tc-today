Southwest Florida communities celebrate America's 250th with fireworks and parades

Southwest Florida communities are celebrating America's 250th birthday with a full slate of Independence Day events. Bradenton and Palmetto are marking the semiquincentennial together with festivities on both sides of the Manatee River, including activities along the Bradenton Riverwalk, Rossi Park, Old Main Street, and Palmetto's Riverside Park. Palmetto's 15th annual Fourth Fest at Riverside Park will feature 14 food trucks, 20 artisan and community vendors, a "Lil Firecrackers" kids zone, and live music, with fireworks visible from both sides of the river. Sarasota's bayfront will host its America250 fireworks display beginning at 9 p.m.

Additional celebrations span the region from Sarasota to Fort Myers Beach. Venice will hold a patriotic parade through Historic Downtown Venice starting at 9:30 a.m., followed by a fireworks show at the South Jetty scheduled between 9 and 9:15 p.m., with July 5 as a rain date. Siesta Beach will host its 35th annual community fireworks show from 8 to 9:30 p.m., funded entirely by local residents, businesses, and community supporters. Downtown Fort Myers will present a free concert by Deb The Dynamics and a combined fireworks and drone show at the Caloosa Sound Amphitheater from 7 to 9:30 p.m. Fort Myers Beach will hold a parade at 10 a.m. starting at Bay Oaks Recreation Center and continuing down Estero Boulevard to Times Square, followed by fireworks launched from a barge near Times Square around 9 p.m.
