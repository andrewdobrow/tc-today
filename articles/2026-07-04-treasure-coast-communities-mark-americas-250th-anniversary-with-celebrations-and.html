Southwest Florida communities celebrate America's 250th with fireworks and parades

Fourth of July weekend is bringing fireworks, parades, drone shows and waterfront celebrations across Southwest Florida as communities mark America's 250th birthday. The semiquincentennial gives this year's Independence Day events a special anniversary hook, with festivities planned from Sarasota's bayfront and Nathan Benderson Park to Manatee County, Venice, North Port and Fort Myers Beach.

Sarasota's bayfront will host a city co-sponsored America250 fireworks display beginning at 9 p.m. Waterside Place in Lakewood Ranch will celebrate with circus acts, carnival games, a kids zone, food and a drone show over the water at 9 p.m. Siesta Beach will host its 35th annual community fireworks show from 8 to 9:30 p.m., with fireworks launching at dusk. The display is funded entirely by local residents, businesses and community supporters. Fort Myers will celebrate with a free concert by Deb The Dynamics and a fireworks and drone show at the Caloosa Sound Amphitheater. Fort Myers Beach will kick off celebrations with a parade at 10 a.m. starting at Bay Oaks Recreation Center and heading down Estero Boulevard to Times Square, followed by fireworks launched from a barge near Times Square around 9 p.m. Matanzas Pass Bridge will close from around 8:30 to 11:30 p.m. for the fireworks display.
