Southwest Florida communities celebrate America's 250th with fireworks, drone shows and parades

Fourth of July weekend brought fireworks, parades, drone shows and waterfront celebrations across Southwest Florida as communities marked America's 250th birthday. Festivities were planned from Sarasota's bayfront and Nathan Benderson Park to Manatee County, Venice, North Port and Fort Myers Beach. The semiquincentennial gave this year's Independence Day events a special anniversary focus.

Bradenton and Palmetto celebrated together with activities along the Bradenton Riverwalk, Rossi Park, Old Main Street and Palmetto's Riverside Park. Palmetto's 15th annual Fourth Fest at Riverside Park featured 14 food trucks, 20 artisan and community vendors, a kids zone and live music, with fireworks visible from both sides of the Manatee River. Sarasota's bayfront hosted its America250 fireworks display beginning at 9 p.m., while Siesta Beach held its 35th annual community fireworks show, funded entirely by local residents, businesses and community supporters. Venice celebrated with a patriotic parade through Historic Downtown Venice at 9:30 a.m., starting at West Venice Avenue and Avenue des Parques and ending near West Blalock Park. The city's America250 fireworks show was scheduled for 9 to 9:15 p.m. at the South Jetty, with contingency plans to reschedule to July 5 in case of inclement weather. North Port hosted its Freedom Festival: Cheers to 250 Years at CoolToday Park from 6 to 9:30 p.m., featuring patriotic entertainment, family activities, food vendors, live music and fireworks. Downtown Fort Myers offered a free concert by Deb The Dynamics and a combined fireworks and drone show at the Caloosa Sound Amphitheater.
