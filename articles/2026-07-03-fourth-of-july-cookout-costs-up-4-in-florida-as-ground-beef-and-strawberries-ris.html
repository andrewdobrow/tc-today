Fourth of July cookout costs up 4% this year, survey finds

This year's Fourth of July celebrations will cost families more than they spent last year. The American Farm Bureau Federation's Summer Cookout Cost Survey shows that preparing a holiday meal for 10 people now costs $73.82, representing a 4% increase from the previous year. The price hike reflects broader inflationary pressures affecting groceries across multiple categories, with some items experiencing more significant jumps than others.

Inflation is the primary driver behind rising cookout expenses. Ground beef prices have climbed 5.5%, while hamburger buns have increased 7.7%. Strawberries faced particularly steep price increases, jumping 12.4% due to damage from a Florida frost. Ice cream prices are also up, rising 5.3% compared to the previous year. These increases across key Fourth of July staples mean families planning traditional cookouts should expect to pay noticeably more at checkout.

However, not all grocery prices have risen this year. Potato prices have declined, offering savings for those planning to serve potato chips or potato salad at their gatherings. Corn prices have shown more volatility throughout the season, though shoppers can find better deals on locally grown corn from the Glades region. These modest price decreases in some categories provide limited relief from the overall upward trend in cookout costs.

Budgeting expert Andrea Woroch recommends several strategies to manage Fourth of July expenses without sacrificing the holiday meal. She emphasizes the importance of shopping strategically by comparing prices across different stores to identify the best deals for items on your grocery list. Woroch also highlights that families can significantly reduce costs by minimizing food waste, which begins with careful meal planning before heading to the store. By taking time to plan meals in advance and shopping with intention, consumers can better control their cookout budgets despite the inflationary environment affecting grocery prices this season.
