Fourth of July cookout costs rise 4% this year

The Fourth of July cookout tradition is getting pricier this year. According to the American Farm Bureau Federation's Summer Cookout Cost Survey, a holiday meal for 10 people now costs $73.82, up 4% from a year ago. The survey points squarely at inflation, with ground beef up 5.5% and buns up 7.7%. Strawberries, hit hard by a Florida frost, are up 12.4%, while ice cream prices have risen 5.3%.

Not everything is more expensive, though. Potato prices are down this year, which is good news for potato chip and salad lovers. Corn prices have fluctuated, but locally grown corn from the Glades can offer the best deal. Budgeting expert Andrea Woroch said families can keep costs in check by shopping savvier than ever and comparing stores to see who has the best deals for groceries on their list. She emphasized that the biggest thing for families is cutting down food waste, which begins with a meal plan.
