Fourth of July cookout costs up 4 percent this year

A Fourth of July cookout for 10 people now costs $73.82, up 4 percent from a year ago, according to the American Farm Bureau Federation's Summer Cookout Cost Survey. The price increases are driven by inflation across multiple traditional holiday foods. Ground beef prices rose 5.5 percent and buns increased 7.7 percent. Strawberries saw the largest price jump at 12.4 percent following a Florida frost that damaged crops. Ice cream prices also climbed 5.3 percent.

Not all items have increased in cost. Potato prices are down this year, offering savings for those planning potato dishes. Corn prices have fluctuated, though locally grown corn from the Glades can provide the best deals. Budgeting expert Andrea Woroch recommends shoppers compare stores to find the best deals for groceries on their lists. She emphasized that cutting down food waste through meal planning is the biggest thing families can do to control costs during the holiday season.
