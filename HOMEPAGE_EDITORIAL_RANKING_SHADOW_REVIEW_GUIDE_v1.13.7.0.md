# Homepage Editorial Ranking Shadow Review Guide — v1.13.7.0

After each production Generate News run, inspect:

1. `data/homepage-ranking-review.md`
2. `data/homepage-ranking-recommendations.json`
3. `data/top-stories-ranking-report.json`
4. The live homepage for comparison

## Review questions

For each proposed hero/deck move, judge whether the recommendation improves:

- local consequence and public importance,
- freshness and genuine new development,
- story mix without artificial county quotas,
- avoidance of routine crime/event saturation,
- promotion of validated material updates,
- treatment of TCT/custom reporting on equal editorial footing,
- overall front-page news judgment.

## Automatic red flags

Do not advance toward enforcement if any run shows:

- a story older than the freshness contract promoted into the recommended deck,
- an unresolved identity conflict influencing a move,
- a duplicate/canonical-equivalent story in the recommended deck,
- a manual pin moved,
- weak county content promoted only to satisfy representation,
- a routine story displacing a materially more consequential current story,
- the ranker changing the rendered homepage itself.

## Promotion threshold

Keep v1.13.7.0 shadow-only for at least 3–5 representative production runs. A later increment may grant narrowly scoped card-order authority only after the recommendations are consistently editorially superior and the reports show clean identity/freshness behavior. Hero authority should remain a separate, later decision.
